# Reasoning for Mobile User Experience with Multimodal LLMs: Task, Benchmark, and Approach

**Arxiv:** 2606.13192 | **Date:** 2026-06-17 | **Area:** multi-modal

## What it does
The paper introduces UXBench, a multimodal benchmark of 2,000 visual-question-answering samples built on real-world user-interface screenshots. Eight tasks require fine-grained diagnosis of user-experience problems across layout relationships, visual hierarchy, and content consistency. The work pairs the benchmark with a reasoning approach tuned for screen understanding.

## Delta from parents
Where DeepEyesV2 and PyVision give a model tools to reason over general images, this paper fixes the image type to user interfaces and demands a fault diagnosis with a ground-truth answer, because a constrained domain makes the reasoning measurable rather than open-ended. The contribution is the task formulation and labeled diagnostic data more than a single headline accuracy. Both parents sit in graphify community 0 (LLM Agents and Reasoning).

## Why Leo should care
The idea of a fine-grained, checkable diagnostic benchmark transfers cleanly to wearable signals: ask a model which channel and which time segment drives a label. No imported method here closes off biosensing fruit; instead the benchmark design is the portable piece. See Gap Watch.
