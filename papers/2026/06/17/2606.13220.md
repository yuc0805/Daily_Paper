# LLM-as-an-Investigator: Evidence-First Reasoning for Robust Interactive Problem Diagnosis

**Arxiv:** 2606.13220 | **Date:** 2026-06-17 | **Area:** agent

## What it does
The paper frames interactive problem diagnosis as evidence-gathering before conclusion. Rather than committing to a hypothesis and then justifying it, the agent is structured to collect evidence first and let the diagnosis follow from what it found. The setting is multi-turn interaction where the agent asks, observes, and revises.

## Direct parents (in Leo's library)
- [J8DYBKW2] ADaPT (Prasad 2023) — as-needed decomposition and planning of a task into sub-plans.
- [77ERE7HA] LATS (Zhou 2024) — language agent tree search over action sequences.

## Delta from parents
Where ADaPT decomposes a task into sub-plans on demand and LATS searches over action trees, this paper reorders the loop so evidence collection precedes commitment, because premature hypotheses are the failure mode both planning and search inherit. The claim is robustness when the first guess is wrong. Both parents are in graphify community 0.

## Why Leo should care
Evidence-first control is relevant to any diagnostic agent over noisy signals, including health monitoring where a wrong early hypothesis is costly. Imported method: decomposition-and-search agents, here reordered to gather evidence before concluding.
