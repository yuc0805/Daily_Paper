# PauseRec: Implicit Reasoning for LLM-based Generative Recommendation

**Arxiv:** 2606.14142 | **Date:** 2026-06-17 | **Area:** reasoning

## What it does
PauseRec adds a lightweight implicit-reasoning step to LLM-based generative recommendation. Instead of writing out an explicit chain of thought before predicting the next item, the model reasons internally through inserted "pause" steps. The authors report up to 6.22% higher accuracy than explicit chain-of-thought prompting and up to 65% fewer GPU hours in training.

## Direct parents (in Leo's library)
- [HBLPTRMY] Chain-of-Thought Prompting (Wei 2023) — explicit step-by-step reasoning written as text.
- [Z5IWHZAE] DeepSeek-R1 (2025) — long explicit reasoning traces trained with reinforcement learning.

## Delta from parents
Where Chain-of-Thought spends tokens writing the reasoning out and DeepSeek-R1 trains long explicit traces, PauseRec keeps the extra computation but drops the text, because for ranking a final item the written rationale is overhead rather than signal. The result is most of the reasoning benefit at lower training cost. Both parents are in graphify community 0.

## Why Leo should care
Implicit reasoning budgets (compute without text) are a general efficiency lever. For time-series forecasting with LLMs, where TS-Agent and ChatTS already spend tokens on explicit reasoning, an implicit-pause variant is a plausible next step. Imported method: chain-of-thought reasoning, here made implicit.
