# ARM: An AutoRegressive Large Multimodal Model with Unified Discrete Representations

**Arxiv:** 2606.11188 | **Date:** 2026-06-17 | **Area:** multi-modal

## What it does
ARM is a 7B autoregressive model that handles image understanding, text-to-image generation, and instruction-guided editing in one next-token framework. A discrete semantic visual tokenizer maps images into short token sequences, trained jointly for semantic discriminability, language alignment, and faithful reconstruction. Reinforcement learning after pretraining optimizes visual quality, instruction adherence, and edit consistency.

## Direct parents (in Leo's library)
- [DUERBZGM] DALL-E 2 (Ramesh 2022) — text-to-image generation through a diffusion path, separate from understanding.
- [YJ9TK993] DiT (Peebles 2023) — transformer-backbone diffusion for scalable image generation.
- [PHB9VRVM] Attention Is All You Need (Vaswani 2017) — the next-token transformer backbone ARM reuses.
- [Z5IWHZAE] DeepSeek-R1 (2025) — reinforcement-learning post-training that ARM adapts from text reasoning to image tasks.

## Delta from parents
Where DALL-E 2 and DiT generate pixels through iterative denoising and keep understanding in a separate model, ARM does understanding and generation as a single next-token stream over discrete tokens, because a shared discrete latent lets both tasks reuse one set of parameters. Where DeepSeek-R1 used reward optimization to sharpen text reasoning, ARM shows the same post-training step transfers to image generation and editing, and reports cross-task synergy (gains on generation also help editing). SigLIP, the natural vision-language alignment parent, is not in the library yet and is the closest missing neighbor in graphify community 3.

## Why Leo should care
The transferable pattern is a discrete tokenizer plus one autoregressive backbone plus reinforcement-learning post-training across understanding, generation, and editing. This has not been applied to biosignals. Imported method: autoregressive next-token modeling and RL preference optimization from the LLM and generative-vision lines; it is now demonstrated as a unified multimodal recipe but remains unported to time-series and bio-sensing.
