# CogOmniControl: Reasoning-Driven Controllable Video Generation via Creative Intent Cognition

**Arxiv:** 2605.19995 | **Date:** 2026-06-01 | **Area:** generative-cv

## What it does
CogOmniControl factorizes controllable video generation into two stages: first infer the user's creative intent (a reasoning step), then generate the video conditioned on that inferred intent. The split is meant to give more faithful control when the prompt is abstract or underspecified, rather than mapping a control signal straight to pixels.

## Delta from parents
Where DiT and DDPM generate directly from a conditioning signal through the denoising process, CogOmniControl inserts a reasoning stage ahead of generation, because a diffusion backbone follows the condition it is given but does not decide what an underspecified prompt should mean. The added cognition stage borrows the same idea as Chain-of-Thought, applied to generation control rather than to text answers.

## Why Leo should care
Secondary-area watch item. The reasoning-before-generation pattern is the generative analogue of MemReread's reasoning-before-answering, and is worth noting as a recurring design move: insert an explicit reasoning stage in front of a base model rather than retraining the base model.
