# Bio-Inspired Self-Supervised Learning for Wrist-worn IMU Signals (Bio-PM)

**Arxiv:** 2603.10961 | **Date:** 2026-06-22 | **Area:** self-supervised

## What it does
Bio-PM is a self-supervised encoder for wrist accelerometer data. Instead of cutting the signal into fixed-length windows, it tokenizes the stream into "movement segments" defined by acceleration zero-crossings, a unit motivated by the submovement theory of motor control. A 5-layer Transformer with time-aware positional encodings is pretrained on these segments by masked reconstruction over the NHANES corpus (about 28,000 hours, 11,000 participants). On six subject-disjoint human activity recognition benchmarks it improves macro-F1 by an average of 6 percent over self-supervised baselines trained on the same corpus.

## Delta from parents
Where Yuan 2024 kept the input as fixed-length windows and varied the objective (augmentation prediction), Bio-PM keeps the large-scale pretraining idea but changes the input unit, because the authors argue fixed windows hide the compositional structure of movement and the tokenizer, not the objective, is the bottleneck. Where Zhang 2023 reconstructed masked raw patches, Bio-PM reconstructs masked motion-aligned segments, so the prediction target carries an event boundary rather than an arbitrary sample grid. Relative to the Gu 2025 survey, which maps the general direction, this paper supplies a concrete tokenization-and-pretraining recipe for the accelerometer case. The controlled setup (same corpus, same protocol across baselines) isolates the tokenization effect.

## Why Leo should care
This is in Leo's primary area (wearable sensing, self-supervised learning). It imports two outside ideas into bio-sensing: the masked-autoencoder objective from vision and the "meaningful token" lesson from language modeling. Both moves are now demonstrated for wrist IMU, so the plain version of "apply masked reconstruction to accelerometer windows" is closed as low-hanging fruit; the open question it raises is whether motion-aligned tokenization transfers to other sensor placements and to full fine-tuning.
