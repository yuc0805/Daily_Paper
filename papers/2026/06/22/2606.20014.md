# Hierarchical Control in Multi-Agent Games: LLM-based Planning and RL Execution

**Arxiv:** 2606.20014 | **Date:** 2026-06-22 | **Area:** agent

## What it does
The paper proposes a two-level architecture for multi-agent control. A pretrained large language model acts as a central strategic controller that selects among pretrained reinforcement learning skill policies, while the RL policies handle low-level reactive execution. In a competitive 2v2 King of the Hill game, the LLM-plus-RL system reaches a win rate statistically equal to a hand-built behavior tree (46.4 percent versus 51.5 percent, p = 0.103), and both clearly beat flat RL trained without skill decomposition. A user study (n = 15) found 60 percent of participants rated the LLM-plus-RL agents as the most human-like.
