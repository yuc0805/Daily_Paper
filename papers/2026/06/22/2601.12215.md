# Wavelet-Driven Masked Multiscale Reconstruction for PPG Foundation Models (MMR)

**Arxiv:** 2601.12215 | **Date:** 2026-06-22 | **Area:** llm-health

## What it does
MMR is a self-supervised pretraining framework for photoplethysmography (PPG) foundation models. It decomposes each PPG segment with a discrete wavelet transform into multiple time-frequency bands and trains a Transformer encoder to reconstruct randomly masked wavelet coefficients across scales. Pretrained on about 17 million 10-second segments from roughly 32,000 smartwatch users, it matches or beats open PPG and time-series foundation models on 17 of 19 downstream health tasks.

## Delta from parents
Where the Gu 2025 survey maps the general goal, MMR supplies a concrete objective for PPG, because it argues the missing piece is explicit multi-scale time-frequency structure that time-domain or fixed-window Fourier methods do not capture. Where Zhang 2023 masked raw-signal patches, MMR moves the masking target to wavelet coefficients across decomposition levels, so the encoder is forced to combine fine waveform morphology with coarse rhythm. Compared to HeAR, which used spectral representations for acoustic health signals, MMR uses an adaptive wavelet decomposition tuned to the non-stationary nature of PPG.

## Why Leo should care
PPG is in Leo's primary bio-sensing area and is present in nearly every consumer wearable. MMR imports wavelet multi-resolution analysis and cross-scale masked reconstruction into PPG self-supervised learning. With this demonstrated, "add a fixed Fourier branch to a PPG encoder" is closed as easy ground; the wavelet, cross-scale-masking version is the new reference point.
