# QUBRIC: Co-Designing Queries and Rubrics for RL Beyond Verifiable Rewards

**Arxiv:** 2606.03968 | **Date:** 2026-06-02 | **Area:** reasoning

## What it does
QUBRIC addresses a structural bottleneck in rubric-based RL: rubric quality is constrained by query structure. Open-ended queries yield vague rubrics; narrowing them naively introduces fabricated references that cause all responses to fail. QUBRIC rewrites open-ended queries into scenario-based evaluable questions using teacher-derived key points, generates contrastive rubrics from teacher-policy gaps, and filters for learnability before GRPO training. It achieves +5.5 points on ArenaHard and transfers to legal, moral, and narrative reasoning benchmarks (+6.3 average).

## Delta from parents
Where DeepSeek-R1 showed RL works well when rewards are verifiable (math answers, code tests), QUBRIC extends RL training to tasks where correctness cannot be automatically verified. The core insight is that you must co-design the query and the rubric together — fixing one while varying the other produces either vague or impossible evaluation criteria. This is a practical step toward RL-trained reasoning on open-ended scientific tasks.

## Why Leo should care
Most research tasks (literature synthesis, experimental design, clinical interpretation) do not have verifiable answers. QUBRIC shows a path for RL training on such tasks, which could eventually apply to scientific reasoning agents.
