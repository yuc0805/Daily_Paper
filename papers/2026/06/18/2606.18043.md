# Uncertainty Quantification for Flow-Based Vision-Language-Action Models

**Arxiv:** 2606.18043 | **Date:** 2026-06-18 | **Area:** generative-cv

## What it does
This paper studies VLAs whose action head is a flow-matching generator trained on large robot datasets. Such models act well but give no signal about when they are unsure. The paper adds a way to quantify confidence in a generated action and to flag actions that may be unreliable, giving an out-of-distribution and low-confidence detector for flow-based action models.
