# Agentic Reasoning for Large Language Models

**Arxiv:** 2601.12538 | **Date:** 2026-06-30 | **Area:** reasoning

## What it does
This paper frames reasoning as an agentic loop: instead of producing one chain of thought, the model plans, calls tools or sub-routines, reads back results, and revises. It argues that hard problems are solved better by interleaving reasoning with action than by longer single-pass traces. ## Delta from parents
Where Chain-of-Thought and DeepSeek-R1 keep reasoning inside one text trace, this work moves to a loop where the model acts, observes, and reasons again, so a wrong step can be corrected by evidence rather than by more text. Where ToolkenGPT added tool calls, this paper makes the tool call part of a reasoning policy rather than a one-off capability. The change is structural: reasoning becomes a control loop instead of a single output.
