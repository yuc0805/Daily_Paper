# Biosignal Fingerprinting: A Cross-Modal PPG-ECG Foundation Model

**Arxiv:** 2605.09579 | **Date:** 2026-05-14 | **Area:** llm-health, time-series

## What it does
Presents a Multi-modal Masked Autoencoder (M2AE) trained on 3.4 million paired ECG and PPG signal segments. The model combines cross-modal contrastive learning with signal reconstruction to learn shared representations across the two biosignal modalities. It achieves AUROC 0.974 for 5-class cardiovascular disease classification and 0.877 for hypertension detection, and it works with only a single modality at inference time — meaning a PPG-only wearable can benefit from ECG-informed representations.

## Delta from parents
Where zhang2023_mae applied masked autoencoders to single-modality time-series and baur2024_hear focused on ECG-only representations, this paper trains a joint MAE across paired PPG and ECG signals. The cross-modal contrastive objective forces the model to learn modality-invariant features — the representation of a heartbeat pattern should be similar whether observed through electrical (ECG) or optical (PPG) measurement. This is more than simply training two separate MAEs; the pairing pressure creates representations that transfer across sensor types, which is why single-modality inference works so well.
