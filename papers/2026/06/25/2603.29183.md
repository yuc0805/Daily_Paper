# IMPACT: Influence Modeling for Open-Set Time Series Anomaly Detection

**Arxiv:** 2603.29183 | **Date:** 2026-06-25 | **Area:** time-series

## What it does
IMPACT addresses open-set anomaly detection on time series, where a few labeled anomalies seen in training must help catch both seen and unseen anomaly types at test time. It quantifies how much each training sample changes test risk, then uses those influence scores to relabel contaminated training points (flipping labels that raise risk) and to synthesize realistic unseen anomalies (perturbing features in the direction influence says matters). Accepted at ICML 2026.

## Delta from parents
VAN-AD attacks the anomaly score itself, making it a calibrated likelihood from reconstruction error and density. IMPACT works one level earlier, at the training data: it uses influence to decontaminate labels and to build realistic synthetic anomalies, rather than touching the model's scoring head. Where prior open-set methods imported from other domains created trivial or unrealistic anomalies because they ignored sequence structure, IMPACT keeps the synthetic anomalies on the data manifold by steering perturbations with influence.

## Why Leo should care
Open-set detection matters for wearable monitoring, where the anomaly types at deployment are rarely the ones labeled in training. On the porting axis: influence-function data attribution is now applied to time-series anomaly detection, so that specific transfer is taken.
