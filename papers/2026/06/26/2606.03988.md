# Imaginative Perception Tokens Enhance Spatial Reasoning in Multimodal Language Models

**Arxiv:** 2606.03988 | **Date:** 2026-06-26 | **Area:** multi-modal

## What it does
The paper introduces Imaginative Perception Tokens (IPT), intermediate tokens that represent what a vision language model would perceive under a different spatial configuration while staying consistent with the observed input. Built on the unified VLM BAGEL and trained on roughly 20K examples across perspective taking, path tracing, and multiview counting, IPT supervision beats textual chain-of-thought training and raises multiview-counting accuracy by 3.4%, with no image generated at inference.

## Delta from parents
Where chain-of-thought externalizes reasoning as a text trace, IPT externalizes it as a perceptual trace in token form, because the authors find that forcing spatial computation through language can lower accuracy. The delta is the claim that for spatial problems the intermediate representation should be perceptual, not linguistic, and the evidence that perceptual tokens generalize better than the text-trace baseline.

## Why Leo should care
This is a multimodal method, watched for porting. The transferable idea is supervising a model with "imagined" intermediate states rather than text. For bio-signals, the analogue would be tokens representing what a signal would look like under a different physiological state, used to supervise reasoning about unobserved conditions. That porting has not been done.
