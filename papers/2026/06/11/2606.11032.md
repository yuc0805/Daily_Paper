# U-TTT: Towards Generalizable PET Image Denoising via Test-Time Training

**Arxiv:** 2606.11032 | **Date:** 2026-06-09 | **Area:** test-time-training

## What it does
U-TTT is a U-shaped denoising network for PET imaging whose inner blocks are Test-Time Training layers: parameters are updated during inference through a self-supervised loss computed on each test instance, so the model adapts to the specific dose level and scanner that produced the input. A dual-domain design uses a Spatial TTT layer for structural degradation and a Frequency TTT layer that suppresses global noise spectra while restoring high-frequency detail. The paper reports state-of-the-art denoising and improved generalization to unseen dose levels and unseen scanners.

Where TTT Layers framed adaptation as a generic sequence-modeling primitive, this paper specializes it to a concrete clinical robustness problem: fixed-parameter denoisers fail when dose protocol or scanner hardware changes between training and deployment.
