# Predict and Reconstruct: Joint JEPA + MLM for Self-Supervised Language Representations

Combines JEPA-style latent prediction loss with MLM reconstruction on shared encoder. Learnable scalar balances objectives during training. More uniform embeddings (uniformity < -0.16 vs -0.05) with similar downstream accuracy on GLUE. JEPA objective reshapes latent space geometry in ways accuracy metrics miss.

Key delta from SSL for HAR (Yuan 2024): applies hybrid JEPA+masked objective to language; transfer to sensor data is the open opportunity. From MAE Theory (Zhang 2023): extends theory of how masking strategy affects representation quality.
