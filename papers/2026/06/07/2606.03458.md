# KVarN: Variance-Normalized KV-Cache Quantization

Calibration-free KV-cache quantizer: Hadamard rotation + dual-scaling variance normalization. Fixes token-scale outliers that cause error accumulation during autoregressive decoding. 3-5x KV-cache capacity at 2-bit with FP16-level accuracy on MATH500, AIME24, HumanEval. Ships as single vLLM flag.

Key delta from DeepSeek-V3 MLA: quantization-level compression vs architecture-level compression. Complementary approaches.
