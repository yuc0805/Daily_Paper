# SARAF: Stationarity-Aware Retrieval-Augmented Time Series Forecasting

Retrieval-augmented forecasting that addresses non-stationarity. Candidate pool via temporal similarity, diversity-aware selection modulated by dataset-level stationarity, stationarity-aware aggregation. Improves accuracy and robustness on 8 datasets, especially under non-stationary conditions.

Key delta from LLMs for TS Survey (Zhang 2024): adds retrieval augmentation that adapts to regime shifts. From iTransformer and PatchTST: adds external evidence conditioning rather than relying purely on the model's parametric knowledge.
