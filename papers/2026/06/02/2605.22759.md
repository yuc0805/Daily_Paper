# Towards a General Intelligence and Interface for Wearable Health Data

**Arxiv:** 2605.22759 | **Date:** 2026-06-02 | **Area:** llm-health

## What it does
A foundation model for wearable health, pretrained on more than one trillion minutes of unlabeled sensor signal from about five million participants. Scaling model size with data volume gives steady gains across 35 health prediction tasks (cardiovascular, metabolic, sleep, mental health, lifestyle, demographics). The representation supports label-efficient few-shot learning and daily metric generation. A classroom of LLM agents searches the space of downstream predictive heads on the frozen embeddings, and the best heads feed a Personal Health Agent validated with 1,860 clinician ratings.

## Delta from parents
Where Yuan 2024 pretrained on 700K person-days for activity recognition, this paper scales the same self-supervised idea to one trillion minutes and five million people and adds explicit scaling-law evidence, because the open question moved from "does self-supervision help" to "how far does it scale". Where Liu 2023 fed sensor numbers into a prompt, this paper trains predictive heads on a learned representation and lets LLM agents choose the heads. It also extends the Sensor2Text bridge between bio-sensing (Community 4) and llm-health (Community 1): where Sensor2Text hand-built a sensor-to-language path, the new work replaces it with a scaled representation plus agentic head search and a clinician-rated health agent.

## Why Leo should care
This is the center of Leo's primary area: a population-scale self-supervised model for wearable physiological signals with an LLM interface. It imports the masked self-supervised pretraining and scaling-law playbook from NLP and vision and applies it to wearable signals at a scale individual labs cannot reach, which closes that particular transfer as low-hanging fruit. What remains open is per-channel tokenization and continual indexing of long signal histories.
