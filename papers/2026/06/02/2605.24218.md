# QUEST: Training Frontier Deep Research Agents with Fully Synthetic Tasks

**Arxiv:** 2605.24218 | **Date:** 2026-06-02 | **Area:** agent

## What it does
QUEST is an open family of deep research agents (2B to 35B parameters) for long-horizon search: fact seeking, citation grounding, and report synthesis. The recipe combines mid-training, supervised fine-tuning, and reinforcement learning, fed by a data-synthesis pipeline built on unified rubric trees that produce tasks with verifiable rewards and no human annotation. A built-in context-management mechanism supports long-horizon reasoning. Using only 8K synthesized tasks, QUEST approaches or surpasses closed-source agents across eight deep-research benchmarks and is the best open-weight agent reported. Models, data, and scripts are released.

## Delta from parents
Where the surveys map out what agents should do and DeepSeek-V3 showed RL post-training at model scale, QUEST does the narrower thing of training a search agent end to end on synthetic verifiable tasks. The new piece is the rubric-tree data synthesis, which produces checkable rewards across task types without human labels, because the bottleneck for deep research agents is labeled long-horizon trajectories.

## Why Leo should care
Deep research agents are the closest agent class to Leo's own literature workflow. The rubric-tree synthesis recipe is reusable: verifiable synthetic tasks could in principle train an agent over a structured corpus such as a personal paper library, without hand-labeled trajectories.
