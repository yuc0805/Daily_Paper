# Causal Semantic Alignment for LLM-based Time Series Forecasting (CVAformer)

**Arxiv:** 2606.08262 | **Date:** 2026-06-21 | **Area:** time-series

## What it does
The paper introduces CVAformer (Causal Variable-level Alignment Transformer), a method for forecasting with a pretrained language model as the backbone. It separates each input variable into an invariant part (stable semantics) and a dynamic part (short-term fluctuation) before mapping the series onto word embeddings, then applies a causal backdoor intervention so that the dynamic part does not act as a confounder during alignment. The goal is unbiased alignment between temporal patterns and the language model embedding space.

## Direct parents (in Leo's library)
- [MKICLA63] Time-LLM — established the idea of aligning time series patches with frozen language model word embeddings (reprogramming).
- [6QQNUTL7] GPT4TS — showed a frozen pretrained transformer can serve as a general time series backbone across tasks.
- [QBX2TI2X] iTransformer — introduced variable-level (per-channel) tokenization rather than per-time-step tokens.
- [BB78CC56] Causal Inference Intro (Pearl) — provides the backdoor-adjustment machinery used to remove confounding.

## Delta from parents
Where Time-LLM and GPT4TS align raw temporal patterns with language embeddings and treat each series as a single entangled signal, CVAformer first splits each variable into invariant and dynamic components and treats the dynamic component as a confounder, because the dynamic part influences both the invariant embedding and the aligned output and therefore induces spurious correlation. Where iTransformer made the channel the unit of tokenization, CVAformer keeps that variable-level view but adds a causal step on top of it. The new piece is the backdoor intervention from Pearl-style causal inference, which is not present in any of the three time-series parents; it is the bridge that connects Community 4 (Time Series + LLM Integration) to Community 6 (Model Interpretability and Causal Inference) in Leo's library.

## Why Leo should care
This sits directly in Leo's primary area: language-model-based time series forecasting. It imports a method, backdoor adjustment, from causal inference into the LLM-for-time-series alignment step. That import is now partly closed off as low-hanging fruit for the basic case, but the same causal framing has not yet been carried into bio-sensing signals (PPG, ECG, IMU), where invariant subject physiology and dynamic state are also entangled. That gap is a transfer opportunity for Leo's own work.
