# ARM: An AutoRegressive Large Multimodal Model with Unified Discrete Representations

**Arxiv:** 2606.11188 | **Date:** 2026-06-21 | **Area:** multi-modal

## What it does
ARM is a 7B autoregressive model that handles image understanding, generation, and editing in one next-token-prediction framework. Images are mapped to a compact discrete token sequence by a semantic visual tokenizer trained for discriminability, language alignment, and reconstruction at once. Text and image tokens are interleaved and modeled with a single transformer. Reinforcement learning is then applied to optimize task-level rewards (visual quality, instruction adherence, edit consistency), raising WISE overall from 0.50 to 0.56 and GEdit-Bench-EN from 5.75 to 6.68, with cross-task gains between generation and editing.

## Direct parents (in Leo's library)
- [PHB9VRVM] Attention Is All You Need — the transformer backbone and next-token prediction (Community 2).
- [DUERBZGM] DALL-E 2 — text-to-image generation through learned visual representations (Community 3).
- [SC8KWYVK] Flamingo — interleaving image and text tokens in one sequence for a single model.

## Delta from parents
Where DALL-E 2 used a separate diffusion-style decoder over CLIP latents and Flamingo kept vision and language in distinct modules joined by cross-attention, ARM puts both modalities into one discrete vocabulary and one autoregressive transformer, so generation and understanding share the same parameters and objective. Where the original transformer predicted text tokens only, ARM predicts a unified text-plus-image token stream and then adds reinforcement learning on task rewards, which is the step that produces the cross-task synergy. The novelty is the single discrete representation plus reward optimization, not the autoregressive idea itself.

## Why Leo should care
ARM is outside Leo's primary area, but the recipe (one discrete tokenizer shared across modalities, next-token prediction, then reinforcement learning on task rewards) is a candidate to port into multi-signal physiological sensing, where PPG, ECG, IMU, and EEG could share one token vocabulary. That transfer has not been done, so it is an open opportunity rather than closed-off fruit.
