# Process-Verified Reinforcement Learning for Theorem Proving via Lean

**Arxiv:** 2606.20068 | **Date:** 2026-06-23 | **Area:** reasoning

## What it does
This work uses the Lean proof assistant as a process-level reward oracle for reinforcement learning, rather than only as a pass/fail checker at the end. Each proof attempt is parsed into a sequence of tactics; Lean's elaboration marks which steps are locally sound and which is the first failing step, giving a dense, type-checked credit signal. These signals feed a GRPO-style objective with first-error propagation and first-token credit. With STP-Lean and DeepSeek-Prover-V1.5, tactic-level supervision beats outcome-only baselines on MiniF2F and ProofNet.

## Direct parents (in Leo's library)
- [Z5IWHZAE] DeepSeek-R1 (deepseek2025_r1, community 0) — set the template of RLVR with a single binary verifiable reward, which this paper argues is too coarse.
- [HBLPTRMY] Chain-of-Thought Prompting (wei2023_cot, community 0) — origin of explicit intermediate reasoning steps, here made checkable tactic by tactic.

## Delta from parents
DeepSeek-R1 trains on one outcome reward per attempt: a proof is right or wrong. That signal is sound but sparse, so credit for a long correct prefix that ends in one bad step is lost. Where DeepSeek-R1 used a single binary check, this paper turns the verifier itself into a dense process reward by reading Lean's per-tactic elaboration, marking the earliest failing step and propagating credit only up to it. The reward stays sound (it comes from type theory, not a learned critic) but becomes fine-grained, which is the specific gap between structured symbolic feedback and unstructured scalar rewards that the paper targets.

## Why Leo should care
The general idea — replace a single end-of-episode reward with a sound, dense, externally computed process signal — transfers to any domain that has a checkable intermediate structure. For time-series reasoning, statistical operators or consistency checks could play the role Lean plays here, supplying verifier-grounded intermediate rewards instead of a single final-answer check.
