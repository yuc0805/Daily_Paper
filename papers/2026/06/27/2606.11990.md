# Time-Series Foundation Model Embeddings for Remaining Useful Life Estimation

**Arxiv:** 2606.11990 | **Date:** 2026-06-27 | **Area:** time-series

## What it does
The paper freezes a pretrained time-series foundation model (Chronos-2) and trains only a small regression head to predict remaining useful life from multivariate sensor streams. The frozen-backbone embeddings beat recurrent, convolutional, Transformer, and gradient-boosting baselines, and accuracy improves as the input history grows. The message is that a general time-series foundation model carries enough structure that a light head is competitive with task-specific architectures.

## Delta from parents
Where the survey catalogued attachment methods and TS-Agent built an agent on top of a foundation model, this paper takes the plainest version: freeze the backbone, train a one-layer head, measure. The delta is a clean ablation showing the embeddings alone, with no fine-tuning, transfer to a regression target. The method imported into sensing is frozen-foundation-model probing, which is now a closed transfer for this setting.

## Why Leo should care
This is a baseline recipe to copy before building anything heavier for bio-sensing: probe a frozen foundation model with a light head and see how far it goes, then justify any added complexity against it.
