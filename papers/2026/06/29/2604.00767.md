# ActivityNarrated: An Open-Ended Narrative Paradigm for Wearable Human Activity Understanding

**Arxiv:** 2604.00767 | **Date:** 2026-06-29 | **Area:** time-series

## What it does
The paper reframes wearable human activity recognition (HAR) away from a fixed label set and toward open-ended natural-language narratives of behavior. It contributes three parts: a dataset of unscripted multi-position IMU recordings with time-aligned narration, an evaluation framework based on sensor-language retrieval that generalizes closed-set classification, and a model (ActNarrator) that maps variable-duration, partially observed sensor streams to text. ActNarrator reports near doubling of Macro-F1 over closed-set HAR baselines when classification is run as a downstream task.

## Delta from parents
Where Sensor2Text produced fixed natural-language descriptions tied to a closed activity vocabulary and a single sensor position, ActivityNarrated removes the predefined taxonomy and models open-ended narratives over heterogeneous, multi-position, partially observed streams, because real wearable use is long-tailed and personalized rather than confined to a benchmark label set. Where SSL for HAR learned position-specific representations that are then probed by a closed classifier, ActivityNarrated keeps the self-supervised representation idea but supervises it with retrieval against free-text narration, so the evaluation measures semantic alignment rather than top-1 accuracy. The result is that activity understanding is scored by how well motion aligns with language, not by how well it fits a fixed set of classes.
