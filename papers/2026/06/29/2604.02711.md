# Foundation Models Defining A New Era In Sensor-based Human Activity Recognition: A Survey And Outlook

**Arxiv:** 2604.02711 | **Date:** 2026-06-29 | **Area:** time-series

## What it does
This survey reviews 132 papers on foundation models for sensor-based human activity recognition and organizes them with a lifecycle taxonomy across input design, pretraining, adaptation, and utilization, structured along nine technical and operational axes. It names three dominant directions: building HAR-specific foundation models from scratch, adapting general time-series and multimodal foundation models to sensor data, and using large language models for reasoning, annotation, and human-AI interaction. It lists open challenges in data curation, multimodal alignment, on-device personalization, and responsible deployment. It was conditionally accepted to ACM IMWUT.
