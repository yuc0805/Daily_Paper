# SignalMC-MED: A Multimodal Benchmark for Evaluating Biosignal Foundation Models on Single-Lead ECG and PPG

**Arxiv:** 2603.09940 | **Date:** 2026-06-29 | **Area:** time-series

## What it does
SignalMC-MED is a benchmark for evaluating biosignal foundation models on synchronized single-lead ECG and PPG. It contains 22,256 visits with 10-minute overlapping ECG and PPG signals and defines 20 clinically relevant tasks: demographics, emergency-department disposition, laboratory-value regression, and detection of prior ICD-10 diagnoses. The headline findings are that domain-specific biosignal foundation models beat general time-series models, that ECG+PPG fusion beats either signal alone, that using the full 10-minute window beats shorter segments, that larger model variants do not reliably beat smaller ones, and that hand-crafted ECG features stay competitive as complementary baselines.

## Delta from parents
Where the Gu 2025 survey described what biosignal foundation models should be and HeAR built one for a single acoustic modality, SignalMC-MED supplies the controlled measuring stick: a fixed set of paired ECG+PPG tasks on which any such model can be compared, because the field had models but no common multimodal clinical benchmark. The delta is empirical rather than architectural: it isolates which design choices matter (modality fusion, signal length, model size) and reports that bigger is not reliably better and that hand-crafted features still help. This is the negative-result counterweight to the foundation-model push in Leo's library.

## Why Leo should care
This is in Leo's primary area and gives him a standard way to compare biosignal foundation models, including the kind of cross-modal models he tracks. The finding that hand-crafted features remain competitive echoes the FEEL result already in his graph (handcrafted features beat raw-signal models under noise), so the two together form a consistent caution about over-trusting raw-signal foundation models.
