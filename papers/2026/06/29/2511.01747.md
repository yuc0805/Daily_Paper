# AnyPPG: An ECG-Guided PPG Foundation Model Trained on Over 100,000 Hours of Recordings

**Arxiv:** 2511.01747 | **Date:** 2026-06-29 | **Area:** llm-health

## What it does
AnyPPG is a foundation model for photoplethysmography (PPG) pretrained on large-scale multi-source PPG paired with synchronized ECG, using the ECG channel as guidance for the PPG encoder. Pretraining covers over 100,000 hours of recordings. The model is evaluated across public datasets including PPG-DaLiA, the UCI cuff-less blood-pressure dataset, and WESAD, covering tasks from activity and stress to physiological estimation.

## Delta from parents
Where the Gu 2025 survey laid out single-modality biosignal pretraining and HeAR built one for acoustics, AnyPPG adds a cross-signal guidance step: it uses synchronized ECG to supervise the PPG encoder during pretraining, because PPG alone is noisy and ECG carries a cleaner cardiac timing signal that constrains what the PPG representation should capture. The scale (over 100,000 hours) and the paired-modality pretraining are the deltas; the downstream protocol stays close to prior biosignal foundation-model evaluations.
