# Agent Planning Benchmark (APB): A Diagnostic Framework for Planning Capabilities in LLM Agents

**Arxiv:** 2606.04874 | **Date:** 2026-06-03 | **Area:** agent

## What it does
APB is a planning-specific diagnostic benchmark with 4,209 multimodal cases across 22 domains. It covers holistic planning, feedback-conditioned step-wise planning, and robustness under extraneous tools, broken tools, and unsolvable tasks. Testing 12 MLLMs, it reveals systematic weaknesses in long-horizon planning and calibrated refusal. APB-guided refinement improves plan correctness on downstream execution benchmarks (ToolSandbox, tau-bench).

## Delta from parents
Where ADaPT and LATS proposed specific planning algorithms and evaluated on narrow task suites, APB creates a systematic diagnostic framework that separates planning failures from execution failures. The key addition is testing robustness under adversarial conditions (broken tools, extraneous tools, unsolvable tasks) — none of the prior agent frameworks evaluated calibrated refusal. APB shows that even frontier models fail at knowing when to say "this task cannot be done."

## Why Leo should care
This benchmark provides a structured way to evaluate any agent system, including potential TS-agent or health-agent pipelines. The finding that calibrated refusal is systematically weak is relevant for clinical deployment where refusing an uncertain interpretation is better than hallucinating one.
