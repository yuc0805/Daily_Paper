# Vivaldi: A Multi-Agent Framework for Interpreting Multivariate Physiological Time Series

**Arxiv:** 2603.04142 | **Date:** 2026-03-04 | **Area:** time-series, llm-health, agent

## What it does
Vivaldi is a role-structured multi-agent system that produces natural-language explanations of multivariate physiological time series. Each agent handles a distinct function (signal decomposition, clinical contextualization, synthesis). A controlled pilot with emergency medicine experts shows that agentic pipelines improve explanation quality by +6.9 (justification) and +9.7 (relevance) points for non-thinking and medically fine-tuned models, though gains are smaller for frontier reasoning models.

## Delta from parents
Where TS-Agent uses a single LLM controller with atomic operators for generic time-series reasoning, Vivaldi introduces a multi-agent architecture with specialized roles for clinical physiological data and validates with real clinicians. Where Sensor2Text trains a sensor-language bridge, Vivaldi avoids alignment training entirely by distributing interpretation across cooperating agents. The clinical evaluation reveals that the benefit of agentic interpretation is model-dependent — an important nuance missing from prior work.
