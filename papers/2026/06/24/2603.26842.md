# VAN-AD: Visual Masked Autoencoder with Normalizing Flow for Time Series Anomaly Detection

**Arxiv:** 2603.26842 | **Date:** 2026-06-24 | **Area:** time-series

## What it does
Renders a numeric time series as an image-like input, masks it with a checkerboard pattern, and reconstructs it with a masked autoencoder; reconstruction error signals an anomaly. A statistical-space mapping amplifies the gap on abnormal windows, and a normalizing-flow module models window probability density so the anomaly score is a calibrated likelihood rather than a raw error.

## Delta from parents
Where MAE learns image representations by reconstructing masked patches, VAN-AD reuses the masked-reconstruction objective as an anomaly score for multivariate time series, then adds a normalizing-flow density model because reconstruction error alone does not give a calibrated probability of abnormality. The contribution is the combination plus the statistical-space step that widens the score gap between normal and abnormal windows.
