# Imaginative Perception Tokens Enhance Spatial Reasoning in Multimodal Language Models

**Arxiv:** 2606.03988 | **Date:** 2026-06-24 | **Area:** multi-modal

## What it does
Introduces Imaginative Perception Tokens (IPT), intermediate perceptual representations a vision-language model is trained to produce that record what it would perceive under an alternative, unobserved spatial configuration. Across three constructed tasks (Perspective Taking, Path Tracing, Multiview Counting) with about 20,000 examples each, IPT supervision improves spatial reasoning and often beats textual chain-of-thought, while textual chain-of-thought can lower performance.

## Direct parents (in Leo's library)
- [HBLPTRMY] Chain-of-Thought Prompting (Wei 2023) — established the intermediate reasoning-step trace as a way to improve model performance.

## Delta from parents
Where Chain-of-Thought makes a model reason in natural-language steps, IPT moves the reasoning trace into a perceptual representation in the native visual modality, because pushing spatial computation through language distorts it. The paper reports a modality mismatch: the text trace not only fails to help on these spatial tasks, it can actively hurt, and the perceptual trace recovers the lost performance without generating images at inference time.

## Why Leo should care
The transferable point is that when a problem is non-linguistic, supervising an intermediate representation in the native modality can beat verbalizing the reasoning. For time-series and bio-sensing, where forcing numeric reasoning through text is the usual failure mode, this suggests supervising a native-signal intermediate (a reconstructed or counterfactual signal segment) rather than a verbal trace. No new method is imported into time-series here; the idea is the import candidate.
