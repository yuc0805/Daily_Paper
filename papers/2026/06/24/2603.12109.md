# On Information Self-Locking in Reinforcement Learning for Active Reasoning of LLM Agents

**Arxiv:** 2603.12109 | **Date:** 2026-06-24 | **Area:** agent

## What it does
Identifies a failure mode in reinforcement-learning training of question-asking agents: the agent stops asking informative questions and stops absorbing evidence it already has, locking into a low-information loop. It decomposes active reasoning into action selection and belief tracking, shows weakness in either starves exploration during training, and proposes injecting cheap directional critiques to break the loop, reducing self-locking.
