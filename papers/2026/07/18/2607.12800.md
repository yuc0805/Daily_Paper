# UniVR: Thinking in Visual Space for Unified Visual Reasoning

**Arxiv:** 2607.12800 | **Date:** 2026-07-18 | **Area:** multi-modal

## What it does
UniVR learns reasoning, physical dynamics, and long-horizon planning from pure visual demonstrations, with no image-text pairs. Its core is VR-GRPO, a reinforcement learning scheme with a global reward for final-answer correctness and a step-level reward that enforces logical and physical consistency along the reasoning process. The authors build VR-X, a benchmark from 16 sources covering long-horizon manipulation, spatial puzzles, and physical reasoning, evaluated under a purely visual protocol. UniVR reports up to 25% improvement on VR-X and higher scores on standard multimodal understanding benchmarks.

## Direct parents (in Leo's library)

- [HBLPTRMY] Chain-of-Thought Prompting (Wei 2023) — established explicit step-by-step reasoning in language.

## Delta from parents
Where DeepSeek-R1 rewards a correct final answer in text and Chain-of-Thought produces the reasoning trace in language tokens, UniVR moves both the reasoning and the reward into visual space and adds a step-level consistency reward, because a text-only reward cannot check whether an intermediate visual state is physically plausible. It is the reinforcement-learning-for-reasoning recipe already in the library, retargeted from language to raw pixels, with the reward split into a global term and a per-step physical-consistency term.

## Why Leo should care
The transferable piece is the step-level consistency reward. In language reasoning the reward is usually on the final answer; UniVR shows a per-step reward that checks physical plausibility of intermediate states. For physiological time-series reasoning, an analogous per-step reward could check whether each intermediate claim is consistent with signal physics (for example, that an inferred heart-rate change is physically reachable), rather than only scoring the final label.
