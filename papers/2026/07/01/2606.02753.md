# MetaWorld: Scaling Multi-Agent Video World Models from Single-view Video Data

**Arxiv:** 2606.02753 | **Date:** 2026-07-01 | **Area:** generative-cv

## What it does
MetaWorld trains multi-agent video world models from ordinary single-view videos, avoiding expensive multi-camera recordings. Monocular World-State Unrolling decomposes each clip into the camera operator's ego-motion and the visible subject's trajectory, recovering synchronized multi-agent motion inside one shared 3D space. A Subject-Aware World Generator conditions on per-agent identity images, and a World-State Alignment module inserts per-frame cross-attention at every layer of the video diffusion transformer so multiple views denoise together and stay geometrically and dynamically consistent. Experiments report better cross-view consistency and identity fidelity than single-view baselines.

## Direct parents (in Leo's library)
- [YJ9TK993] DiT: Scalable Diffusion Models with Transformers (Peebles 2023) — the diffusion-transformer backbone for generation.
- [GX7WR7KA] DDPM: Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models (Ho 2020) — the denoising process the model runs.

## Delta from parents
Where DiT generates one view by denoising a single latent stream, MetaWorld runs coupled streams and ties them with cross-attention at every transformer layer, because independent denoising cannot keep a shared world consistent across views. The contribution is a coupling mechanism and a monocular decomposition step added on top of the DiT and DDPM machinery, so multi-view training data can be extracted from single-view footage.

## Why Leo should care
The idea worth porting is world-state alignment: enforcing that several views of one scene stay consistent by coupling their generation. Applied to physiological sensing, several sensor channels recorded at once (PPG, ECG, accelerometer) are different views of one physiological state, and a cross-view consistency loss could enforce that a single latent state explains all channels, helping when one channel is noisy or missing. That transfer is not yet demonstrated.
