# Tandem Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards

**Arxiv:** 2606.28166 | **Date:** 2026-07-01 | **Area:** reasoning

## What it does
Tandem Reinforcement Learning (TRL) trains a strong "senior" model together with a frozen weaker "junior". The two alternate stochastically to co-generate each reasoning rollout, the joint output is scored by a verifiable reward, and the standard GRPO loss updates only the senior. This pushes the senior to reason in a way the junior can continue. Training Qwen3-4B-Instruct on competition math, TRL matches plain GRPO on solo accuracy while producing stronger handoff to the junior, less distributional drift from the junior, and a chain of thought the junior finds more legible.

## Direct parents (in Leo's library)
- [Z5IWHZAE] DeepSeek-R1 (2025) — showed that reinforcement learning with a simple verifiable reward (GRPO) produces structured reasoning without a learned reward model.
- [HBLPTRMY] Chain-of-Thought Prompting (Wei 2023) — produced legible intermediate reasoning steps by prompting.

## Delta from parents
Where DeepSeek-R1 optimized a single model for correctness and let the reasoning style drift, TRL adds a second frozen model to the rollout and rewards the pair, because a correct but unreadable trace cannot be used by a weaker model or a person. Where Chain-of-Thought asked for legible steps through prompting, TRL makes legibility an emergent property of the training structure rather than a prompt instruction. The change is a two-model rollout that keeps solo skill while restoring compatibility.

## Why Leo should care
This sits on the DeepSeek-R1 to Chain-of-Thought bridge in your library (reasoning via reinforcement learning versus reasoning via prompting). It also suggests a transfer for time-series work: co-train a large time-series reasoning model with a frozen small model that can run on a watch, so the reasoning hands off to an on-device model for continuous monitoring. That transfer is not yet demonstrated for time series.
