# ISO: An RLVR-Native Optimization Stack

**Arxiv:** 2607.19331 | **Date:** 2026-07-22 | **Area:** optimizer

## What it does
The paper studies what reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) actually changes in a model's weights. Using a singular value decomposition of each weight matrix, it reports spectral inheritance: RLVR mostly keeps the base model's singular values and moves the new behavior into the input and output singular frames. It turns this into Isospectral Optimization (ISO), which freezes the spectrum and optimizes only the frames. Offline, ISO-Merger combines shared-base specialists with no extra data, rollouts, or gradients. Online, ISO-Optimizer runs AdamW or Muon on the frame variables; on Qwen3-8B-Base it matches AdamW's aggregate accuracy of 0.495 in 100 steps instead of 270, then reaches 0.509.

## Direct parents (in Leo's library)
- [JRH3CE5M] Adam — The base optimizer ISO-Optimizer runs on the frame variables while holding the spectrum fixed.
- [Z5IWHZAE] DeepSeek-R1 — Defined the RLVR reasoning setting whose weight-space behavior ISO analyzes and speeds up.

## Delta from parents
Where Adam updates every weight coordinate freely, ISO restricts the same optimizer to the singular frames and keeps the base singular values frozen, on the observation that RLVR does not need to move them. Where DeepSeek-R1 treated RLVR as a full-model update and paid the associated step count, ISO argues the useful change lives in a lower-dimensional subspace and reaches matched accuracy in up to 2.7 times fewer steps. The offline ISO-Merger side also replaces on-policy distillation for combining specialists with a data-free spectral composition.

## Why Leo should care
This connects the optimizer area to reasoning training and gives a concrete, measurable claim: the spectrum is close to fixed under RLVR, so post-training can be designed around the frames. For anyone fine-tuning foundation models on limited compute, including biosignal or time-series models, the fixed-spectrum idea is a cheap thing to test and could cut fine-tuning cost. It is a clean example of an optimizer-level method that does not assume the text domain.
