# TurnOPD: Making On-Policy Distillation Turn-Aware for Efficient Long-Horizon Agent Training

**Arxiv:** 2607.05804 | **Date:** 2026-07-12 | **Area:** agent (Community 0: LLM Agents & Reasoning)

## What it does
Improves how a student agent is trained to imitate a stronger teacher on its own rollouts (on-policy distillation) for long-horizon tasks. The authors identify two inefficiencies in vanilla agent on-policy distillation: full-horizon rollouts spend wall-clock time on tail turns that give weak, noisy KL supervision, and trajectory-level KL objectives pile most of the loss on shallow early tokens, leaving deep decision turns under-trained. TurnOPD adds two turn-level budget controllers: adaptive rollout-depth budgeting, which uses probe-based turn statistics to set rollout length, and progressive turn-normalized loss budgeting, which shifts KL weight from token-level toward turn-balanced over training. On ALFWorld, WebShop, and Multi-Hop Search it reaches higher validation accuracy than vanilla on-policy distillation under equal wall-clock budget.

## Delta from parents
Where DeepSeek-R1 trained reasoning with reinforcement learning on outcome rewards and PPO optimized on-policy at the token level, TurnOPD keeps the on-policy idea but changes the unit of accounting from token to turn, because in long agent rollouts uniform token weighting starves the deep decision points that matter. Where LATS spent inference-time search to reach good long-horizon trajectories, TurnOPD spends training-time budget to distill a teacher into the student efficiently. This paper extends the DeepSeek-R1 to Chain-of-Thought bridge in your library (reasoning via reinforcement learning versus reasoning via prompting): R1 already crossed prompting and reinforcement learning, and TurnOPD pushes that reinforcement-learning line toward multi-turn agent distillation with turn-aware credit.

## Why Leo should care
The transferable idea is turn-normalized credit assignment for multi-step agents, and it is not yet applied to time-series or bio-sensing agents such as TS-Agent or Sensor2Text. A wearable-sensing agent that queries PPG or ECG streams over many steps has the same problem TurnOPD names, so the method is an open porting opportunity: replace text turns with sensor-query turns and budget supervision by decision depth.
