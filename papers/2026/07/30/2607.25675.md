# DecoEvo: Score-Decoupled Co-Evolution of Solver and Rubric-Generator Skills in Text Space

**Arxiv:** 2607.25675 | **Date:** 2026-07-30 | **Area:** reasoning

## What it does
DecoEvo improves a language model on open-ended tasks by editing external natural-language artifacts, a solver skill and the rubric that grades it, rather than model weights. It separates the two update signals: the solver is updated with criterion-level feedback, while the rubric generator is revised through audits of requirement coverage and answer discrimination that do not use the solver's aggregate score. It reports 2.8 to 5.0 percent relative gains over SkillOpt averaged across five benchmarks and three backbones.

## Delta from parents
Where DeepSeek-R1 trains against a single fixed reward and Chain-of-Thought fixes the reasoning trace format, DecoEvo makes the evaluator itself a moving, separately-audited target. The reason is that a static reward or rubric stops separating good answers from bad ones once the solver saturates it, and updating the rubric by the solver's own score can be gamed by making the rubric easier. This is the same reward-hacking concern that Skill Self-Play (2607.22529) raised in the 2026-07-27 digest, handled here by decoupling the score that trains the solver from the audit that trains the grader.
