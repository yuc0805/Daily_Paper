# Forecasting Realized Volatility with Time Series Foundation Models: A Comparison with Econometric Benchmarks

**Arxiv:** 2607.05291 | **Date:** 2026-07-30 | **Area:** time-series

## What it does
This paper tests whether pretrained time-series foundation models beat classical econometric models at forecasting realized volatility. It finds that performance varies a lot across foundation-model architectures, and that picking the right architecture matters more than the broad choice between a foundation model and an econometric baseline.

## Delta from parents
Where Chronos is presented as a general zero-shot forecaster, this paper measures where that generality holds and where it fails on a hard, non-stationary financial target. It connects to the Post-Training framework by showing that the frozen model's architecture, not only its weights, sets the accuracy ceiling, which is the motivation for the adaptation and output-processing families that framework catalogs.
