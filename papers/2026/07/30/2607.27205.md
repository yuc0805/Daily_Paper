# TurboVLA: Real-Time Vision-Language-Action Model at 32 Hz on an RTX 4090 with under 1 GB VRAM

**Arxiv:** 2607.27205 | **Date:** 2026-07-30 | **Area:** multi-modal

## What it does
TurboVLA reports a vision-language-action policy that runs at 32 Hz on a single consumer RTX 4090 while using under 1 GB of GPU memory. The contribution is on the deployment side of the VLA line rather than new task capability: prior open VLA models are accurate but too heavy and too slow for closed-loop control on commodity hardware.

## Direct parents (in Leo's library)
No direct parents in Leo's library; vision-language-action models are not tracked. Closest neighbor:
- [SC8KWYVK] Flamingo (graphify Community 3, Vision-Language and Generative) — the image-plus-text encoder pattern that VLA policies extend with an action head.

## Delta from parents
Where Flamingo answered questions about an image, TurboVLA maps image and instruction to motor actions under a hard latency and memory budget, because control has to close the loop many times per second. The novelty relative to the vision-language line is not the input encoder but the compression and scheduling that let the policy run in real time within sub-1 GB memory.

## Why Leo should care
Indirect but useful. The number to watch is the memory footprint: sub-1 GB continuous inference is the regime that on-device and wearable sensing also need. The aggressive memory and latency budgeting has not been pushed into wearable bio-sensing foundation models, which is an open transfer.
