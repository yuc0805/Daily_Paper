# Expert-Guided Forecast Editing for Time-Series Foundation Models

**Arxiv:** 2607.19659 | **Date:** 2026-07-30 | **Area:** time-series

## What it does
A time-series foundation model produces a forecast that is then fixed, with no clean way to fold in expert feedback short of retraining. DEFT edits the forecast at test time while keeping the model frozen. It first exploits the model's predictive samples in a decomposed trend-and-seasonal space, then explores by refining trend and seasonal components separately, and it reuses each expensive expert score across the components that appeared in the scored trajectories so one query yields component-level feedback.

## Delta from parents
Where Chronos produces a fixed forecast and the cross-entropy method searches the horizon as an unstructured high-dimensional vector, DEFT does neither in isolation. It exploits Chronos-style predictive samples inside a trend-seasonal decomposition and then explores component-wise, because a scarce expert query buys more information when its score is reused across structured components than when it grades one whole guess. Relative to the Post-Training framework, DEFT supplies a concrete method for the calibrated output-processing direction that framework named as open. It therefore links Community 4 (Time Series + LLM Integration) to Community 7 (Reinforcement Learning) by importing a query-efficient search idea into forecast editing.

## Why Leo should care
Directly in the primary area and fully test-time: no retraining, frozen model, cost is a few expert scores. For bio-signal forecasting where a clinician or a physical constraint can rank candidate trajectories but labels are scarce, the component-level query-reuse trick is portable. It also imports test-time search from reinforcement learning and optimization into time-series editing, so that class of method is now largely closed off as an easy transfer for the general forecasting case.
