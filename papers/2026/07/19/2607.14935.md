# VideoChat3: Fully Open Video MLLM for Efficient and Generalist Video Understanding

**Arxiv:** 2607.14935 | **Date:** 2026-07-19 | **Area:** multi-modal

## What it does
VideoChat3 is a fully open 4B-parameter video multimodal LLM that handles short motion, hour-long video, temporal grounding, and live streaming in one model. It introduces an Inflated 3D Vision Transformer (I3D-ViT) for spatiotemporal features and an Adaptive Frame Resolution scheme for streaming perception, and it is trained with a scalable data-synthesis pipeline yielding three datasets (Academic2M, LV116K, OL617K). The reported claim is that it beats prior open-source video MLLMs of equal or larger size.

## Direct parents (in Leo's library)
- [WYQSCSRF] I3D — inflated 2D convolutions into 3D to read motion from video.
- [SC8KWYVK] Flamingo — bridged a frozen vision encoder to a language model for image-and-text dialogue.
- [4N5WXKPI] BLIP-2 — connected a frozen image encoder and LLM through a lightweight query transformer.

## Delta from parents
Where I3D inflated 2D convolutions into 3D, VideoChat3 inflates a 2D Vision Transformer into an I3D-ViT, carrying the inflation trick from ConvNets to transformers. Where Flamingo and BLIP-2 bridged a frozen vision encoder to an LLM with fixed frame sampling, VideoChat3 adds streaming-aware adaptive frame resolution, because hour-long and live video make fixed sampling too costly. The result stays in the vision-language community but pushes it toward efficient long and streaming video.

## Why Leo should care
The inflation-of-a-transformer idea and the streaming frame-resolution controller are portable design patterns. Streaming perception at constant cost is the same pressure faced in continuous bio-signal and wearable streams, so the adaptive-resolution idea is worth noting for sensor time series.
