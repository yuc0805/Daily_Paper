# TiRex-2: Generalizing TiRex to Multivariate Data and Streaming

**Arxiv:** 2607.01204 | **Date:** 2026-07-19 | **Area:** time-series

## What it does
TiRex-2 is an xLSTM-based time-series foundation model that forecasts one or many target series directly from history, optionally conditioned on past and future-known covariates, in a streaming fashion and zero-shot. Its recurrent, memory-centric design keeps cost per new patch constant under streaming; it pairs a bidirectional time mixer with an asymmetric grouped-attention variate mixer that mixes across variables while keeping strict causality on the targets. Pretraining uses a synthetic coupling pipeline that composes multivariate samples on the fly from univariate corpora.

## Delta from parents
Where HARMamba and Bi-Mamba+ used recurrent state-space backbones for constant-cost sequence modeling on sensor and forecasting data, TiRex-2 uses an xLSTM recurrent backbone with the same constant-per-step goal but extends it to true multivariate forecasting with future-known covariates and streaming updates, because Transformer foundation models pay quadratic context cost and recompute full history as new points arrive. Where the survey framed time-series ability as something to obtain by attaching an LLM, TiRex-2 stays fully numeric and recurrent, so it sits on the native-forecasting branch rather than the LLM-integration branch.
