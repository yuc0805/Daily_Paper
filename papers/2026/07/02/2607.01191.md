# Perceive-to-Reason: Decoupling Perception and Reasoning for Fine-Grained Visual Reasoning

**Arxiv:** 2607.01191 | **Date:** 2026-07-02 | **Area:** multi-modal

## What it does
Perceive-to-Reason (P2R) splits fine-grained visual reasoning into two stages: a Perceiver that localizes the question-relevant region in a high-resolution image, and a Reasoner that answers using the annotated image and the cropped region. Training uses Perception-Reasoning Alternating GRPO (PRA-GRPO), which alternates perception-focused and reasoning-focused updates with only final-answer supervision, so no bounding-box labels are needed. On Qwen3-VL-Instruct backbones, P2R-4B reaches 93.2% on V-Star, 81.9% on HR-Bench-4K, and 80.5% on HR-Bench-8K.

## Direct parents (in Leo's library)
- [U4ZPM5DN] Latent-GRPO (Deng 2026) — a GRPO variant; PRA-GRPO is a role-aware GRPO variant.
- [Z5IWHZAE] DeepSeek-R1 (2025) — the reward-based reasoning optimizer (GRPO/RLVR) that both build on.
- [FPEC4PIH] Large Multimodal Reasoning Survey (Li 2025) — maps the multimodal-reasoning landscape P2R refines.

## Delta from parents
Where plain GRPO applies one undifferentiated reward to the whole reasoning trace, PRA-GRPO alternates between rewarding perception steps and rewarding reasoning steps, because a single reward cannot tell the model whether it failed at finding the evidence or at reasoning over it. Relative to test-time cropping and visual-search methods, P2R makes the perceive-then-reason split explicit and trains it end to end from final-answer reward alone, avoiding region annotation.

## Why Leo should care
The decoupled perceive-then-reason structure and the alternating role-aware reward are directly portable to signal reasoning: a Perceiver stage could localize the diagnostically relevant window or channel in a long multichannel recording, and a Reasoner stage could answer over it, trained with only the final label. That transfer to time-series and bio-sensing is not yet done, so it is an open opportunity.
