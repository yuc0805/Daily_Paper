# Cura 1T: Specialized Model for Agentic Healthcare

**Arxiv:** 2607.15314 | **Date:** 2026-07-20 | **Area:** llm-health

## What it does
Cura 1T is a healthcare-specialized language model trained through a human-gated self-evolution loop. In each round a training agent picks a target capability, trains the model, reads benchmark trajectories, and rewrites the data mixture from the observed failures, rather than doing one generic medical-data update. The loop is meant to stop the usual problem where fixing one healthcare skill degrades another (consultation, clinical reasoning over text and images, interactive diagnosis, electronic health record tool use). It ranks at or near the top of a healthcare evaluation suite among frontier baselines while staying competitive on out-of-domain reasoning and agentic benchmarks.

## Delta from parents
Where Liu 2023 showed few-shot health competence and CounselBench measured failure modes, Cura 1T turns those measured failures into the training signal itself through a closed loop, because a single medical fine-tune cannot cover consultation, imaging, and tool use at once without regressing one for another. Where Gu 2025 catalogued fixed biosignal encoders, Cura 1T is an agentic system whose data mixture changes each round, placing it on the self-improving-system branch rather than the fixed-encoder branch.
