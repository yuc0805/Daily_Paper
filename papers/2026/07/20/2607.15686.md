# S1-Omni: A Unified Multimodal Reasoning Model for Scientific Understanding, Prediction, and Generation

**Arxiv:** 2607.15686 | **Date:** 2026-07-20 | **Area:** multi-modal

## What it does
S1-Omni maps heterogeneous scientific objects (crystal structure files, molecular SMILES strings, protein sequences, spectra, scientific images) plus natural-language instructions into one shared representation, then uses task-specific decoders for property prediction, spectrum-to-molecule generation, protein site and structure prediction, and scientific image generation and editing. It is trained on a corpus covering 200 scientific tasks with millions of reasoning samples and evaluated on more than 60 benchmarks, reportedly outperforming GPT-5.5 and Gemini-3.1-Pro on most and matching domain-specific models on several.

## Direct parents (in Leo's library)
- [DUERBZGM] DALL-E 2 (Ramesh 2022) — aligned text and images in one embedding space.
- [YJ9TK993] DiT (Peebles 2023) — showed a transformer backbone can drive diffusion generation.

## Delta from parents
Where DALL-E 2 aligned text and images in a shared space and DiT drove generation with a transformer backbone, S1-Omni does the same shared-space-plus-decoder move but across many scientific modalities at once, because a spectrum or a protein sequence needs a domain-native decoder rather than a pixel decoder. No direct scientific-modality parent sits in Leo's library yet; the closest neighbors are these two generative vision-language anchors in graphify community 3.

## Why Leo should care
The unified heterogeneous-modality design is a candidate pattern for wearable sensing: map PPG, IMU, ECG, and temperature plus instructions into one shared space with per-signal decoders. Community 5 covers single-stream wearable modeling but not a shared-space model with signal-native decoders across all channels at once, so this is an open transfer.
