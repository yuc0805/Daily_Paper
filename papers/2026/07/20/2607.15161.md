# On-Policy Delta Distillation (OPD^2)

**Arxiv:** 2607.15161 | **Date:** 2026-07-20 | **Area:** llm

## What it does
On-policy distillation transfers a teacher's reasoning by giving token-level supervision on the student's own rollouts, which avoids the need for a reward model. OPD^2 changes the target: instead of imitating the teacher's full output distribution, it distills the delta signal, the difference between the reasoning-tuned teacher and its base model before instruction tuning. That difference isolates what reasoning tuning added, giving a cleaner transfer signal. Across mathematics, science, and code-reasoning benchmarks OPD^2 beats standard on-policy distillation; gains hold across several Qwen3 sizes in thinking and non-thinking modes and transfer to Gemma 4, with only a short post-training period.

## Delta from parents
Where DeepSeek-R1 produced reasoning through RL and left a costly teacher, OPD^2 does cheap transfer of exactly that ability, because copying the whole teacher distribution also copies base-model behavior the student already has. The delta idea, subtracting the base model from the tuned teacher, is the same "measure what tuning added" move that the day's Tier A chess paper makes by comparing fine-tuned and post-RL policies. ## Why Leo should care
Distilling only the reasoning delta is a transferable recipe for Leo's watched areas: a reasoning-tuned time-series forecaster and its base could be differenced, and only the added forecasting-reasoning behavior distilled into a small student. This keeps the numeric backbone the student already has and avoids re-copying it.
