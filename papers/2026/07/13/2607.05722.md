# Nemotron-Labs-Diffusion: A Tri-Mode Language Model Unifying Autoregressive, Diffusion, and Self-Speculation Decoding

**Arxiv:** 2607.05722 | **Date:** 2026-07-13 | **Area:** llm (also generative-cv lineage)

## What it does
Nemotron-Labs-Diffusion trains one network with a joint autoregressive-plus-diffusion objective so it can switch decoding modes at deployment. Diffusion supplies lookahead planning; autoregression supplies left-to-right language priors. In self-speculation mode diffusion drafts and autoregression verifies, beating multi-token prediction on acceptance rate and real-device speed. At 3B, 8B, and 14B parameters it reports higher accuracy and speed than open baselines; the 8B model decodes 6x more tokens per forward pass than Qwen3-8B at comparable accuracy.

## Delta from parents
Where DDPM applies diffusion to continuous images and the Transformer decodes text one token at a time, this paper trains a single model under both objectives so diffusion handles parallel lookahead and autoregression handles verification, because each covers the other's weak spot in decoding. It joins two lineages in Leo's library (autoregressive Transformer and image diffusion) that rarely meet in one model.

## Why Leo should care
A joint autoregressive-diffusion objective is a candidate for wearable forecasting, which needs both tight next-step vitals prediction and long-horizon trend planning. Method imported: dual-objective training that supports mode switching. Not yet ported to Community 4 (Time Series + LLM), so the transfer is open.
