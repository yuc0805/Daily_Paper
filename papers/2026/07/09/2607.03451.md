# SkillOpt-Lite: Agent Self-evolution via Zeroth-Order Optimization

**Arxiv:** 2607.03451 | **Date:** 2026-07-09 | **Area:** agent (Community 0: LLM Agents & Reasoning)

## What it does
Reframes agent skill self-evolution as zeroth-order optimization and reduces the pipeline to three justified parts: file-system trajectory exploration, consensus attribute mining, and independent validation gating. SkillOpt-Lite converges faster than full SkillOpt and improves LiveMath by 8.8 points on GPT-5.5 and 25.4 points on GPT-5.4-nano. Extended to full harness optimization, it reaches 0.7758 on SpreadsheetBench with GPT-5.4-nano, above GPT-5.5 at 0.7620.

## Delta from parents
Where ADaPT decomposed tasks on demand and LATS searched over action trees per episode, SkillOpt-Lite instead optimizes the reusable skills and harness themselves offline, treating a skill as editable code updated by a numerical-optimization loop with convergence and generalization arguments from zeroth-order optimization and PAC learning. This paper extends ADaPT and LATS, which already bridge planning-decomposition and tree-search agents in the library, pushing that bridge from per-episode planning toward persistent, improvable skill libraries.

## Why Leo should care
The zeroth-order framing turns skill improvement into a small, analyzable optimization problem, which is a cleaner way to think about self-improving research agents than ad hoc loops. Watch as a method to port: interpretable trajectory feedback as the gradient surrogate could apply to automated experimentation over time-series pipelines.
