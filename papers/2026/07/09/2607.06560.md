# Vision as Unified Multimodal Generation (SenseNova-Vision)

**Arxiv:** 2607.06560 | **Date:** 2026-07-09 | **Area:** multi-modal (Community 3: Vision-Language & Generative)

## What it does
Poses many computer-vision tasks (detection, OCR, keypoints, segmentation, depth, surface normals, point maps, camera pose) as one generation problem inside a unified multimodal model. Natural-language instructions plus optional visual prompts go in; text, image, or mixed outputs come out, with no task-specific heads. Training uses a 50M instruction-response corpus converted from standard vision annotations, and a single model matches leading task-specialized systems across structured understanding, dense geometric prediction, segmentation, and multi-view geometry.

## Direct parents (in Leo's library)
- [DUERBZGM] DALL-E 2 (ramesh2022_dalle2) — hierarchical text-conditional image generation.
- [YJ9TK993] DiT (peebles2023_dit) — Transformer backbone for diffusion image generation.
- [PHB9VRVM] Attention Is All You Need (vaswani2017_transformer) — the shared architecture.

## Delta from parents
Where DALL-E 2 and DiT generated pixels from text for their own sake, SenseNova-Vision reuses the same generative image space to emit dense predictions such as depth and segmentation, because posing perception as conditional image generation removes the need for a separate decoder per task. The contribution is less a new generator than a reformulation: convert diverse vision labels into instruction-response pairs in text and image spaces, then train one model on all of them.

## Why Leo should care
The "perception as conditional generation" recipe is a candidate to port to signals: pose imputation, anomaly localization, and forecasting as masked-region generation over a 2D time-frequency map, so one generative model handles several sensor tasks without a per-task head. Not yet applied to time series.
