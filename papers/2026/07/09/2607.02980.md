# HiLS-Attention: Hierarchical Sparse Attention Done Right

**Arxiv:** 2607.02980 | **Date:** 2026-07-09 | **Area:** llm (Community 2: Transformer & SSM Architectures)

## What it does
Introduces Hierarchical Landmark Sparse (HiLS) Attention, a chunk-wise sparse attention mechanism that learns chunk selection end-to-end under the language-modeling loss. Each query attends independently to each retrieved chunk, and the outputs are fused by chunk retrieval scores that are trained inside the forward pass. This gives sub-quadratic cost while matching or beating full attention and extrapolating past training length.

## Direct parents (in Leo's library)
- [PHB9VRVM] Attention Is All You Need (vaswani2017_transformer) — established dense self-attention as the only sequence-mixing operator, at quadratic cost.
- [HE9X47KN] HARMamba (li2024_harmamba) — represents the Mamba / state-space line that cuts attention cost with a fixed-size recurrent state.

## Delta from parents
Where the Transformer read every token pair at quadratic cost and Mamba compressed history into a fixed recurrent state (losing content-based random access), HiLS keeps attention's content addressing but only over selected chunks, because it learns which chunks to retrieve under the LM loss. The retrieval scores enter the attention computation directly, so selection is differentiable rather than a separate heuristic retriever. The result is one mechanism that is both cheaper and better on long-context tasks, and that can be grafted onto an existing full-attention model with light continued pretraining.

## Why Leo should care
Long physiological and wearable recordings are the setting where quadratic attention fails first. HiLS imports the idea of a learned, differentiable retriever over sequence chunks; that idea is not yet applied to time series, so it is an open porting opportunity — swap text chunks for fixed-duration signal windows and learn which past windows to read under a forecasting or classification loss.
