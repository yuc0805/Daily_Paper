# SEED: Self-Evolving On-Policy Distillation for Agentic Reinforcement Learning

**Arxiv:** 2607.14777 | **Date:** 2026-07-17 | **Area:** agent

## What it does
SEED trains an LLM agent for long-horizon tasks by turning its own finished trajectories into training-time "hindsight skills" and distilling their effect back into the policy. The current policy both collects trajectories and analyzes them, writing natural-language rules that capture reusable workflows, decisive observations, or failure-avoidance heuristics. It then re-scores sampled actions under plain and skill-augmented contexts and converts the probability shift into a dense token-level signal, which is optimized jointly with ordinary outcome-based reinforcement learning. On text and vision agentic benchmarks it improves both accuracy and sample efficiency and generalizes to unseen scenarios.

## Delta from parents
Where DeepSeek-R1 gives the model a single trajectory-level reward, SEED adds a dense token-level signal by re-scoring each action with and without self-written hindsight skills, so intermediate decisions receive guidance that sparse outcome rewards cannot supply. Where ADaPT and LATS add planning or search structure at inference time, SEED internalizes the improvement into the weights during reinforcement learning, so no external skill bank or search tree is needed at test time. The skill analyzer is the policy itself, so the auxiliary supervision evolves with the policy rather than coming from a fixed teacher. This sits inside graphify Community 0 (LLM Agents and Reasoning) and extends the cross-area bridge deepseek2025_r1 to wei2023_cot, pushing the reinforcement-learning side of that bridge toward self-generated dense supervision.

## Why Leo should care
The mechanism is a self-distillation signal built from the model's own successful runs, with no extra labels or separate reward model. That pattern transfers to sparse-label settings Leo works in: a labeled day of wearable windows could be treated as a completed trajectory whose "skills" re-weight ambiguous windows. As a CV/NLP method it is not yet ported to time-series or bio-sensing, so the opportunity is open.
