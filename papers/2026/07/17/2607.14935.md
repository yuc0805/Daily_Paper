# VideoChat3: Fully Open Video MLLM for Efficient and Generalist Video Understanding

**Arxiv:** 2607.14935 | **Date:** 2026-07-17 | **Area:** multi-modal

## What it does
VideoChat3 is a fully open video multimodal LLM aimed at broad generalization at low cost. It introduces an Inflated 3D Vision Transformer (I3D-ViT) and Adaptive Frame Resolution for streaming perception, which together cut the cost of turning video into tokens during training and inference. It also releases a video data synthesis pipeline and three training sets covering general, long-form, and streaming video. With 4B parameters it reports beating prior open-source models of equal or larger size across general, long-form, and streaming video benchmarks, and it releases code, datasets, and weights.

## Direct parents (in Leo's library)

## Delta from parents
Where ViT processes a single image and Flamingo and BLIP-2 attach a frozen 2D vision encoder to an LLM, VideoChat3 inflates the Vision Transformer to three dimensions so tokens carry time, and adds adaptive frame resolution so streaming video stays affordable. The result reaches stronger video benchmarks at only 4B parameters, trading the older "freeze a 2D encoder" recipe for an efficiency-first spatiotemporal tokenizer plus a synthetic-data pipeline. Parents sit across graphify Community 3 (Vision-Language and Generative) and Community 9 (Video Action Recognition). The multi-modal god node SigLIP is not yet in Leo's library, so the closest tracked vision-language anchors are Flamingo and BLIP-2.

## Why Leo should care
The I3D-ViT idea, inflating a 2D encoder to handle time cheaply, is a direct template for wearable and physiological streams, which are also long, multi-channel sequences where token cost dominates. The adaptive-resolution trick for streaming maps onto variable-rate sensor sampling.
