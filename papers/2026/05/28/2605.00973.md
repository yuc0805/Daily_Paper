# Physiology-Aware Masked Cross-Modal Reconstruction for Biosignal Representation Learning (xMAE)

**Arxiv:** 2605.00973 | **Date:** 2026-05-01 | **Area:** time-series, llm-health, self-supervised

## What it does
xMAE is a biosignal pretraining framework that uses masked cross-modal reconstruction across temporally ordered biosignals — specifically ECG and PPG — as a training-time constraint. Instead of treating multi-sensor signals as interchangeable views, it enforces the directional temporal relationship (ECG captures electrical activation first, PPG records the resulting pulse with vascular delay). Pretraining with xMAE outperforms unimodal and multimodal baselines on 15 of 19 downstream tasks spanning cardiovascular prediction, lab test detection, sleep staging, and demographic inference.

## Delta from parents
Where Zhang 2023 studied MAE in a unimodal setting (masking and reconstructing within a single modality), xMAE extends masking across two modalities with an explicit temporal ordering constraint: the ECG signal leads the PPG signal in time, and the reconstruction loss is designed to respect this causal direction. Where Gu 2025 surveyed the landscape and flagged the lack of physiology-aware pretraining objectives as a gap, xMAE fills that gap by encoding the ECG-to-PPG pulse transit relationship directly into the loss function. Where HeAR (Baur 2024) pretrained on health audio using standard SSL, xMAE shows that incorporating known physiological structure into the objective yields stronger representations for downstream health tasks.
