# Olivia: Harmonizing Time Series Foundation Models with Power Spectral Density

**Arxiv:** 2605.17340 | **Date:** 2026-05-17 | **Area:** time-series

## What it does
Olivia is a time series foundation model that uses a Harmonizer module to reshape the power spectral density of different pretraining datasets into a shared spectral space. This corresponds to a shared reparameterization of second-order temporal correlations. A HarmonicAttention mechanism performs self-attention in a low-dimensional resonator-based interaction space. Accepted at ICML 2026. Achieves state-of-the-art on TSLib and GIFT-Eval under zero-shot, few-shot, and full-shot forecasting.

## Direct parents (in Leo's library)
- [4QQTVIYC] Sundial (Liu 2025) — TS foundation model using flow-matching for continuous-valued time series without discrete tokenization.
- [72DFULQQ] Chronos (Ansari 2024) — Pretrained probabilistic TS model using tokenization and cross-entropy loss.
- [YY67LF3R] PatchTST (Nie 2023) — Introduced patching for TS transformers, establishing local semantic retention through subseries patches.

## Delta from parents
Where Sundial used flow-matching to handle continuous values and Chronos used quantization-based tokenization, Olivia addresses a different bottleneck: spectral heterogeneity across pretraining domains. PatchTST showed that patching helps local semantic retention but assumed spectral homogeneity across datasets; Olivia removes that assumption by harmonizing power spectral densities before attention. The theoretical contribution is showing that token interactions under spectral harmonization can be mediated by a compact set of resonators, yielding a more efficient attention mechanism.

## Why Leo should care
Health signals (ECG, PPG, accelerometry) have very different spectral profiles from financial or weather time series. If you use TS foundation models on wearable data, spectral mismatch during pretraining is a real concern. Olivia's PSD harmonization is the first principled approach to this problem and could improve transfer from general-domain TS pretraining to health-specific downstream tasks.
