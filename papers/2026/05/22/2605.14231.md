# AudioMosaic: Contrastive Masked Audio Representation Learning

**Arxiv:** 2605.14231 | **Date:** 2026-05-14 | **Area:** audio

## What it does
AudioMosaic is a contrastive learning encoder for general audio. It constructs positive pairs via structured time-frequency masking on spectrogram patches, enabling memory-efficient large-batch contrastive pretraining. It outperforms generative self-supervised approaches on standard audio benchmarks under both linear probing and fine-tuning, and improves audio-language model performance when used as the audio encoder.

## Delta from parents
Where Audio Mamba changes the architecture from Transformer to SSM, AudioMosaic shows that the pretraining objective matters more than the architecture for audio. Where Yuan 2024 uses masking as a reconstruction target alongside a separate contrastive loss, AudioMosaic uses masking itself as the augmentation strategy for generating contrastive pairs — a simpler and more unified design.

## Why Leo should care
The masking-as-augmentation idea for contrastive learning could transfer directly to biosignal pretraining. Instead of designing hand-crafted augmentations for ECG/EEG (jitter, scale, crop), you could use structured time-frequency masking as a universal augmentation, which is domain-agnostic and respects the spectro-temporal structure of physiological signals.
