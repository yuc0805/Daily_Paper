# Fast Training of Mixture-of-Experts for Time Series Forecasting via Expert Loss Integration

**Arxiv:** 2605.10330 | **Date:** 2026-05-11 | **Area:** time-series

## What it does
An adaptive MoE framework for time series forecasting that adds expert-specific loss terms to the training objective, so per-expert prediction errors shape routing alongside the global forecasting loss. Combined with partial online learning for incremental updates without full retraining. Outperforms Transformers and WaveNet on economic, tourism, and energy datasets.

## Delta from parents
Where the LLM TS Survey documented the landscape of neural TS methods, this paper contributes a specific architectural innovation: feeding expert-level loss information back into MoE routing for TS. The partial online learning strategy also adds a deployment-time efficiency dimension that most TS foundation models lack — the model can adapt incrementally without full retraining when new data arrives.

## Why Leo should care
Directly relevant: MoE + time series intersection. The loss-aware routing mechanism could be applied to biosignal models where different experts specialize on different physiological modalities (ECG vs. PPG vs. accelerometer). The online learning aspect is especially relevant for wearable deployment where models must adapt to individual users.
