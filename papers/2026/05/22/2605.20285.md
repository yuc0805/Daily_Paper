# Introspective X Training: Feedback Conditioning Improves Scaling Across All LLM Training Stages

**Arxiv:** 2605.20285 | **Date:** 2026-05-19 | **Area:** llm

## What it does
IXT uses a thinking reward model to annotate pretraining data with natural language critique-based feedback. Models are then prefix-conditioned with this feedback during training, enabling quality-aware token weighting from the earliest stage of the pipeline. On 7.5-12B dense transformers trained up to 18T tokens, IXT achieves up to 2.8x compute efficiency and reaches performance levels on math and code that are unattainable without feedback conditioning.

## Delta from parents
Where DeepSeek-R1 applies RL rewards only during the post-training phase, IXT argues that quality-aware signals should shape representations from pretraining onward, using reward-conditioned data annotation rather than reward-based gradient updates. Where CoT prompting conditions the model at inference time with reasoning traces, IXT conditions the model at training time with quality feedback, making the quality awareness persistent in the weights rather than prompt-dependent. The scaling analysis extends the compute-optimal tradition by showing that feedback conditioning bends the scaling curve rather than merely shifting it.

## Why Leo should care
If you pretrain a domain-specific model for time series or biosignals, the core idea — annotate pretraining data with domain quality scores and condition training on them — is directly transferable. A biosignal reward model that scores signal quality, artifact contamination, or clinical relevance could make a 2-3x difference in pretraining efficiency.
