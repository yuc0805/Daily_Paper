# Efficient Pre-Training with Token Superposition

**Arxiv:** 2605.06546 | **Date:** 2026-05-07 | **Area:** llm

## What it does
Token Superposition Training (TST) is a drop-in pretraining method that combines contiguous tokens into bag representations trained with multi-hot cross-entropy, followed by standard recovery training. It achieves 2-3x wall-clock speedup at matched FLOPs without changing the model, optimizer, tokenizer, or data. Validated at 270M to 10B-A1B MoE scales, beating baselines on loss and downstream benchmarks (HellaSwag, ARC, MMLU).

## Direct parents (in Leo's library)
No direct parents in Leo's library. Closest neighbors are DeepSeek-V3 (deepseek2024_v3, Community 0) for training efficiency at scale, and the Transformer architecture (vaswani2017_transformer, Community 2) as the base architecture.

## Delta from parents
Where DeepSeek-V3 improved training efficiency through MoE architecture design and expert load balancing, TST is orthogonal — it compresses the training data stream itself by folding adjacent tokens into bags. This means TST can be combined with MoE and other architectural efficiency methods for compounding gains. The approach is conceptually simple (bag-of-tokens plus multi-hot loss) but the engineering contribution is showing it works at scale without degrading downstream performance.

## Why Leo should care
Transfer hypothesis for TS foundation models: contiguous time patches have high local redundancy and could be superposed during pretraining to achieve similar speedups for models like Sundial or Olivia. This has not been tried.
