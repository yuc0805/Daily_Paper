# Nexus: An Agentic Framework for Time Series Forecasting

**Arxiv:** 2605.14389 | **Date:** 2026-05-14 | **Area:** time-series

## What it does
Nexus decomposes time series forecasting into a multi-agent pipeline with specialized stages for macro-level trends, micro-level fluctuations, and contextual information integration. It shows that LLMs have stronger intrinsic forecasting ability than previously recognized when numerical and contextual reasoning are properly organized. On post-cutoff data (Zillow, stocks), Nexus matches or outperforms TSFMs and LLM baselines.

## Delta from parents
Where TS-Agent uses the LLM to orchestrate external tools, Nexus uses the LLM as the forecasting engine, decomposed into specialized reasoning stages. Where ChatTS requires fine-tuning, Nexus is zero-shot via prompt design. The multi-agent architecture extends the cross-area bridge between Community 4 (TS + LLM) and Community 0 (Agents).
