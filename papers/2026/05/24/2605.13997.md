## Summary

Identifies that pairwise expert merging scores are blind to irreducible cycles among triples of experts. Formalizes via Hodge decomposition of simplicial Laplacian on a 2-complex of experts. HodgeCover greedily covers harmonic-critical structures. Better accuracy retention than pairwise merging at same compression ratio.

## Key Contribution

First formal analysis of why greedy pairwise expert merging fails in MoE, with a principled fix from algebraic topology.
