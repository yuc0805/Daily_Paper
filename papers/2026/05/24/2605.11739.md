## Summary

Explains why on-policy distillation (OPD) is more efficient than off-policy for LLM post-training. OPD establishes stable update trajectory early (foresight) at two levels: module-allocation (concentrates on critical modules) and update-direction (stronger low-rank concentration with early alignment to final subspace). Proposes cheap prediction of OPD-aligned update directions.

## Key Contribution

First mechanistic explanation of on-policy distillation efficiency at the parameter level, showing it operates via early subspace alignment.
