## Summary

3B-active-parameter native unified model for image/video understanding, generation, and editing. Dual-stream MoE on interleaved multimodal sequences. Modality-aware RoPE to mitigate heterogeneous token interference. Trained from scratch on 128 A100s. Outperforms larger specialized models.

## Key Contribution

Shows collaborative multi-task training with proper decoupling (dual-stream MoE + modality-aware positional encoding) achieves strong unified multimodal capability without massive scale.

## Lineage
