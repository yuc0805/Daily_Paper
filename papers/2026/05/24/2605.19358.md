## Summary

CES uses token-level entropy as an uncertainty signal during RL training (built on DAPO). At high-entropy "forking point" tokens, it penalizes entropy on correct paths (compress reasoning) and rewards entropy on incorrect paths (encourage exploration). Evaluated on DeepSeek-R1-Distill-7B across 12 math benchmarks: improves accuracy while reducing response length vs DAPO. Generalizes to 1.5B model and OOD benchmarks.

## Key Contribution

First method to adaptively shape reasoning depth per-problem during RL training via token-level entropy signals, without requiring explicit problem difficulty labels.
