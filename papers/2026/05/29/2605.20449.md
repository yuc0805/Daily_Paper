# LLM Pretraining Shapes a Generalizable Manifold: Insights into Cross-Modal Transfer to Time Series

**Arxiv:** 2605.20449 | **Date:** 2026-05-29 | **Area:** time-series

## What it does
The paper explains why a frozen language model transfers to time-series forecasting. Using linear probes on frozen hidden states, nearest-neighbor retrieval, and a loss-surface analysis, it shows that time-series structure is present in the representation before fine-tuning and that language pretraining preconditions optimization. The conclusion is that pretraining builds a reusable manifold and fine-tuning projects numerical dynamics onto its task-relevant directions.

## Delta from parents
Where GPT4TS and FPT demonstrated that frozen language-model weights help time series, and Time-LLM showed reprogramming the series into the language space works, this paper does not add a transfer recipe; it gives the mechanism. It argues the language manifold already encodes sequence structure (probes decode realistic trajectories with no paired supervision), so fine-tuning only rotates numerical dynamics onto directions that already exist, because the pretrained initialization yields coherent gradients and an anisotropic loss surface that random initialization does not. This moves the MAE-Theory style geometric argument from vision pretraining to language-to-time-series transfer.
