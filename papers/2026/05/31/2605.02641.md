# Mamoda2.5: Enhancing a Unified Multimodal Model with DiT-MoE

**Arxiv:** 2605.02641 | **Date:** 2026-05-31 | **Area:** generative-cv

## What it does
Mamoda2.5 is a single AR-Diffusion architecture for both multimodal understanding and generation. Its generation backbone is a Diffusion Transformer equipped with a fine-grained Mixture-of-Experts layer (128 experts, top-8 routing), producing a 25B-parameter model that activates only 3B parameters per step. It reaches top-tier scores on VBench 2.0 and a video-editing record on OpenVE-Bench, and a joint few-step distillation plus reinforcement-learning stage compresses a 30-step editing model to 4 steps, reported as up to 95.9 times faster inference.

## Direct parents (in Leo's library)
- [YJ9TK993] DiT (Peebles 2023) — replaced the U-Net in a diffusion model with a transformer and showed clean scaling (graphify Community 3).
- [GX7WR7KA] DDPM (Ho 2020) — the underlying iterative denoising process (graphify Community 3).

## Delta from parents
Where DiT used a single dense transformer as the diffusion backbone, this paper makes the transformer sparse with an expert-routing layer, so the DiT scaling argument is pushed further by activating only a fraction of parameters per token, cutting compute while raising nominal capacity. The denoising mechanism itself still traces back to DDPM. The added distillation-plus-RL stage is an inference-acceleration step that the parents did not address.

## Why Leo should care
The sparse-expert design is an unported opportunity for bio-sensing. No wearable-sensing foundation model in Leo's library uses per-modality expert routing. Transfer hypothesis: route each sensor channel (accelerometer, PPG, ECG, temperature) to specialized experts so a large multivariate model keeps per-step compute low on long continuous physiological streams.
