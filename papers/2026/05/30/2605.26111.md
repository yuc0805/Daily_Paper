# Squeezing Capacity from Multimodal Large Language Models for Subject-driven Generation

**Arxiv:** 2605.26111 | **Date:** 2026-05-30 | **Area:** generative-cv

## What it does
The paper does subject-driven image generation: placing a specific subject from a few reference images into new generated scenes. It conditions a diffusion generator on a multimodal language model that jointly encodes the text prompt and reference images, adds VAE-based identity conditioning, and introduces a Dual Layer Aggregation module that combines features from several language-model layers rather than only the last one. The result is better identity preservation and prompt-following on subject-driven benchmarks.

## Delta from parents
Where DALL-E 2 conditioned on one pooled embedding and DiT changed the backbone, this paper changes the conditioning interface: it aggregates features across language-model layers, because one pooled vector throws away layer-specific detail needed to preserve a subject's identity. It stays in the Vision-Language and Generative community but moves conditioning from a single vector to a multi-level stack.

## Why Leo should care
Secondary watch area. The transferable idea is multi-level feature aggregation from a frozen multimodal model used as a conditioner, rather than last-layer pooling. For multimodal health work, the same principle could improve conditioning a generator or predictor on a biosignal encoder by reading several encoder depths instead of the final embedding.
