# Assessing the Operational Viability of Foundation Models for Time Series Forecasting

**Arxiv:** 2605.24381 | **Date:** 2026-05-30 | **Area:** time-series

## What it does
The paper (Google) asks whether large time-series foundation models earn their inference cost in production. It compares zero-shot foundation models against industry-standard supervised forecasters on real workloads and finds the answer depends on series complexity. It proposes a Complexity Router that reads simple empirical features of each series and sends it to the cheapest adequate model class, reporting higher accuracy and much lower inference cost than deploying one universal foundation model.
