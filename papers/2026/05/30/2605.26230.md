# GARD: Geometry-Aware Representation Denoising for Robust Multi-view 3D Reconstruction

**Arxiv:** 2605.26230 | **Date:** 2026-05-30 | **Area:** generative-cv

## What it does
Feed-forward 3D reconstruction models are trained on clean images but used on degraded real photos, which breaks them. GARD runs a diffusion-based restoration step that denoises directly in the geometry-aware feature space of the feed-forward reconstruction model rather than in raw image space. Because that feature space already carries geometry, denoising there recovers accurate scene structure, improving reconstruction under degraded inputs without retraining the reconstructor.

## Delta from parents
Where DDPM denoises in pixel or latent image space, GARD does the denoising inside a task model's geometry-aware feature space, because the objective is correct 3D structure, not a clean image. It is a relocation of the diffusion noise model to wherever the downstream task keeps its information, rather than a new diffusion formulation.

## Why Leo should care
Secondary watch area, but the core move is portable: run diffusion denoising in a downstream model's feature space instead of raw signal space. For biosignals, this is a concrete transfer hypothesis — denoise motion-corrupted PPG or ECG inside the embedding space of a biosignal foundation model, so artifact removal is judged by downstream health-task geometry rather than waveform smoothness. Not yet ported to time-series or bio-sensing.
