## Summary

Uses autoregressive normalizing flows to unify text and image generation under a single causal-mask architecture. The Pretzel architecture interleaves a pretrained VLM stream with a TarFlow stream. Both text and image tokens share the KV-cache, avoiding the encode-then-denoise bottleneck of diffusion-based unified models. From Apple and UIUC.

## Lineage

Extends SigLIP (Zhai 2023, Community 3) for visual encoding and DDPM (Ho 2020, Community 3) / DiT (Peebles 2023, Community 3) for generation. Replaces diffusion with flows for architectural unification.
