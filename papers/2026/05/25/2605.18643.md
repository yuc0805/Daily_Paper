## Summary

ZEDA converts a post-trained static MoE into a dynamic one via self-distillation. A learnable router decides per-token how many experts to activate. Achieves roughly 50% reduction in activated parameters on easy inputs with negligible accuracy loss, applicable to existing trained MoE models without retraining from scratch.
