## Summary

Identifies reasoning-level semantic redundancy as a signal for early exit in reasoning LLMs. When successive reasoning steps stop introducing new information, the trajectory has converged and generation can stop. Detects convergence by measuring semantic similarity between consecutive reasoning windows. Saves tokens at inference time without premature exit.
