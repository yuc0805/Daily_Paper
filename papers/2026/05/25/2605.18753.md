## Summary

DashAttention replaces the non-differentiable top-k block selection in hierarchical sparse attention (NSA, InfLLMv2) with alpha-entmax, a differentiable sparse mapping that selects a variable number of KV blocks per query. This makes the full two-stage attention hierarchy end-to-end trainable. The method achieves full-attention accuracy at 75% sparsity and dominates the Pareto frontier versus NSA and InfLLMv2 at high sparsity. Includes a Triton implementation with speedup over FlashAttention-3.

## Lineage

Direct descendant of Attention Is All You Need (Vaswani 2017). No prior work in Leo's library on hierarchical sparse attention, so this opens a new branch from the Transformer anchor node. Relevant to time-series foundation models that inherit the dense-attention bottleneck.
