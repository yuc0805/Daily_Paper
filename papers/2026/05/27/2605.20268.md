# Chronicle: A Multimodal Foundation Model for Joint Language and Time Series Understanding

**Arxiv:** 2605.20268 | **Date:** 2026-05-18 | **Area:** time-series

## What it does
A 324M-parameter decoder-only transformer trained from scratch on both natural language and time series in a single unified architecture. Both modalities share the same transformer blocks, attention mechanism, and residual stream. Pretraining uses mostly unimodal batches with a short cross-modal alignment stage. It matches Gemma-3-270M-PT on 19 NLU tasks, sets a new bar for frozen-embedding TS classification on 24 UCR/UEA datasets, and beats all supervised fusion baselines on Time-MMD.

## Direct parents (in Leo's library)
- [xie2025_chatts] ChatTS (Xie 2025) — aligned time series with pretrained LLMs via post-hoc adaptation
- [liu2025_tsagent] TS-Agent (Liu 2025) — used LLM reasoning for time series analysis via tool-use agents

## Delta from parents
Where ChatTS and TS-Agent adapted pretrained language models to handle time series as a secondary modality, Chronicle argues that post-hoc adaptation inherits text-biased representations. The key shift is joint pretraining from scratch, which allows the shared transformer to learn temporal representations that are not constrained by a text-only initialization. The result is competitive performance in both language and time series from one backbone.

## Why Leo should care
If joint pretraining from scratch proves scalable, it could replace the adapter-based pipelines (ChatTS, TS-Agent) that currently dominate the TS+LLM space. The frozen-embedding TS classification result is strong and directly comparable to Leo's work. The compact 324M size also makes this practically reproducible.
