# Towards a General Intelligence and Interface for Wearable Health Data

**Arxiv:** 2605.22759 | **Date:** 2026-05-21 | **Area:** time-series, llm-health

## What it does
A foundation model pretrained on more than one trillion minutes of unlabeled wearable sensor signals from five million participants. It is evaluated on 35 health prediction tasks (cardiovascular, metabolic, sleep, mental health, lifestyle, demographics). A "classroom" of LLM agents autonomously searches the space of downstream predictive heads built on frozen embeddings. A Personal Health Agent integrates these predictors and is validated by 1,860 clinician ratings.

## Delta from parents
Where Gu 2025 surveyed existing small-to-medium biosignal models and flagged scaling as an open direction, this paper fills that gap with 5M-participant pretraining. Where GLOBEM focused on cross-dataset generalization with traditional ML pipelines, this paper shows that a single pretrained representation transfers across 35 tasks via frozen embeddings. Where Sensor2Text connected sensors to language through distillation, this paper uses LLM agents as an AutoML layer on top of sensor representations — a structurally different connection between language models and sensor data.

## Why Leo should care
This is the largest wearable health foundation model reported. The few-shot transfer results are directly relevant to bio-sensing work with limited labels. The LLM-agent AutoML pattern for downstream heads is a novel design that could be applied to Leo's own sensor representation work. The paper imports the LLM agent paradigm (Community 0) into wearable health (Community 5), creating a new cross-area bridge.
