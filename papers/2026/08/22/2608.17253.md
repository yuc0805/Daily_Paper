# Co-RL: Unsupervised Reasoning Emerges from Diverse Cohort in Multi-agent RL

**Arxiv:** 2608.17253 | **Date:** 2026-08-22 | **Area:** reasoning

## What it does
Co-RL trains several models that share no parameters at the same time, with each model's reward derived from its peers rather than from ground-truth labels. The paper's argument is that pure self-rewarding reinforcement learning collapses because a single model's errors are correlated with its own judgements, so raising cohort diversity across model families, model sizes and rephrased training samples decorrelates those errors and breaks the feedback loop. Reported gains are 3.0 to 8.6% averaged over seven text-only benchmarks for language models and 2.3 to 7.2% over four multimodal benchmarks for vision-language models, without any ground-truth labels, matching or beating supervised methods in several settings.

## Delta from parents
Where DeepSeek-R1 required a verifiable reward and therefore a domain where correctness can be checked automatically, Co-RL removes the verifier and substitutes peer agreement across a deliberately heterogeneous cohort, because the authors expect verifiable rewards to run out as tasks pass the point where humans can score them. Where chain-of-thought treated the reasoning trace as something to elicit from a fixed model, Co-RL treats the *spread* of reasoning traces across different models as the training signal itself. The mechanism claim is specific and testable: training collapse in self-rewarding setups is caused by correlated errors, and cohort diversity is the lever that reduces the correlation.

## Why Leo should care
This paper extends the DeepSeek-R1 to Chain-of-Thought bridge already recorded in the graph, which crosses reasoning-via-RL and reasoning-via-prompting inside Community 0. Co-RL pushes that bridge toward label-free training, which matters wherever labels are the bottleneck. In bio-sensing they usually are: clinical annotation is expensive and often unavailable at scale, so a peer-agreement reward over a heterogeneous cohort of physiological encoders is a concrete thing to try.
