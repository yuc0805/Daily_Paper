# Massive Activations in Hybrid Linear Attention Large Language Models: Pre-Attention Spikes and Inter-Spike Plateaus

**Arxiv:** 2608.12149 | **Date:** 2026-08-14 | **Area:** mamba

## What it does
Hybrid linear-attention models interleave cheap linear-attention layers with a smaller number of full-attention layers. This paper is a measurement study of where the very large activation outliers ("massive activations") sit in such stacks. Two shapes recur: activations spike immediately before every full-attention layer (pre-attention spikes), and they can survive across the intervening linear-attention layers as flat plateaus (inter-spike plateaus). As full attention gets denser the spikes join up through the plateaus and the pattern converges to the stable outlier morphology of an ordinary full-attention model. The finding holds across five linear-attention architectures, six hybridization ratios, five data domains, and open models from 1.2B to 397B total parameters, with controlled pretraining of gated-delta-net hybrids up to 1.3B.

## Delta from parents
Where HARMamba and Bi-Mamba+ argued that state space layers can *replace* attention at lower cost, this paper studies what happens when you keep both, and reports that the mixture is not neutral: the outlier structure of the network reorganizes itself around wherever the full-attention layers sit. Where the parents evaluated by downstream accuracy, this paper evaluates by internal activation statistics and offers a mechanism, namely that spike shape is governed by the timing of activation cancellation, with pre-attention spikes following a localized write-sink-cancel process and plateaus reflecting delayed cancellation. The delta that matters is practical: outlier placement is what breaks low-bit quantization, so a hybrid's quantization behavior is predictable from its hybridization ratio rather than being a per-model surprise.

## Why Leo should care
Leo's Mamba cluster is built on the argument that state space layers handle long physiological sequences more cheaply than attention. Hybrids are the natural next step for multi-day sensor streams, and this paper says the hybridization ratio is a design knob with a measurable and predictable side effect on numerical range. The imported method here is outlier analysis from LLM quantization work, applied to hybrid stacks; it has not been applied to time-series or bio-sensing hybrids at all, so that transfer is open. Note the limitation: this is descriptive, not a fix, and all evidence is on language pretraining.
