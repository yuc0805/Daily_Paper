# ReasonCast: Towards Explainable Time Series Forecasting with Reasoning

**Arxiv:** 2608.01875 | **Date:** 2026-08-19 | **Area:** time-series

## What it does
ReasonCast is a fine-tuning recipe that makes any LLM backbone emit a reasoning chain and a numeric forecast together in one autoregressive pass, rather than routing understanding and generation down separate task-specific paths. The paper also releases ReasonTS-Bench, which decomposes time series into five fundamental patterns and scores forecast error (MAE) alongside three reasoning metrics averaged over those primitives. The reported result is that backbones tuned this way exceed specialised time-series forecasters on accuracy while producing reasoning that can be checked against the series. Code is at https://github.com/seunghan96/reasoncast.

## Delta from parents
Where ChatTS aligned the series so an LLM could describe it, and TS-Agent made the reasoning verifiable by pushing every numeric claim out into a tool call recorded in an evidence log, ReasonCast puts both capabilities back inside a single forward pass and turns the reasoning chain into a supervised training target, because a chain that is trained can be scored on a fixed benchmark whereas a chain that is prompted or tool-mediated cannot be compared across backbones. Relative to Chain-of-Thought, the shift is from reasoning elicited at inference to reasoning learned during training, and from text tasks to numeric forecasting. This extends the graphify cross-area bridge `deepseek2025_r1 -> wei2023_cot`, which already crossed Community 0 and the reasoning area by moving from prompted to trained reasoning; ReasonCast pushes that bridge into Community 4 by applying the same move to forecasting.

## Why Leo should care
This closes an opening. The obvious next paper in the time-series-plus-LLM line was "train the forecaster to explain its own forecast", and it now exists with an accompanying benchmark. The imported method is supervised reasoning-chain fine-tuning from language modelling, and it should be treated as ported and low-yield for a follow-up. What remains open is the evaluation side: ReasonTS-Bench covers five synthetic pattern primitives, not physiological signals, so the equivalent benchmark for bio-signal reasoning is still unbuilt.
