# WIRED: Weighted Adaptive Prediction with Structured Dependence for Probabilistic Multiseries Forecasting

**Arxiv:** 2608.12998 | **Date:** 2026-08-19 | **Area:** time-series

## What it does
WIRED, by Giancarlo Vercellino, forecasts many related series jointly while keeping marginal distributions and cross-series dependence structurally separate. Marginals come from a library of simple predictive distributions combined with CRPS-based adaptive mixture weights; dependence is reconstructed afterwards by a Gaussian or Student t copula used for cross-series simulation. Evaluation covers four synthetic data-generating processes at three horizons with 30 replicates per pair, nine ablations with external baselines, and a rolling-origin study on EuStockMarkets. The authors describe the contribution as architectural and diagnostic rather than a new state of the art, and ship it as an R package.

## Delta from parents
Where Forecast Collapse diagnosed the problem and Cross Variable Loss patched it inside the training objective of a neural backbone, WIRED removes the coupling altogether by fitting marginals and dependence in separate stages, because if dependence is carried by an explicit copula then no property of the marginal objective can destroy it. The cost of that separation is a parametric dependence family: a Gaussian or Student t copula imposes a shape on the dependence, whereas the graph penalty learns whatever structure the residuals actually show. Read together, the three papers give the diagnosis, the in-loss repair and the architectural repair for one problem.

## Why Leo should care
Multivariate physiological recording is exactly the setting where marginal calibration and cross-channel dependence get conflated, and where flattened forecasts are easy to mistake for well-calibrated ones. The separation WIRED proposes is method-agnostic: the marginal stage could be any modern forecaster, with the copula stage bolted on afterwards. There is no method import from CV or NLP here — this is classical statistics applied to a deep-learning failure mode — so the transfer direction is unusual and the idea is not closed off.
