# FOUND-AF: Benchmarking ECG Foundation Models for Atrial Fibrillation Detection

**Arxiv:** 2608.03597 | **Date:** 2026-08-19 | **Area:** llm-health

## What it does
FOUND-AF evaluates nine public ECG foundation models from five families, including HuBERT-ECG, CLEF, ST-MEM, ECG-JEPA and ECGFounder, under one leakage-controlled protocol across four heterogeneous datasets (AFDB, CinC2017, CPSC2021, LTAFDB). Every encoder is frozen and fed to a fixed XGBoost classifier with standardised preprocessing, model-native resampling and recording-level grouped cross-validation, so no recording spans a split boundary. Reporting includes paired recording-level bootstrap comparisons with Holm correction, embedding-space visualisation and profiling of model size, inference time and memory. ECGFounder is strongest overall and sits at a good point on the accuracy-versus-cost curve.

## Delta from parents
Where the biosignals survey collected each model's claims, FOUND-AF tests them under one protocol and produces a ranking, because a survey inherits every paper's own evaluation setup and therefore cannot say which encoder is better. Where HeAR and SSL-for-HAR each validated a single encoder against baselines its own authors selected, FOUND-AF fixes the downstream classifier and the split policy across all nine encoders, so the comparison isolates representation quality instead of measuring how much fine-tuning effort each group invested. The added ingredient specific to physiological data is recording-level grouping, which removes the within-subject leakage that inflates most published ECG numbers.

## Why Leo should care
The imported method is the frozen-encoder plus fixed-downstream-classifier evaluation protocol, standard practice in vision and language representation research, now applied to ECG with a leakage control added for the physiological case. Treat it as ported: a "we benchmark bio-signal foundation models properly" paper for ECG is now taken, and the remaining room is in modalities FOUND-AF does not cover. `gu2025_biosignals` already sits on both the time-series and llm-health area pages, functioning as a bridge between Community 1 (Health AI and Self-Supervised) and Community 5 (Wearable Sensing and Behavior). FOUND-AF strengthens that bridge on the evaluation side rather than the modelling side, giving the two communities the shared protocol they lacked.
