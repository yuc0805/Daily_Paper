# MorphologyFM: A Foundation Model for Morphology-Aware Representation Learning from ECG and Pulse Oximetry Waveforms

**Arxiv:** 2607.09749 | **Date:** 2026-08-02 | **Area:** llm-health

## What it does
A multimodal foundation model pretrained on paired ECG and SpO2 (pulse oximetry) waveforms from the MIMIC critical-care database. Combines morphology-guided masking, cross-modal representation learning between the two waveform types, and contrastive latent alignment, all self-supervised. Aims to produce representations that preserve clinically meaningful waveform shape for transfer to cardiovascular and hemodynamic tasks.

## Delta from parents
Where standard masked autoencoders mask random patches and reward reconstruction of pixels or raw values, MorphologyFM masks with waveform morphology in mind and adds a contrastive objective, because for physiological waveforms the diagnostic content is in shape (intervals, slopes, beat-to-beat structure), not average level, so plain reconstruction can score well while discarding the clinically useful part. Where the Gu 2025 survey describes mostly single-signal biosignal pretraining, MorphologyFM extends the program to paired cross-modal (ECG plus SpO2) pretraining with explicit cross-modal alignment.

## Why Leo should care
Bio-signal foundation model in the primary area, with a design choice (morphology-preserving objective) worth borrowing for any waveform pipeline where shape carries the label. The fresh angle is the morphology-guided masking, which is the part still open to variation.
