# LLM Agents for Time-Series: A Survey

**Arxiv:** 2608.26226 | **Date:** 2026-08-30 | **Area:** time-series

## What it does
Organizes LLM-based time-series agents by the problem they address rather than by their components, splitting the literature into forecasting and reasoning, augmentation and synthesis, anomaly detection and diagnosis, and decision support. Within each category it asks how the task requirement shapes agent architecture, tool use and memory design. It collects the datasets and environments in use and compares reported numbers where settings are shared or close enough to permit comparison. Accepted to Findings of EMNLP 2026.

## Delta from parents
Where Zhang's 2024 survey organized by attachment method — prompting, alignment, fine-tuning — this one organizes by task and asks what the task demands of the agent loop, because two years of systems have made the attachment method the less informative variable. Where TS-Agent and ChatTS each presented a single architecture with its own evaluation, this survey places them side by side under shared or closely related settings, a comparison neither could make for itself. The new organizing axes — architecture, tool use, memory design — are imported from the agent literature rather than the forecasting literature, which is the actual move here.

## Why Leo should care
This is a Community 4 to Community 0 crossing: memory design and tool use are agent-community concepts, carried in Leo's library by ToolkenGPT and the Memory Mechanisms survey, now used as organizing axes for time-series systems. The memory-design sections are the part worth reading, since that is the axis the library is thinnest on for time series, and it is the same gap Tier D flags as an unported opportunity.
