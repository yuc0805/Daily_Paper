# TTPO: Test-Time Policy Optimization

**Arxiv:** 2608.27448 | **Date:** 2026-08-29 | **Area:** test-time-training

## What it does
TTPO removes the ground-truth label from reasoning post-training so that the update can run at test time on the problems the model is actually facing. It samples rollouts, forms a majority-vote pseudo-label, and then treats the two sides of the vote differently: rollouts that agree with the vote are distilled through on-policy self-distillation, and rollouts that disagree are penalised with grouped reinforcement learning. Token-level selection down-weights positions the distillation branch has already converged on and restricts the penalty to confidently wrong tokens. Without labels, TTPO matches label-supervised on-policy self-distillation on five competition benchmarks and lifts Qwen3-1.7B from 38.0 to 45.2 percent under test-time training.

## Delta from parents
Where Akyurek's TTT built a supervision signal by augmenting the target task, TTPO builds it from the model's own rollout distribution, so nothing task-specific has to be constructed. Where DeepSeek-R1 needed a verifiable reward to define the advantage, TTPO substitutes a majority vote and then repairs the substitution's main weakness: an incorrect vote corrupts the teacher for every token. The observation that carries the paper is an asymmetry — a rollout that disagrees with the vote is usually wrong whether or not the vote itself is right — which means the negative branch stays valid even when the positive branch is being taught the wrong answer. Sun's TTT layers made the update mechanism internal; TTPO makes the update objective self-supplied. The cross-area bridge in the library between `deepseek2025_r1` and `wei2023_cot` marked the shift from prompting for reasoning to training for it; this paper pushes that bridge further, into training for reasoning without any label at all.

## Why Leo should care
The failure this paper works around is exactly the failure that limits bio-sensing: labels are scarce, expensive and often arrive long after the signal. TTPO is a recipe for extracting a training signal from agreement across stochastic samples of the same input, which is a structure that transfers directly to a wearable model asked to adapt to a new subject or a new device with no annotation. The method is currently tied to discrete answers that can be voted on, which is what would need replacing for a continuous physiological target. Note that this is a case of an NLP method not yet ported, rather than one closed off.
