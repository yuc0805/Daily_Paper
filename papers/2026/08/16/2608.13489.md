# DreamX-Phi 1.0: Action-Conditioned Video World Model for Robotic Manipulation

**Arxiv:** 2608.13489 | **Date:** 2026-08-16 | **Area:** world-model-rl

## What it does
Given one observed frame, a language instruction, and a prescribed action sequence of end-effector poses and gripper states, DreamX-Phi predicts the resulting future video. The paper's argument is that visual realism and action faithfulness are separate properties: a rollout can look correct and still move the wrong arm or drop the manipulated object. Three components address faithfulness. Per-arm SE(3) transformations are injected into attention through PRoPE-style geometric encoding so arm identity and rigid-motion structure survive. A lightweight depth branch constrains scene geometry. SAM3 object masks with a frozen V-JEPA teacher hold small manipulated objects consistent through the grasp. The multi-step generator is then distilled into a few-step student by distribution-matching distillation. The model took first place on Track 1 and second on Track 2 of the WorldArena 2.0 Challenge.

- [938DAXZT] World Models (Ha and Schmidhuber 2018) — the framing that a learned forward model of observations can substitute for the environment.

## Delta from parents
Where Navigation World Models conditioned prediction on actions and left the model to infer how the action changes the scene, DreamX-Phi hands the model the transformation explicitly, encoding each arm's SE(3) pose into attention so that arm identity cannot be confused. Where DINO-WM used a frozen visual encoder as the planning latent space, this paper uses a frozen V-JEPA teacher as a consistency constraint on specific masked objects rather than as the representation itself, which is a narrower and more targeted use of the same asset. Relative to DiT, the change is not the backbone but what enters attention: geometric structure rather than only position and time. The reason for the design is that manipulation failures in prior video world models are structured, not random, so the fix is to remove the specific degrees of freedom that produce them rather than to increase capacity.

## Why Leo should care
The transferable idea is injecting known geometry into attention rather than expecting the model to learn it. That pattern is not vision-specific. The reported result is a competition placement rather than a controlled ablation table in the abstract, so the attribution across the three components remains unverified from the abstract alone.
