# OpenMHC: Accelerating the Science of Wearable Foundation Models

**Arxiv:** 2607.16235 | **Date:** 2026-08-03 | **Area:** time-series

## What it does
OpenMHC releases the largest open-access wearable health dataset to date, built from more than a decade of the My Heart Counts study app: over 60 million hours of data across 19 sensor channels and up to 169 linked health, lifestyle, mood, and behavior variables from 11,894 participants. It ships open-source implementations of recent wearable foundation models and a single benchmark with three tracks: downstream health and behavior prediction, multivariate imputation, and time-series forecasting.

## Delta from parents
Where GLOBEM gave a multi-year behavior dataset with a cross-dataset benchmark, and where Yuan 2024 showed self-supervised pretraining works at very large scale on one sensor stream, OpenMHC releases a broader open dataset plus open model weights and a three-track benchmark in one package. The change is scale and openness together: data, reference model code, and a shared benchmark are released in the same place, because the blocking problem is now reproducibility and fair comparison rather than whether pretraining works. It extends the Sensor2Text-to-few-shot-health bridge that already crossed Wearable Sensing (community 5) and Health AI (community 1) in the library, pushing that bridge toward standardized evaluation by linking raw sensor channels with health and mood outcomes inside one benchmark rather than one model.

## Why Leo should care
This is directly in Leo's working area. It provides a public dataset, reference models, and a benchmark he can run his own methods against, and its imputation and forecasting tracks match the fragmented-wearable-gap problem seen in the 2026-08-02 zero-shot HRV forecasting paper. The imported method it benchmarks is masked-reconstruction and multivariate-time-series-foundation-model pretraining applied to wearables; that path is now well ported and closed off as low-hanging fruit, so the open value here is the shared testbed, not a new model.
