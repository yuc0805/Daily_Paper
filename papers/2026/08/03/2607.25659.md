# CoRT: Counterfactual Replay for Token-Level Rubric-Guided Policy Optimization

**Arxiv:** 2607.25659 | **Date:** 2026-08-03 | **Area:** reasoning

## What it does
CoRT is a token-level credit weighting method for rubric-conditioned GRPO. It rescores the same sampled response twice, once under the rubric-conditioned prompt and once under a matched criteria-free prompt, and uses the token-by-token log-likelihood difference as a proxy for how much each token depends on the rubric. It maps these differences to bounded, response-normalized weights and redistributes the GRPO advantage across tokens, with no auxiliary scoring model and no change to the response-level reward. It improves over matched response-level GRPO by 4.4 percentage points on average.

## Delta from parents
Where DeepSeek-R1 assigns one advantage to the whole response, CoRT splits that advantage across tokens using the model's own likelihood contrasts, because uniform credit wastes the fine-grained signal a rubric already contains. Where Chain-of-Thought exposes reasoning steps in text, CoRT does not change the text; it changes which tokens the update rewards, keeping the simplicity and stability of GRPO while adding within-response credit.
