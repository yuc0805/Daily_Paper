# PhysBrain 1.5: From Vision-Language Models to Physical Foundation Models

**Arxiv:** 2609.14973 | **Date:** 2026-09-15 | **Area:** world-model-rl

## What it does
Starts from a general vision-language model and encodes language responses, end-effector motion, and dense visual targets as discrete token sequences, then jointly optimizes all three with autoregressive next-token prediction. Embodied supervision in pre-training comes entirely from human interaction videos, organized into task-centered episodes that pair semantic and spatial context with recovered motion and the subsequent observation; supervised fine-tuning then mixes human demonstrations, robot trajectories, and simulated experience. The 8B model averages 72.5 across 28 embodied understanding benchmarks, best among open models on 14 of them, and reported on par with GPT-6-Astra and Gemini 3.6 Flash. Weights for 8B and 2B were released the same day.

## Direct parents (in Leo's library)
- [938DAXZT] World Models (Ha 2018) — established the observe, act, predict loop with an internal state that supports rollout.
- [W44RSJJI] DINO-WM (Zhou 2025) — established building a world model on top of frozen pretrained visual features rather than training the encoder from scratch.
- [SC8KWYVK] Flamingo (Alayrac 2022) — established starting from a pretrained language model and attaching visual conditioning rather than training a multimodal model from zero.

## Delta from parents
Where Ha's world model learns its latent from interaction data collected in a simulator, this paper takes the supervision from human interaction video, because robot trajectories are the scarce resource and video of people manipulating objects is not. Where DINO-WM keeps understanding and prediction in separate modules with a frozen feature extractor underneath, here understanding, action generation, and future-state prediction are three token streams under one autoregressive objective, so the same parameters answer a question, emit an end-effector trajectory, and render the next RGB, depth, and robot-mask frame. The Flamingo lineage shows in the starting point rather than the architecture: the model inherits general multimodal ability from a vision-language model and is reported to retain it after embodied training, which is the usual failure mode of embodied fine-tuning.

## Why Leo should care
The recoverable-supervision argument is the transferable part. Motion is recovered from passive video rather than recorded from instrumented hardware, which is the same structural problem as bio-sensing, where labelled physiological episodes are scarce and unlabelled passive recordings are not. The numbers to treat with care are the 28-benchmark average and the parity claim against proprietary models, since benchmark averages across heterogeneous suites are easy to move by suite composition, and the future-state prediction results are reported qualitatively rather than with a held-out prediction error.
