# Omni-Streaming Thinking

**Arxiv:** 2609.15128 | **Date:** 2026-09-15 | **Area:** multi-modal

## What it does
Identifies premature cross-modal commitment: in streaming audio-visual models, visual cues often support an interpretation before the corresponding utterance or sound event finishes, and once that interpretation is written into memory as a fact, later reasoning keeps relaying it even after audio contradicts it. The method generates structured outputs holding observed evidence, forecasts of future evidence, and claims, with each claim marked pending and bound to a verification interval. Audio and visual evidence are stored separately and each claim is checked against the specified modality at the end of its interval; a refutation step downweights refuted claims and their dependents, and an answer gate decides when to respond. On a frozen Qwen3-Omni-30B-A3B-Instruct backbone with lightweight adaptation, it beats the strongest open baselines on five streaming and audio-visual benchmarks by more than 10 percent relative on average, and reaches d-prime 2.95 on the new OST-DiagBench against at most 1.38 for open baselines.

## Direct parents (in Leo's library)
- [2609.05871] Where Does the Sound Go? (Jo 2026) — established by layer-wise probing that acoustic information survives to the last hidden layer, so audio underuse is a readout problem rather than an encoding problem.
- [ESEEEH85] LTU (Gong 2023) — established audio-conditioned language modelling, answering questions about a complete audio clip.
- [SC8KWYVK] Flamingo (Alayrac 2022) — established interleaved multimodal conditioning where visual context is written into the language stream as it arrives.

## Delta from parents
Where Jo showed that the acoustic evidence is present but not used, this paper gives a mechanism that forces it to be used, by refusing to let a visually motivated claim become a fact until the audio window that would confirm or refute it has actually passed. Where LTU conditions on a finished clip and Flamingo writes each incoming observation straight into the context, the change here is that writing is deferred and reversible, because in a stream the order in which evidence arrives is an artifact of timing rather than of importance. The separate audio and visual evidence stores are what make refutation possible at all: a single fused memory cannot say which modality supported a claim. The diagnostic benchmark is the other contribution, holding video fixed and editing audio to construct agreement, absence, contradiction, coexistence, and subtitle-speech conflict cases, which turns a qualitative complaint about vision dominance into a d-prime.

## Why Leo should care
This paper extends the Sept 13 audio readout paper, which already bridged the audio and multi-modal areas in the library; the new work pushes that bridge from diagnosis into a correction mechanism. The pending-claim and verification-interval construction is modality-agnostic and is not yet present anywhere in Community 5 (Wearable Sensing and Behavior), which makes it a concrete unported opportunity for streaming physiological sensing, where a fast motion channel routinely commits to a label before a slower cardiac channel can contradict it.
