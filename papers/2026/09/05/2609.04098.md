# Why Gated DeltaNet Survives 4-Bit Quantization: NVFP4 W4A4 for the Recurrent Half of a Hybrid 27B LLM

**Arxiv:** 2609.04098 | **Date:** 2026-09-05 | **Area:** mamba

## What it does
Hybrid LLMs pair softmax attention with linear-attention layers such as Gated DeltaNet, whose recurrent state summarizes the context in fixed size. Community 4-bit quantizations of Qwen3.8-27B left the GDN block at 8 or 16 bit, especially its decay and write-strength gates, on the intuition that errors in a recurrence accumulate. The authors quantize all 496 linear layers to NVFP4 W4A4 with calibration-only post-training quantization and match BF16 within seed noise, a five-task average of minus 0.52, across MMLU-Pro, GSM8K, AIME'25, GPQA-Diamond, LiveCodeBench and RULER retrieval to 64K, at 17.5 GiB and 14 to 19 percent faster prefill. The mechanism study finds that the supposedly fragile gate projections are the least sensitive layers, compressing about 11 percent GEMM error to about 2 percent output error, and that the delta rule forgets an injected state impulse within hundreds of steps because each write overwrites the state along the current key direction.

## Delta from parents
Where Audio Mamba and the other state-space entries in the library established what a fixed-size recurrent state can represent, this paper measures how much numerical precision that state actually needs, and the answer is four bits. The reason given is structural rather than empirical: the delta rule is contractive along the write direction, so quantization noise is overwritten rather than integrated, and the per-token quantization cost washes out with context instead of compounding. That inverts the assumption MambaOut-style analyses inherited, that a recurrence is the fragile part of a hybrid model. Against DeepSeek-V2 the comparison is now sharper, because if the recurrent half is also the cheap half to quantize, the memory argument for latent KV compression weakens relative to hybrid designs.

## Why Leo should care
Community 5, wearable sensing and behavior, contains no quantization work at all, and continuous sensing is the setting where on-device precision matters most. The mechanistic claim here, that a delta-rule recurrence forgets injected numerical noise within hundreds of steps, is exactly the property an always-on 4-bit sensing model would need, and it is stated in a form that is testable on accelerometer or PPG streams rather than only on text benchmarks.
