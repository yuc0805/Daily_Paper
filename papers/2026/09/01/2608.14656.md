# P2E-VQ: ECG-linked representation augmentation for PPG via discrete patch retrieval

**Arxiv:** 2608.14656 | **Date:** 2026-09-01 | **Area:** time-series

## What it does
PPG is cheap and easy to collect from a wrist device, but it measures peripheral pulse rather than cardiac electrical activity, so it lacks the morphological cues that ECG-based cardiac prediction relies on. The standard fix is to reconstruct an ECG waveform from PPG, which is an ill-posed inverse problem and, as the paper notes, waveform fidelity does not track downstream accuracy. P2E-VQ drops reconstruction and does retrieval instead: PPG is cut into patches, each patch is quantised to a discrete token, and the token indexes a memory bank of ECG-linked representations built only from training data. Inference needs PPG alone. Across five public datasets and six downstream tasks spanning clinical endpoints and affective state, the method beats pretrained baselines under one frozen-feature linear-probing protocol.

## Delta from parents
Where Sensor2Text maps a sensor stream into another modality and treats the generated target as the output, this paper keeps the cross-modal supervision but throws away the generated target, because the mapping is ill-posed and generation quality is not what the downstream task consumes. The ECG is used to build a lookup table of representations at training time and is never produced at inference. Where Yuan 2024 improves the PPG encoder by pretraining on more unlabelled PPG, this paper leaves the encoder's input distribution alone and adds a second modality only as a retrieval index, which is a different way to spend an ECG-paired dataset. Gu 2025 sets the evaluation frame both share, and the contribution here is measured inside it rather than against it: same frozen-feature linear probe, better numbers.

## Why Leo should care
This is squarely in the primary area and it closes off a specific piece of low-hanging fruit. The imported method is discrete tokenisation plus retrieval augmentation, a pairing that has been standard in language modelling for several years and is now demonstrated on physiological signal. If Leo was holding retrieval-augmented bio-signal representation as an open transfer, it is no longer open in the PPG-to-ECG direction. The reusable idea is the reframing rather than the architecture: when a cross-modal mapping is ill-posed, retrieve the paired representation instead of generating the paired signal. That argument applies to IMU-to-video, PPG-to-blood-pressure, and any other wearable pairing where the reconstruction target is under-determined.
