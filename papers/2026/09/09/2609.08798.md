# Eliciting Weak-to-Strong Generalization with On-Policy Reverse Distillation

**Arxiv:** 2609.08798 | **Date:** 2026-09-09 | **Area:** reasoning

## What it does
Addresses whether a stronger student can learn from a weaker teacher and end up above it, which matters when a new model generation should inherit post-training gains without repeating frontier-scale post-training from scratch. On-Policy Reverse Distillation measures the weak teacher's policy shift relative to its own reference policy, evaluated on the student's rollouts rather than the teacher's, and amplifies the component of the student's verifier-driven policy gradient lying along that direction. Because only verifier-supported updates are rescaled, the stationary points of the student's policy optimization are preserved, so the teacher accelerates the student without becoming a target the student converges to. Reported across successive model transfer and multi-teacher consolidation, OPRD reaches the final performance of competing RL and distillation methods substantially earlier and then passes them; response-style analysis shows OPRD students resemble verifier-only RL models more than they resemble their teachers.

## Delta from parents
Where conventional distillation treats the teacher's output distribution as the optimization target, and therefore transfers the teacher's capacity ceiling along with its knowledge, OPRD never makes the teacher a target at all: it uses the teacher only to pick a direction in the student's own gradient to amplify, because a direction can be borrowed without inheriting a ceiling. Where DeepSeek-R1 obtained reasoning purely from verifier reward and paid for it in sample count, OPRD keeps the same verifier signal and uses weak-teacher guidance to reach the same place with fewer student updates. Where PPO constrains the update to stay near the current policy for stability, OPRD adds an anisotropic rescaling that deliberately stretches one direction, and leans on the verifier-support restriction rather than a trust region to keep that safe. The claim that gains extend to conventional strong-to-weak distillation as well suggests the mechanism is about combining verifier reward with directional guidance generally, not about the capacity ordering.

## Why Leo should care
The reusable idea is separating what a teacher is for from what a teacher is copied into: guidance as a direction rather than a target. That is directly relevant to any setting where a small specialist model must absorb what a larger or older model learned without being capped by it, which is the normal situation when a time-series or biosignal model is retrained as data accumulates. It also depends on having a verifier, and identifying what plays the verifier role outside math and code is the open question worth carrying into his own area.
