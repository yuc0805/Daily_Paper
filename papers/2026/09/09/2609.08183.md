# NeoHorse-1: Towards Recursive Self-Improvement via Agentic Post-Training with Routing Harness

**Arxiv:** 2609.08183 | **Date:** 2026-09-09 | **Area:** agent

## What it does
Builds a family of agent-native models around a closed loop in which serving traffic becomes training data. A heterogeneous model pool sits behind an intelligent router, and for each user turn the system records the predicted capability demand, the service tier selected, and the resulting interaction. Those records are converted into training examples that keep interleaved reasoning, tool calls and harness context intact, admitted through structural validation, six-dimensional semantic evaluation and subscene-level labeling. The same routing signals then organize supervised fine-tuning into a three-stage curriculum and drive routing-guided on-policy distillation, after which capability-guided allocation turns evaluation feedback into the next training mixture. Across eleven benchmarks covering harness-based agents, tool use, coding and instruction following, post-training raises the macro-average from 58.94 to 64.87 at 4B and from 65.60 to 69.04 at 9B, with the post-trained 4B closing much of the gap to the 9B base model.

## Direct parents (in Leo's library)

- [J8DYBKW2] ADaPT (Prasad 2023) — established as-needed decomposition of agent tasks, the capability-demand signal the router is predicting.
- [2JCKA7GI] DeepSeek-V3 (2024) — the reference for routing plus staged post-training as an engineering pipeline at scale.

## Delta from parents
Where LATS searched over trajectories at inference time and discarded the search once an answer was produced, NeoHorse retains the trace and feeds it back as supervision, because the expensive part of agent rollouts is the harness context and throwing it away each turn is waste. Where ADaPT decomposed a task on demand to decide how much planning a step needed, NeoHorse predicts that same demand ahead of the turn and uses the prediction twice, first to route to a service tier and then to place the resulting example in a curriculum stage. Where DeepSeek-V3 routed among experts inside one model, the routing here is across a pool of separate models at different service tiers, so the router's decision is a capability estimate that survives outside the forward pass and can label data. The headline is the loop rather than any single stage: what the system learns to do determines what it learns from next. The numbers are real but modest, roughly six and three and a half macro-average points, and eleven benchmarks with a self-selected data pipeline is a setting where contamination is hard to rule out.

## Why Leo should care
The transferable structure is using a routing or triage decision as a free label on incoming data, which is a cheap way to build a curriculum when annotation is the bottleneck. That maps onto deployed sensing systems, where a device already decides how much computation a window deserves and that decision is discarded. The recursive self-improvement framing is the weakest part of the paper and should be read as one round of a loop rather than evidence about what repeated rounds would do.
