# Motion-Omni: End-to-End Joint Speech and Full-Body Motion for Spoken Dialogue

**Arxiv:** 2609.04250 | **Date:** 2026-09-07 | **Area:** multi-modal

## What it does
A conversational avatar has to decide what to say and how to move while saying it, but those abilities sit in separate model families, so the standard system is a cascade that generates speech first and then runs a motion model over the finished audio. Motion-Omni removes the cascade: a spoken dialogue model natively emits facial expression together with hand, upper-body and lower-body motion, generated from the same hidden states that produce the speech. The authors show joint training is required rather than optional, since freezing the speech pathway leaves motion misaligned with audio, and only co-adapting the language model, speech generator and motion generator under both objectives recovers alignment while keeping dialogue ability. Supervision comes from a model-agnostic pipeline that pseudo-labels consistent-voice speech responses with a replaceable motion teacher, giving 422,856 quality-ranked pairs across 1,402 hours. On a Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct backbone, Motion-Omni-Q7 stays within 2 percent of the same-audio teacher cascade on reference-free motion metrics while responding 5.4x faster at RTF 0.78, and reaches a 2.62 percent word error rate.

## Delta from parents
This paper extends `chen2024_sensor2text`, which already crossed Community 4 and Community 5 in Leo's library by joining wearable sensing to language. The new work pushes that bridge in the generative direction: Sensor2Text reads body motion and writes language, while Motion-Omni writes body motion and language together from one hidden state. The technical claim that carries beyond avatars is the ablation, since freezing the speech pathway and training only the motion head leaves the two streams misaligned, which says the alignment lives in the shared representation rather than in the decoder. Where a cascade treats motion as a function of the finished audio, Motion-Omni treats both as functions of the same latent plan, because the timing relationship between speech and gesture is decided before either surface form exists.

## Why Leo should care
The frozen-backbone ablation is the transferable result. Most multimodal work on physiological signals attaches a new head to a frozen language model and reports that it works; this paper gives a case where that recipe measurably fails and shows the failure is in temporal alignment specifically, which is the property that matters most for continuous sensor streams. The pseudo-labelling pipeline is also worth noting as a template, since it produces 1,402 hours of paired supervision from an unpaired corpus with a replaceable teacher, and paired data is the standing bottleneck in bio-sensing.
