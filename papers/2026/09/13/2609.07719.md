# A radiographic world model for clinical reasoning and evidence generation (MedDream)

**Arxiv:** 2609.07719 | **Date:** 2026-09-13 | **Area:** llm-health

## What it does
MedDream learns one continuous latent state from paired chest radiograph and report observations, and then reads that state out in two directions: diagnosis and conditional image generation. Pretraining used 2.65 million leakage-controlled radiograph-text pairs curated from 4.40 million candidates. The claim under test is that discrimination and simulation are two readouts of the same internal state rather than two separate mappings that happen to share a dataset.

## Direct parents (in Leo's library)
- [6T29JLTN] UNI Pathology (graphify `chen2023_pathology`, Community 1 "Health AI and Self-Supervised") — established that large-scale self-supervised pretraining on medical images yields a general-purpose encoder with strong label-scarce transfer.
- [GX7WR7KA] DDPM (graphify `ho2020_ddpm`, Community 3 "Vision-Language and Generative") — the denoising generative model that makes conditional synthesis of medical images tractable.
- [938DAXZT] World Models (Ha 2018, world-model-rl area) — the formulation of an internal latent state that supports both prediction and rollout.

## Delta from parents
Where UNI Pathology built a medical encoder whose only readout is discriminative, MedDream keeps the same large-scale pretraining logic but requires the latent to also reproduce the observation, because a state that can regenerate the radiograph is the one that can be audited and perturbed. Where DDPM treated conditional generation as an end in itself and was evaluated on image fidelity, MedDream evaluates generated radiographs by whether they raise downstream accuracy on held-out real data, which turns synthesis into an experimental instrument rather than a demo. Where Ha's World Models learned a latent for a controllable simulator in a game environment, MedDream ports the same idea to a setting with no action space, so the "action" is a clinical description and the rollout is a counterfactual radiograph. This crosses graphify Community 1 and Community 3, which in the current seed graph are joined only through shared self-supervised concepts and not through any single paper.

## Why Leo should care
The number to look at is not the AUROC improvement but the subgroup result: conditioning generation on a prespecified performance gap raised weighted F1 by 3.1 points for Asian patients, while matched-volume unguided augmentation lowered it by 2.3 points. That is a 5.4-point spread from the targeting decision alone, and it makes synthetic data a designed intervention with a stated hypothesis rather than a volume knob. The transfer to Leo's setting is direct and unclaimed: a wearable foundation model that can only classify cannot generate the counterfactual segment that would test where it fails, and nothing in graphify Community 5 (Wearable Sensing and Behavior) currently does generative readout. GLOBEM and MindScape are observational. A biosignal world model that synthesizes a physiologically plausible window conditioned on a stated subgroup gap is an open import.
