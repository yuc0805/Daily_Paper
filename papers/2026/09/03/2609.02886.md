# SolarWM: Open Data and Scalable Training for Long-Horizon Video World Models

**Arxiv:** 2609.02886 | **Date:** 2026-09-03 | **Area:** world-model-rl

## What it does
Provides an open pipeline for interactive video world models covering data preparation, training and long-horizon inference. A reconfigurable data engine converts 1.43 million canonical clips from 10 datasets into one frame-aligned contract covering observations, metric camera geometry, captions, quality metadata, selection decisions and provenance, decoupling source processing from mixture construction. Four models from 5B to 33B parameters are instantiated on Wan2.2, LTX-2.5 and MiniMax-H3 under a shared three-stage recipe of bidirectional adaptation, teacher-forced autoregressive initialization, and distribution matching distillation. After training on only 5-second sequences the causal models support real-time rollouts from minutes to hours, and data, weights, recipes and framework are released.

## Direct parents (in Leo's library)
- [938DAXZT] World Models (Ha and Schmidhuber 2018) — established the compact latent dynamics model trained in a single environment with data treated as given.
- [JQ7JD8AR] Navigation World Models (Bar 2024) — pushed video world models toward controllable long-horizon rollout under camera and action conditioning.
- [W44RSJJI] DINO-WM (Zhou 2025) — fixed the visual representation to a pretrained encoder so world models could be compared across tasks.

## Delta from parents
Where Ha and Schmidhuber learned dynamics in one environment and treated the corpus as a given, this paper treats the data contract itself as the object of study, because the current obstacle is not learning dynamics but comparing models trained on incommensurable corpora. Where DINO-WM held the representation fixed and let the task vary in order to make comparison possible, SolarWM holds the data fixed and lets the representation vary across four backbones, which is the complementary control and the one that isolates the training recipe. The long-horizon result is notable on its own terms: minutes-to-hours rollout from 5-second training clips, with no long-sequence fine-tuning and no attention-sink mechanism.

## Why Leo should care
The methodological move — standardise the data contract, then vary the backbone — is the same one that time-series and bio-sensing needs and does not have. Wearable corpora differ in sampling rate, device, wear position, label protocol and missingness in the same way video corpora differ in temporal scale and camera geometry, and the field mixes them naively for the same reason. This paper is worth reading as a template for that contract rather than for its video results.
