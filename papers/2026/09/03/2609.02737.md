# Declarative Attention: Language Models Can Control Their Own Attention

**Arxiv:** 2609.02737 | **Date:** 2026-09-03 | **Area:** llm

## What it does
Introduces a protocol that lets a language model declare, inside its chain of thought, which region of context it needs to attend to at each stage of generation. Generation is partitioned into three modes: global (full context), focus (a named region), and local (recent output only). The inference engine parses these declarations like tool calls and skips most of the KV cache read. Zero-shot on off-the-shelf models it reduces total attended tokens during decoding by 52.0 percent on Gemma-4-31B and 31.1 percent on Qwen-3.6-27B, with accuracy drops of 1.27 and 2.75 percentage points across 15 long-context tasks.

## Direct parents (in Leo's library)
- [PHB9VRVM] Attention Is All You Need (Vaswani 2017) — graphify `vaswani2017_transformer`, Community 2. Established full quadratic attention where every query reads every key, fixing attention cost as an architectural property.
- [HBLPTRMY] Chain-of-Thought Prompting (Wei 2023) — graphify `wei2023_cot`, Community 0. Established the reasoning trace as a place the model writes intermediate content that improves its own final answer.
- [2JCKA7GI] DeepSeek-V3 (2024) — graphify `deepseek2024_v3`, Community 0. Reduced KV cache cost by compressing the per-token key and value representation.

## Delta from parents
Where Vaswani fixed the attention span as a property of the architecture, this paper makes it a decision the model takes at run time, because the fixed span is no longer affordable at million-token context. Where Wei used the chain of thought to hold intermediate answers, this paper uses it to hold control instructions that the serving engine executes, converting the reasoning trace from an output artifact into a compute-scheduling signal. Where DeepSeek-V3 shrank what each cache entry costs to read, this paper leaves the entry alone and declines to read most of them. The three parents span two graphify communities, and the paper's contribution is precisely the crossing: a Community 0 mechanism used to control a Community 2 cost.

## Why Leo should care
This is the cleanest current example of a model's reasoning trace acting as a control signal over its own compute rather than as text to be read. The structure transfers directly to long physiological streams, where the relevant window is small, often nameable in advance from the clinical question, and expensive to scan. That transfer is not covered by Community 4: TS-Agent reaches the series through operator calls and Sensor2Text and ChatTS through a trained alignment bridge, and neither changes what the model reads inside its own context. The declaration protocol is a third integration point and is currently unported to time series and bio-sensing.
