# NCP-ArchPreview Technical Report: Moving towards Latent Space Language Models through Next Concept Prediction

**Arxiv:** 2609.10715 | **Date:** 2026-09-11 | **Area:** llm

## What it does
The model adds a second autoregressive objective above next-token prediction. It builds a concept vocabulary by product-quantizing its own hidden states, then trains a dedicated Concept Module to predict the next discrete concept, where one concept spans several tokens. The predicted concept is fed back down to the token level to condition subsequent token generation, and the two objectives are trained jointly end to end. The architecture was scaled to 8.9B parameters over 5.73T tokens of Dolma-3, which the authors state is the largest latent-space language model trained to date.

## Direct parents (in Leo's library)
- [2BJAGQ5K] Coconut: Training Large Language Models to Reason in a Continuous Latent Space (Hao 2024) — established that a model can carry reasoning state in continuous hidden vectors instead of emitted tokens.
- [PHB9VRVM] Attention Is All You Need (Vaswani 2017) — the next-token autoregressive transformer that supplies the token-level backbone this work keeps.
- [EMXEJYHV] Latent Reasoning: A Survey (Zhu 2025) — the map of latent-space approaches and their shared unresolved problem, which is that latent states are unsupervised and therefore hard to train against.

## Delta from parents
Where Coconut moved reasoning into a continuous latent stream at inference and kept that stream free-floating, this work quantizes the latent space into a discrete vocabulary derived from the model's own hidden states, because a discrete target gives the latent level an actual prediction loss rather than an implicit one. The practical consequence is that the concept level becomes trainable at pretraining scale instead of being a post-training reasoning trick: the concept objective runs alongside next-token prediction over 5.73T tokens rather than being fitted afterward on reasoning traces. Where the transformer of Vaswani 2017 defines its only prediction horizon as one token ahead, the Concept Module predicts a unit that spans several tokens, which changes what the gradient is asked to encode at each position. The survey position of Zhu 2025 was that latent reasoning lacks a supervision signal; product quantization over hidden states is a concrete answer to that, since the codebook index is a label the model can be scored against.

## Why Leo should care
The mechanism is the transferable part rather than the language result. A product-quantized vocabulary built from a model's own hidden states, plus a prediction objective one level above the raw sample, is exactly the structure a physiological time-series model is missing: masked patch reconstruction asks the model to predict signal values, not states. Predicting the next quantized physiological concept over a window of PPG or accelerometer data would give a self-supervised objective whose unit is closer to the thing a downstream health label depends on. The concept-to-token feedback path is also worth reading closely, because that is the piece that would let a coarse state prediction condition fine-grained waveform reconstruction. No CV or NLP method was imported here; this is a method Leo could export.
