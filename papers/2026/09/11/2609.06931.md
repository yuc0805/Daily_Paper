# CARDEA: Auditable Reasoning Grounded in Spatial Evidence for End-to-End Coronary Angiography Interpretation

**Arxiv:** 2609.06931 | **Date:** 2026-09-11 | **Area:** llm-health

## What it does
CARDEA is a vision-language model that reads multi-view coronary angiography video end to end, from keyframe selection through study-level diagnosis. Its reasoning trace is required to cite bounding boxes, a scheme the authors call Chain-of-Box, so a clinician can see which image regions the conclusion rests on. Training is three stages: visual feature alignment, a self-distilled Chain-of-Box cold start, then reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards where the reward pays for both a correct answer and box use. Report generation was held out of training entirely and evaluated zero-shot.

## Direct parents (in Leo's library)

- [6T29JLTN] UNI: A General-Purpose Self-Supervised Model for Pathology (Chen 2023) — the medical-imaging foundation-model recipe this alignment stage follows.
- [2XWEG7AF] Foundation Models for Biosignals (Gu 2025) — the survey that names auditability as the blocking issue for clinical adoption.

## Delta from parents
Where DeepSeek-R1 applied verifiable rewards to math and code, where correctness is checkable in text, this paper applies the same machinery to a setting where the verifiable part is a closed-ended clinical label and the thing it is trying to shape is spatial attention, because a bounding box is checkable in a way a natural-language justification is not. The result that matters is the transfer: only the verifiable-reward stage lifted zero-shot report generation, from 0.513 macro-F1 to 0.686 against an always-normal floor of 0.312, while supervised imitation on the same closed-ended tasks did not. Where UNI produced strong pathology representations and stopped at the representation, CARDEA carries the representation into a reasoning trace that names its own evidence. Gu 2025 argued that biosignal foundation models cannot be adopted clinically while their decision process is opaque; this is one concrete answer, and the accuracy numbers say the answer is not free, since CARDEA trailed a dedicated classifier in-distribution and only drew level under domain shift at 0.91 accuracy.

## Why Leo should care
The transferable claim is that training on verifiable closed-ended tasks can surface an open-ended capability that was never trained. That is a cheap experiment to run in bio-sensing, where closed-ended labels such as arrhythmia class or sleep stage are plentiful and open-ended clinical summarization has no labels at all. The Chain-of-Box idea is an imported CV method, spatial grounding, and it is not yet ported to one-dimensional physiological signals: the analogue would be a chain-of-segment trace where the model cites time intervals of an ECG or PPG trace rather than image boxes. That port is open.
