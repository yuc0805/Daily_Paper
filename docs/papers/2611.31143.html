Head Anything: Correspondence-Guided Joint Generation of 3D Heads from Few Images

Umut Kocasari, Bartłomiej Baranowski, Richard Shaw, Eduardo Pérez-Pellitero et al. · 2026 · arXiv · arXiv:2611.31143

Why recommended

Very recent in your research areas.

Abstract

Large image-to-3D generators recover detailed heads from a single photograph but treat every image as a new object. For several images of one person, they hallucinate unseen regions independently without using information from the other images. We present Head Anything, which generates detailed head meshes from a few images with changing expressions and viewpoints. Instead of training a multi-image prior, we turn a pretrained single-image generator with a native 3D latent space into a joint generator that denoises all images together. Since global attention between images is hard to learn from scarce 3D head data, lightweight correspondence-attention adapters let every 3D token attend only to its corresponding points in the other images. We obtain correspondences by fitting a neural parametric head model to the generator's own single-image outputs, requiring no ground truth at test time. Training on both ground-truth and fitted correspondences makes the adapters robust to fitting errors. Each mesh stays pixel-aligned with its own image and borrows the unseen regions from all others. On head scans, Head Anything lowers the head Chamfer distance of the best image-to-3D generator by 32% and its cross-frame inconsistency by 47%, and its meshes improve face tracking. Code and models will be released.
