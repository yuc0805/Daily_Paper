S1-Omni: A Unified Multimodal Reasoning Model for Scientific Understanding, Prediction, and Generation

Zhao, Liu, Xu, Xu, Wang et al. (ScienceOne-AI) · arXiv 2026 · link · Appears in: Multi-Modal

What

S1-Omni maps heterogeneous scientific objects (crystal structure files, molecular SMILES strings, protein sequences, spectra, scientific images) plus natural-language instructions into one shared representation, then uses task-specific decoders for property prediction, spectrum-to-molecule generation, protein site and structure prediction, and scientific image generation and editing. It is trained on a corpus covering 200 scientific tasks with millions of reasoning samples and evaluated on more than 60 benchmarks, reportedly outperforming GPT-5.5 and Gemini-3.1-Pro on most and matching domain-specific models on several.

Why

AI for science has advanced through domain-specific models, tool-augmented language models, and scientific language models, but capabilities stay fragmented, which limits joint modeling of heterogeneous data, scientific laws, and expert knowledge. A single reasoning model that consolidates these would let one system move across data types and tasks that today require separate narrow tools.

How

The architecture has three parts: a unified representation that maps instructions and scientific objects into a shared space; alignment that folds scientific laws and expert knowledge into data construction and training; and task-specific decoding that produces domain-native outputs. Training uses the S1-Omni-Corpus of 200 tasks with millions of reasoning samples, and evaluation spans over 60 benchmarks across nine disciplines.

Builds on

  • DALL-E 2 (Ramesh 2022)Aligned text and images in one embedding space.

Solves vs predecessors

DALL-E 2 and DiT handle a single generative modality (images). S1-Omni addresses the fragmentation problem across many scientific modalities at once, so one model both predicts properties and generates structures, which separate domain tools could not do jointly.

Method vs predecessors

Where DALL-E 2 used a shared text-image space and DiT used one pixel-oriented decoder, S1-Omni keeps the shared-space idea but attaches domain-native decoders, because a spectrum or a protein sequence needs a decoder that speaks its own format rather than a pixel decoder. No scientific-modality parent sits in the library yet, so these two vision-language anchors in community 3 are the closest predecessors.
