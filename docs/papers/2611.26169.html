Products of Distinguisher Hulls

John Paul Steinberger, Zhe Zhang · 2026 · Explore · arXiv:2611.26169

Why recommended

Picked for exploration — from another field, outside your usual research areas.

Abstract

The distinguisher hull of two distributions is the convex hull of the pairs of acceptance probabilities of tests in a specified class. We study its behavior under independent products for size-bounded Boolean circuits. We derive a geometric consequence of Dwork and Tankala's supersimulator theorem (FOCS 2026), giving a substantially different proof of approximate product containment for distinguisher hulls. In particular, our construction uses nonadaptive sampling in place of adaptive score corrections.
