Near-Optimal Second-Order Convex Finite-Sum Optimization under Individual Smoothness

Xinliang Zhang, Lesi Chen, Zonghan Li, Jingzhao Zhang · 2026 · arXiv · arXiv:2610.42040

Why recommended

Very recent in your research areas.

Abstract

We study the stochastic second-order oracle complexity of solving smooth convex finite-sum optimization with $n$ components. Very recently, Tsedenov \& Kovalev (2026) showed that the minimax optimal complexity under averaged smoothness and convexity is $\tildeΘ(n + n^{6/7} ε^{-2/7})$ by establishing nearly matching upper and lower bounds. In this paper, we show that individual smoothness and convexity provably enable further acceleration, where the lower complexity bound is $Ω(n+ n^{5/7} ε^{-2/7})$ under this stronger assumption. Then, by establishing a novel second-order self-bounding inequality, we design a new Taylor-corrected lazy Newton method, which can lead to a better complexity of $\tilde{\mathcal{O}}(n + n^{5/7} ε^{-2/7})$ by applying the optimal Monteiro-Svaiter acceleration, matching our lower bound up to only logarithmic factors.
