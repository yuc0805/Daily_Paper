Perceive-to-Reason: Decoupling Perception and Reasoning for Fine-Grained Visual Reasoning

Li, Huang, Li, Jiang, Wang, Xu, Zhang, Hong, Huang, Xue, Lu, Xiao, Zhuang, Shen (Zhejiang University) · arXiv 2026 · arXiv:2607.01191 · Appears in: Multi-Modal

What

Perceive-to-Reason (P2R) is a two-stage framework for fine-grained visual reasoning in vision-language models. The model first acts as a Perceiver that localizes the question-relevant region in a high-resolution image, then acts as a Reasoner that answers using the annotated image and the cropped region. Training uses Perception-Reasoning Alternating GRPO (PRA-GRPO), a role-aware reinforcement-learning scheme that alternates perception-focused and reasoning-focused updates with only final-answer supervision. On Qwen3-VL-Instruct backbones, P2R-4B reaches 93.2% on V-Star, 81.9% on HR-Bench-4K, and 80.5% on HR-Bench-8K.

Why

Vision-language models fail when a small but decisive cue is buried in a high-resolution image. Existing fixes repeatedly crop the image or run a test-time visual search, but they do not separate finding the evidence from reasoning about it, and they often need region annotation. The paper's aim is to make the perceive-then-reason split explicit and learn it without bounding-box labels.

How

The two roles share one model but are trained with alternating updates: perception-focused updates push the Perceiver to localize the right region, and reasoning-focused updates push the Reasoner to answer correctly, both driven only by the final-answer reward. Because the reward is split by role, the training signal can tell whether a failure came from perception or from reasoning, which a single undifferentiated reward cannot. The gains carry beyond high-resolution benchmarks to broader multimodal reasoning tasks.

Solves vs predecessors

Plain GRPO (as in DeepSeek-R1) rewards the whole reasoning trace with one signal, so on fine-grained visual tasks it cannot tell whether the model failed to find the evidence or failed to reason over it. P2R fixes that failure mode by assigning perception and reasoning their own alternating reward updates, so credit is attributed to the stage that caused the error, while still using only final-answer supervision.

Method vs predecessors

Compared to test-time cropping and visual-search methods, P2R makes the perceive-then-reason split an explicit, trained two-stage process rather than an inference-time heuristic. Compared to Latent-GRPO and DeepSeek-R1, the technical addition is role structure inside the reward: PRA-GRPO alternates between perception and reasoning objectives instead of optimizing one undivided trace.
