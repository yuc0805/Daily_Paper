Maturity$^2$: Assessing the Maturity of Crypto Agility Maturity Models

Leonie Wolf, Robin Leander Schröder, Sebastian Schinzel, Fabian Ising · 2026 · Explore · arXiv:2611.17003

Why recommended

Picked for exploration — from another field, outside your usual research areas.

Abstract

Crypto agility maturity models aim to support organizations in preparing for cryptographic transitions, such as migrating to post-quantum cryptography. Recognizing the importance of measurable maturity, NIST references several such models in its guidance. Given NIST's influence on cybersecurity practice, these models may shape organizational planning and governance mechanisms. However, their design quality has not been systematically evaluated. In this work, we assess three industry-driven maturity models against established design principles. Our analysis reveals recurring weaknesses, particularly the lack of measurable, reproducible assessment criteria and limited prescriptive guidance for improving crypto agility. At the same time, we highlight individual strengths that can assist practitioners in choosing among the models and derive concrete recommendations for strengthening future crypto agility maturity models.
