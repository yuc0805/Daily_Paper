Attention Amnesia in Hybrid LLMs: When CoT Fine-Tuning Breaks Long-Range Recall, and How to Fix It

Zhou, Zhu, Xu, Li, Chen, Wang, Guo · arXiv 2026-06-09 · arXiv:2606.11052 · Appears in: SSM / Hybrid Architectures

What

The paper documents a failure mode of hybrid linear-attention LLMs: chain-of-thought supervised fine-tuning systematically destroys long-context recall. HypeNet-9B drops from 67.2% to 9.4% on NIAH-S2 at 256K context after CoT-SFT. The proposed fix, QK-Restore, is training-free and recovers long-context ability while preserving reasoning gains (S3@256K on HypeNet-5B: 65.4% to 76.4%).

Why

Hybrid linear-attention architectures are the main path to cheap long-context inference, and CoT-SFT is the standard recipe for reasoning ability. The paper shows these two ingredients conflict: reasoning supervision biases attention gradients toward short-range patterns, and the damage concentrates in the query-key projections (W_Q, W_K) responsible for long-range routing. Nobody had isolated where the damage lives or shown it is reversible.

How

After CoT-SFT, revert only W_Q and W_K to the pre-SFT checkpoint and keep all other post-SFT parameters — zero additional training. A Procrustes variant rotates rather than fully reverts the projections, balancing routing preservation against reasoning adaptation. The result holds across architectures including HypeNet and Jet-Nemotron, and the degradation it repairs grows with context length and retrieval difficulty.

Solves vs predecessors

The SSM and linear-attention line established that hybrids can match transformer recall at lower cost, but treated post-training as harmless. This paper fixes the silent-regression failure mode — a model that still benchmarks well on reasoning while its 256K retrieval has collapsed — by localizing the damage to two weight matrices and reverting them surgically.

Method vs predecessors

Where prior interpretability work on fine-tuning damage proposed regularized or replay-based training, QK-Restore is purely post-hoc: no data, no gradients, just parameter surgery informed by a gradient-bias diagnosis. In graph terms the paper adds an edge between the reasoning community (community 0) and the architecture community (community 2), because it shows reasoning adaptation and long-range routing compete for the same parameters.
