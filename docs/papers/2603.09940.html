SignalMC-MED: A Multimodal Benchmark for Evaluating Biosignal Foundation Models on Single-Lead ECG and PPG

Gustafsson, Gu, Carletti, Palo, Eyre, Clifton (Oxford) · arXiv 2026 · arXiv:2603.09940 · Appears in: Signal/Time Series

What

SignalMC-MED is a benchmark for evaluating biosignal foundation models on synchronized single-lead ECG and PPG. It contains 22,256 visits with 10-minute overlapping ECG and PPG and defines 20 clinically relevant tasks spanning demographics, emergency-department disposition, laboratory-value regression, and detection of prior ICD-10 diagnoses. Its findings are that domain-specific biosignal models beat general time-series models, that ECG+PPG fusion beats single-signal input, that the full 10-minute window beats shorter segments, that larger model variants do not reliably beat smaller ones, and that hand-crafted ECG features remain strong complementary baselines.

Why

The field had a growing set of biosignal foundation models but no common multimodal clinical benchmark to compare them, so claims of progress were hard to check across papers. Existing evaluations differed in signals, tasks, and splits. The paper sets out to supply one controlled measuring stick on paired ECG+PPG with a fixed task suite.

How

The benchmark fixes the data (22,256 visits, 10-minute paired ECG and PPG) and the 20 tasks, then runs foundation models and baselines under the same protocol. By varying one factor at a time it isolates the effect of modality fusion, signal length, and model size, and compares against hand-crafted features. The reported result is that fusion and longer signals help, scale does not reliably help, and hand-crafted features stay competitive.

Solves vs predecessors

The Gu 2025 survey described the model class but left evaluation fragmented, so two papers claiming state of the art could not be compared. SignalMC-MED fixes this by standardizing the data and tasks, which also surfaces failure modes the individual papers did not report, namely that bigger models do not reliably win and that hand-crafted features still help.

Method vs predecessors

This is a benchmark rather than a model, so the contribution is empirical control rather than a new architecture. Where prior work built single biosignal models, SignalMC-MED measures them side by side and decomposes performance into modality, length, and size factors, giving a reusable comparison protocol.
