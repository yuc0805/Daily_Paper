AlayaWorld: Interactive Long-Horizon World Modeling

AlayaWorld Team, Zhang et al. (Alaya Lab) · arXiv 2026 · link · Appears in: Generative CV

What

AlayaWorld is an interactive video world model that generates explorable environments from text, an image, or video. It produces 24-fps video at 540p and 720p from a 15B video diffusion transformer, generating short latent chunks autoregressively under a given camera trajectory and switchable text prompts. Its bounded visual context combines a persistent sink frame, compressed temporal history, geometry-aligned spatial memory, and recent-frame conditioning, so the scene stays consistent over long runs. A discrete autoregressive distillation step reduces inference from about 30 sampling steps to four per chunk, and the model reports the best long-horizon score on iWorld-Bench.

Why

Conventional video game creation relies on labor-heavy pipelines for assets, animation, physics, and programming, while a video world model can generate an interactive environment directly from user input. Doing so needs four capabilities at once: interaction, persistent spatiotemporal consistency, stable long-horizon generation, and fast response. Prior generators handle short clips but drift or slow down over long interactive sequences, which is the gap this paper targets.

How

The model generates latent chunks one after another, conditioning on a persistent sink frame, compressed history, a geometry-aligned spatial memory, and recent frames. To limit long-term drift it trains on corrupted histories and on prediction residuals collected from the model's own rollouts, so the model learns to recover from its own errors. A discrete autoregressive distillation formulation that combines distribution-matching distillation, self-forcing++, and consistency distillation cuts sampling to four steps per chunk for interactive speed.

Builds on

  • DiT (Peebles 2023)Established the diffusion transformer backbone AlayaWorld scales to 15B for video.
  • DDPM (Ho 2020)Introduced the denoising diffusion objective the generator is built on.

Solves vs predecessors

DiT demonstrated a transformer diffusion backbone for single images; AlayaWorld extends it to autoregressive video with an explicit memory so frames stay consistent over long horizons rather than drifting. DDPM defined a fixed multi-step denoising process; AlayaWorld distills it to four steps per chunk so generation runs at interactive speed instead of tens of steps per frame.

Method vs predecessors

Where DiT and DDPM produce a fixed-size output through many denoising steps, AlayaWorld adds autoregressive chunking, a bounded memory built from sink frame and geometry-aligned spatial state, and self-generated-residual training for drift control. Both parents sit in the Vision-Language and Generative community of the library, and the CycleGAN-to-DDPM bridge already links image translation to diffusion there; AlayaWorld pushes that line into interactive memory-conditioned video.
