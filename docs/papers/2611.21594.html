Surrogate-Assisted Compact-Model Extraction for SKY130 CMOS at 77 K

Aaron Wang, Olivia Gzamouranis, Vaibhav Lohia, Hershel Fei Xun Weiner et al. · 2026 · arXiv · arXiv:2611.21594

Why recommended

Very recent in your research areas.

Abstract

Cryogenic circuit design requires compact transistor models that reproduce measured low-temperature behavior and run in standard circuit simulators. We fit BSIM4 parameters to measured I-V curves of SkyWater 130 nm (SKY130) transistors at 77 K using a neural surrogate of NGSpice. The surrogate maps compact-model parameters and device geometry to the complete set of output and transfer characteristics. Extraction is a bounded least-squares search: a bank of NGSpice simulations supplies initial candidates, finite differences of the surrogate supply the local Jacobian, and NGSpice re-simulates the resulting cards before selection. On 18 measured geometries, we compare a search over seven card parameters with a search over 43 parameters selected by NGSpice sensitivity. Relative to NGSpice re-simulations of the previously released cards, the two searches reduce the in-sample mean relative root-mean-square error (RRMS) by 17% and 26%, respectively. The 43-parameter search has the lower overall error, but the seven-parameter search has the lower mean on high-bias curves, and some individual curves have higher error than with the released cards under both searches.
