Uncertainty Quantification for Flow-Based Vision-Language-Action Models

See arXiv listing · arXiv 2026 · link · Appears in: Generative CV

What

This paper studies VLAs whose action head is a flow-matching generator trained on large robot datasets. It adds a way to quantify confidence in a generated action and to flag actions that may be unreliable, giving an out-of-distribution and low-confidence detector for flow-based action models.

Why

Flow-based VLAs perform well on robotic manipulation but have no mechanism to report when a prediction is uncertain, which is a real risk in changing environments. Prior diffusion and flow work optimized sample quality, not calibrated confidence.

How

The method attaches uncertainty estimates to the flow-matching action head so the model can signal low confidence and detect inputs outside its training distribution, without giving up the generative action quality the flow model provides.

Builds on

  • DDPM (Ho 2020)Established denoising diffusion as a generative process; flow matching is the continuous-time relative.
  • DiT (Peebles 2023)Showed a Transformer can drive a diffusion model.

Solves vs predecessors

DDPM and DiT produce realistic samples but say nothing about per-sample confidence. This paper fixes the missing-uncertainty failure for a deployed action model, where a confident wrong action is worse than an abstention.

Method vs predecessors

Where DDPM and DiT define and scale the generative process, this work layers an uncertainty estimator on the flow-matching head, shifting the question from sample quality to calibrated confidence and out-of-distribution detection.
