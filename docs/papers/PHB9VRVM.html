Attention Is All You Need

Vaswani, Shazeer, Parmar, Uszkoreit, Jones, Gomez, Kaiser, Polosukhin · NeurIPS 2017 · paper · zotero_key: PHB9VRVM · Appears in: Signal/Time Series, Self-supervised

What

The Transformer is a sequence-to-sequence architecture built entirely from stacked self-attention and position-wise feedforward layers, with no recurrence and no convolution. An encoder maps the input sequence to a set of continuous representations; a decoder generates the output one token at a time, attending to both its own past outputs (masked self-attention) and the encoder's output (cross-attention). Positional encodings are added at the input layer so the model can use sequence order despite the permutation-invariant attention operation.

Why

Prior dominant sequence transduction models (RNN-based seq2seq with attention, ConvS2S) had two structural drawbacks: recurrent models forced sequential computation along the sequence dimension, which blocked GPU parallelism within a training example, and the path length between any two positions in convolutional models grew with their distance, making long-range dependencies hard to learn. The motivation of this paper is to replace recurrence with a mechanism that (a) parallelises fully along the sequence, (b) has O(1) maximum path length between any pair of positions, and (c) still produces state-of-the-art translation quality.

How

Multi-head scaled dot-product attention is the core operator: queries, keys, and values are linear projections of the same sequence (self-attention) or of two different sequences (cross-attention), and each attention head computes softmax(QKT/√d_k)V. Stacking 6 encoder and 6 decoder layers, each layer combines multi-head attention with a position-wise feedforward network and residual connections plus layer normalisation. The decoder uses a causal mask to prevent attending to future positions. Training uses Adam with a warm-up learning-rate schedule and label smoothing; the base model has 65M parameters.

Solves vs predecessors

Compared to RNN-based seq2seq with attention, the Transformer removes the sequential dependency along time, so all positions of a layer are computed in parallel during training, cutting training time on WMT En→De from days to roughly 12 hours on 8 GPUs while raising BLEU by >1 point over the previous ensemble best. Compared to convolutional seq2seq, the maximum path length between two positions drops from O(logk n) (ConvS2S) to O(1), so long-range dependencies need no architectural workaround.

Method vs predecessors

Where prior models added attention as an auxiliary mechanism on top of recurrence or convolution, this paper makes attention the only state-mixing operation. The multi-head design lets different heads focus on different relation types (e.g. syntactic head vs lexical head) without a hand-crafted inductive bias, which earlier attention work did not exploit. Positional encodings replace the implicit ordering signal that recurrence provided, decoupling sequence position from computation order.
