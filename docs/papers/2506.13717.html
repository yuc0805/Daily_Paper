CLAMP: Contrastive Self-Supervised Learning As Neural Manifold Packing

Zhang, Heeger, Martiniani · arXiv 2025-06-16 (v2 2026-01-03) · arXiv:2506.13717 · Appears in: Self-Supervised Framework

What

CLAMP introduces a self-supervised learning framework that recasts representation learning as a manifold packing problem. It uses a loss inspired by repulsive particle systems from physics (jammed packings) to optimize the sizes and positions of per-class sub-manifolds in embedding space.

Why

Standard contrastive SSL methods (SimCLR, BYOL, MoCo) use point-wise comparisons that do not explicitly reason about the geometric structure of learned representations. The connection between contrastive learning and manifold separation in the visual cortex suggests a more principled geometric objective could yield better-organized representations.

How

Each image's augmented views form a sub-manifold. A packing loss based on short-range repulsive potentials (from the physics of simple liquids) pushes sub-manifolds apart while their internal structure is shaped by augmentation consistency. The hyperparameters have direct geometric meaning (repulsion range, interaction strength). Training dynamics parallel jamming transitions in particle physics.

Builds on

  • SSL for HAR (Yuan 2024)Largest SSL pretraining for sensor data; CLAMP offers an alternative loss formulation that could replace standard contrastive objectives in sensor SSL.

Solves vs predecessors

Point-wise contrastive losses (InfoNCE) treat each sample independently, which can lead to representation collapse or suboptimal manifold geometry. CLAMP's manifold-level loss directly optimizes for separation between class manifolds, providing a more principled objective. The physics-inspired formulation also yields interpretable training dynamics.

Method vs predecessors

Where InfoNCE maximizes agreement between positive pairs and disagreement with negatives at the point level, CLAMP operates on manifold-level geometry using a repulsive potential. This replaces the temperature-scaled softmax with a physically motivated interaction function. The approach is complementary to, rather than replacing, the encoder architecture choices in SSL pipelines.
