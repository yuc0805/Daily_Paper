CUVE: Making Supervised Classification more Accessible through Visualization

Sanchitha Kuppusami, Xiyao Wang, Miguel Nacenta · 2026 · Explore · arXiv:2611.14098

Why recommended

Picked for exploration — from another field, outside your usual research areas.

Abstract

We present CUVE, an interface that enables people to build classifiers by generating visualizations that are interpretable by both humans and learning algorithms (the Visualization as Intermediate Representation approach). This UI enables users to create supervised classifiers and to visually investigate how to improve them and how they carry out their function. A preliminary study investigates the usability of our prototype and compares it with a traditional text-based programming interface.
