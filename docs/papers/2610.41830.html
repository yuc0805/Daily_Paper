Resonant Amplification of Demographic Noise in Stochastic Common-Pool Resource Dynamics

Susanna C. Caruso, Fabio Menegazzo, Vito Latora, Samir Suweis · 2026 · Explore · arXiv:2610.41830

Why recommended

Picked for exploration — from another field, outside your usual research areas.

Abstract

Common-pool resources are vulnerable to overexploitation, yet many social-ecological systems exhibit persistent oscillations. We introduce a stochastic eco-evolutionary model in which harvesting strategies and resource availability shape one another. In the infinite population limit the system approaches a stable state through damped oscillations. Demographic noise continually excites these modes, generating instead robust macroscopic quasi-cycles, whose frequencies depend on the noise ratio, the relative timescale between strategy and resource, and the harvesting pressure. The model reproduces the distinct frequency peaks, observed in empirical global fish-landings data for high- and low-income countries, showing that persistent environmental fluctuations can emerge naturally from demographic noise.
