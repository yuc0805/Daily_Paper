Nemotron-Labs-Diffusion: A Tri-Mode Language Model Unifying Autoregressive, Diffusion, and Self-Speculation Decoding

Fu, Whalen, Garg et al. (NVIDIA) · arXiv 2026 · link · Appears in: LLM

What

Nemotron-Labs-Diffusion trains one network with a joint autoregressive-plus-diffusion objective so it can switch decoding modes at deployment. Diffusion supplies lookahead planning; autoregression supplies left-to-right language priors. In self-speculation mode diffusion drafts and autoregression verifies. At 3B, 8B, and 14B parameters it reports higher accuracy and speed than open autoregressive and diffusion baselines; the 8B model decodes 6x more tokens per forward pass than Qwen3-8B at comparable accuracy, giving 4x throughput on one benchmark.

Why

Autoregressive language models decode one token at a time, which caps throughput, while diffusion language models decode in parallel but lack strong left-to-right priors. Running them as separate systems forces a choice between planning quality and linguistic fidelity, and existing multi-token-prediction speculation does not exploit diffusion's parallelism for drafting.

How

A single model is trained under both objectives so the two become complementary inside one set of weights. The study shows diffusion improves lookahead planning while autoregression provides linguistic priors, and in self-speculation mode diffusion drafts while autoregression verifies, beating multi-token prediction on acceptance rate and real-device efficiency. A speed-of-light analysis reports up to 76.5% more tokens per forward pass than self-speculation under an optimal sampler.

Solves vs predecessors

Pure autoregressive models (the Transformer lineage) are throughput-limited, and pure diffusion language models lack left-to-right priors. Nemotron fixes both by training one model under a joint objective so it switches modes, and it beats the DeepSeek-V3 multi-token-prediction baseline on acceptance rate by using diffusion as the drafter.

Method vs predecessors

Where DDPM applies diffusion to continuous images and the Transformer decodes text token by token, this paper trains a single model under both objectives so diffusion handles parallel lookahead and autoregression handles verification. It joins two lineages that rarely meet in one model, and it uses diffusion for drafting rather than the separate small drafter that speculative-decoding baselines use.
