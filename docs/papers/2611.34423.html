Reading the Room with AI: FOMC Deliberation and Growth-at-Risk

Alvaro Ortiz, Tomasa Rodrigo · 2026 · Explore · arXiv:2611.34423

Why recommended

Picked for exploration — from another field, outside your usual research areas.

Abstract

Macro-at-Risk models forecast the growth distribution from public data; policy is set by a committee arguing over their meaning. From FOMC Minutes, public three weeks after each meeting, we add several dimensions of deliberation to a growth quantile model on FRED-MD's 123 series. Only disagreement present in the discussion but absent from the vote lowers out-of-sample loss by 4-6 percent, chiefly in the center, beyond professional, staff and Committee forecasts. Much of deliberation's informational value lies in the recorded discussion itself, and in the disagreement it reveals, rather than in the risk assessments the Committee chooses to state.
