AtomiMed: Hierarchical Atomic Fact-Checking for Universal Clinical-Aware Medical Report Evaluation

Wang, Chang, Jiang, Gao, Zhang, Yuan, Cao, Shi, Zhang, Liu, Zhang (Zhejiang University) · arXiv 2026 · arXiv:2606.31292 · Appears in: LLM-Health (bridge: Hallucination)

What

AtomiMed is a modality-agnostic evaluation framework for generated medical reports. It decomposes a report into a hierarchy of atomic clinical facts — disease-level entities and attribute-level descriptors such as location, morphology, and severity — and runs an agentic cross-verification loop between the ground-truth and predicted reports, imitating a multi-radiologist peer review. This decouples detection accuracy from descriptive accuracy. The authors release MRGEvalKit for automated hierarchical extraction and OmniMRG-Bench, a benchmark covering X-ray, CT, MRI, and ultrasound.

Why

Traditional medical-report-generation metrics rely on surface-level n-gram overlap, which does not capture clinical factual accuracy and can overlook catastrophic diagnostic errors. A report-level overlap score also cannot say which specific claim is wrong. The paper's goal is an evaluation that aligns with radiologist judgment and localizes errors to individual clinical facts.

How

Reports are parsed into the atomic-fact hierarchy, and an agentic cross-verification loop checks each predicted fact against the ground-truth report and vice versa, separating whether a finding was detected from whether it was described correctly. Across expert-annotated reader studies, AtomiMed correlates with radiologist judgment better than traditional and model-based metrics.

Solves vs predecessors

Med-VQA Hallucination measured hallucination on question answering, not on full report generation, and left evaluation of generated reports to overlap metrics that miss factual errors. AtomiMed fixes the specific failure — "an overlap score cannot tell which clinical claim is wrong" — by decomposing the report into atomic facts and verifying each with a cross-verification loop, giving a fact-localized, clinically aligned score.

Method vs predecessors

Compared to n-gram and model-based metrics, AtomiMed replaces a single similarity score with hierarchical atomic-fact extraction plus agentic peer-review verification. Compared to Verifiable Physio Reasoning, which built verifiability into generation, AtomiMed moves the same principle to the evaluation side and makes it modality-agnostic across four imaging types.
