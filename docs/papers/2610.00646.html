A Hilbert-Space Analogy for Sexual Orientation and Gender Identity

Sofia Fatigoni · 2026 · Explore · arXiv:2610.00646

Why recommended

Picked for exploration — from another field, outside your usual research areas.

Abstract

We introduce a finite-dimensional state-space representation of bisexuality in Dirac notation, specify normalized amplitude weights, and analyze the limits of inference from one single observation. We extend the basis for discussing attraction across genders, then define a distinct state space for gender identity with an illustrative normalized time-dependent trajectory. The model is an analogy, not a physical theory or a measurement procedure for human sexuality.
