Breaking Failure Cascades: Step-Aware Reinforcement Learning for Medical Multimodal Reasoning (MRPO)

Jung, Jeong, Lim, Sung, Yun, Roh, Sung, Kang · arXiv 2026 · arXiv:2606.31825 · Appears in: LLM-Health

What

MRPO (Medical Reasoning-aware Policy Optimization) is a reinforcement learning method for multimodal models that answer questions about clinical images. It adds step-wise process rewards to the GRPO objective: when the final answer is wrong, it assigns exponentially larger penalties to tokens in the earlier invalid reasoning steps, so the model learns to fix the root of a reasoning cascade rather than the surface. Across three multimodal backbones it beats standard GRPO, and on Qwen3-VL-8B it surpasses the much larger HuatuoGPT-Vision-34B by 2.79 points while cutting early-stage reasoning failures from 64.0% to 13.0%.

Why

Post-training for medical multimodal models is usually outcome-centric: the reward comes only from whether the final answer is correct. That signal is sparse and does not say which reasoning step failed. The authors show that on medical visual question answering, most wrong answers trace back to an error in an early step that then propagates. Outcome-only methods such as DeepSeek-R1-style RL and standard GRPO cannot target that early step directly, so they optimize the reasoning process weakly.

How

MRPO keeps the GRPO framework but changes credit assignment. When a trajectory ends in a wrong answer, tokens in earlier reasoning steps receive exponentially larger penalties than later ones, breaking the failure cascade without punishing successful paths. The method is applied across three multimodal LLM backbones and evaluated on medical VQA benchmarks, with code and models released.

Solves vs predecessors

DeepSeek-R1 showed that RL on outcome rewards can train strong reasoning, but the reward gives no information about which step went wrong, so on clinical images the model keeps making the same early mistakes. MRPO fixes the specific failure mode — an early invalid step poisons the rest of the chain — by pushing the penalty onto those early tokens, which drops early-stage failures from 64.0% to 13.0%.

Method vs predecessors

Compared with standard GRPO, which spreads the penalty evenly over a failed trajectory, MRPO weights earlier invalid steps exponentially more, because the early error is the cause of the later ones. Compared with DeepSeek-R1's outcome-only RL, MRPO adds explicit process-level supervision inside a multimodal medical setting. The change is in the reward shaping, not the backbone, so it transfers across the three models tested.
