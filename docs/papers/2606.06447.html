Latent Reasoning with Normalizing Flows (NF-CoT)

Tu, Fu, Yu, Tang, Kang, Qin, Zhang, Gu · arXiv 2026 · arXiv:2606.06447 · Appears in: Reasoning

What

NF-CoT replaces explicit chain-of-thought token generation with compact continuous states produced by a normalizing flow head inside the LLM backbone. Continuous-thought positions are generated by a TARFlow-style normalizing flow while text positions use the standard LM head, both within the same causal stream sharing one KV cache. On code-generation benchmarks it improves pass rates over explicit CoT and prior latent-reasoning baselines while using roughly 4x fewer intermediate tokens.

Why

Textual chain-of-thought forces every reasoning step through a discrete, serial token stream, which is wasteful when the underlying update is semantic or uncertain. Existing latent-reasoning methods (Coconut, CALM, Latent-GRPO) move computation to continuous space but sacrifice one or more of: left-to-right generation, KV-cache compatibility, probabilistic sampling, or tractable likelihood estimation. NF-CoT aims to keep all four properties while still reasoning in latent space.

How

The method instantiates a TARFlow normalizing flow inside the transformer backbone. During training, continuous thoughts are distilled from explicit CoT traces: the flow learns to model the distribution of hidden states that correspond to reasoning steps. At inference, the flow head samples continuous thoughts that the model conditions on for subsequent text generation. The normalizing flow provides exact log-likelihoods, enabling both maximum-likelihood distillation and direct policy-gradient optimization in latent reasoning space without reward-model approximation.

Builds on

  • Chain-of-Thought Prompting (Wei 2023)Source of the step-by-step reasoning paradigm; NF-CoT compresses these steps into continuous states.
  • DeepSeek-R1 (2025)Improved reasoning via RL over token outputs; NF-CoT replaces RL-over-tokens with a generative model over latent states.

Solves vs predecessors

Chain-of-Thought (Wei 2023) showed that verbalizing intermediate steps improves reasoning, but the token cost scales linearly with reasoning depth and every step must be expressible in natural language. Latent-GRPO (Deng 2026) moved reasoning into continuous space but used reward-based policy optimization, which introduces variance and requires a reward model. NF-CoT solves both problems: it reasons in continuous space (eliminating token cost) and uses normalizing flows for exact density estimation (eliminating the reward model). The failure mode it fixes is the "reasoning must be verbalized" bottleneck.

Method vs predecessors

Compared to CoT, NF-CoT replaces the text-based reasoning trace with continuous thought positions that share the same causal stream. Compared to Coconut, which also uses continuous thoughts, NF-CoT adds a proper probabilistic model (the normalizing flow) over the latent states, enabling sampling and likelihood-based training rather than deterministic hidden-state propagation. Compared to Latent-GRPO, NF-CoT replaces group relative policy optimization with exact normalizing-flow likelihoods, providing a cleaner optimization signal.
