Language Models Still Struggle to Zero-shot Reason about Time Series

Merrill et al. · 2024 · publisher page · Appears in: Signal (Time Series)

What

Formal evaluation shows LLMs score near random on etiological reasoning and QA over time series, 30 points below humans.

Builds on

  • LLMTime Zero-Shot (Gruver 2023)directly tests the zero-shot forecasting claim and finds reasoning does not follow.
