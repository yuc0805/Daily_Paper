Identification of Rational Inattention-Additive Random Utility Models

Dennis Kristensen, Ruolei Zhu · 2026 · Explore · arXiv:2611.14752

Why recommended

Picked for exploration — from another field, outside your usual research areas.

Abstract

This paper introduces a novel class of econometric models, called Rational Inattention-Additive Random Utility Models. It is based on the discrete choice rational inattention model of Matvejka and McKay (2015) with additive unobserved heterogeneity incorporated. We show that some of the systematic components of the primitives of the model are nonparametrically identified even if the distributions of unobserved variables and the prior of decision makers are both unknown to the Econometrician. If additional information about the prior is known to the Econometrician, even stronger identification results become available.
