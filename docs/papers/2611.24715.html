Interpersonal trust, institutional trust and trust in technology in Air Traffic Control

Rob Procter, Mark Rouncefield · 2026 · Explore · arXiv:2611.24715

Why recommended

Picked for exploration — from another field, outside your usual research areas.

Abstract

Air traffic control (ATC), the safe, orderly and efficient management of planes through controlled airspace is a complex task. In this article, we explore the roles that different kinds of trust play in ATC. Our study provides the essential foundations for examining the conditions that need to be satisfied if Air Traffic Controllers are to establish and sustain trust relationships with technical innovations, such as agent-based systems, and so ensure their safe and dependable embedding within ATC work.
