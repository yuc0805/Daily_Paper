Friendship paradoxes in networks

Eun Lee, Hang-Hyun Jo · 2026 · Explore · arXiv:2611.29945

Why recommended

Abstract

Since Scott L. Feld's seminal paper entitled ``Why your friends have more friends than you do'' [Am.~J.~Sociol.~\textbf{96}, 1464 (1991)], the friendship paradox (FP) has been extensively studied in network science and related fields over the last decades. In this paper we survey the literature on the FP and its generalizations. We first introduce definitions and general results of the FPs defined in networks, and then overview their results on model networks. Empirical results using various social network data as well as applications to real-world problems, such as early detection of outbreaks and network sampling, are also summarized. Finally, we conclude by discussing several directions of possible future works.
