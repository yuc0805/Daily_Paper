SensorFM: A Wearable Health Foundation Model Pretrained on One Trillion Minutes of Sensor Data

Google Research · arXiv 2026 · link · Appears in: Signal/Time Series, Self-supervised

What

SensorFM is a self-supervised foundation model for wearable health data. It ingests 34 one-minute aggregate features from five sensors (PPG, accelerometer, EDA, skin temperature, altimeter) and is pretrained by masked reconstruction on more than one trillion minutes of data from about 5 million people. Downstream tasks attach light heads to the encoder. The largest variant beats conventional baselines on 34 of 35 tasks, adds an average of 9% AUC on classification and 21% Pearson on regression, and retains 99.7% step-count and 99.9% deep-sleep accuracy when 60 contiguous minutes are ablated.

Why

Wearable health models are usually built one task at a time, each from its own labeled dataset. That does not reuse structure across tasks, handles missing sensor windows poorly, and gives downstream health tools no single representation to call. Prior self-supervised work in this space either used one sensor family or stopped at survey scale, leaving open whether one encoder could fuse heterogeneous sensors at population scale.

How

The method follows the masked-autoencoder recipe: mask part of the multi-sensor input, reconstruct it, and reuse the learned encoder as a general feature extractor. Pretraining runs at population scale (about 5 million people, one trillion-plus minutes), and evaluation attaches light task heads for classification and regression. A health-agent study had four board-certified physicians, blinded to condition, rate 1,860 outputs across context, relevance, justifiability, personalization, and potential for harm.

Solves vs predecessors

Yuan 2024 modeled a single sensor family and left open whether one encoder could span many. SensorFM fixes that by pretraining across five sensor families at roughly a thousand times more data, so a single representation now serves 35 tasks instead of one bespoke model per task. It also fixes the missing-data brittleness of task-specific models: with an hour of contiguous data removed, step-count and deep-sleep accuracy barely move.

Method vs predecessors

Where Gu 2025 described the design space and Yuan 2024 used single-sensor masked prediction, SensorFM keeps the masked-reconstruction objective but scales both the sensor set and the data volume, and evaluates through a downstream health agent rather than on encoder metrics alone. The trade-off is that it consumes one-minute aggregate features rather than raw waveforms, so beat-level morphology is discarded before encoding.
