Bounded Treewidth Rulesets Are Not Finitely Controllable

Piotr Ostropolski-Nalewaja, Michaël Thomazo · 2026 · Explore · arXiv:2611.27461

Why recommended

Picked for exploration — from another field, outside your usual research areas.

Abstract

An ontology language $O$ is finitely controllable with respect to a query language $Q$ if the finite and unrestricted variants of the query entailment problem for $\langle O, Q\rangle$ coincide. In this paper, we study finite controllability (FC) in its classical sense for existential rules, where $O$ is a class of existential rules and $Q$ is the class of all Boolean conjunctive queries (BCQs). A natural question in this line of work is whether the class of bounded treewidth sets (BTS) is finitely controllable with respect to BCQs. We answer this question negatively: we exhibit a simple set of rules over a binary signature and prove that it is BTS but not FC. Moreover, using the same set of rules, we show that two subclasses of BTS, namely GBTS and pieceful, are not FC either.
