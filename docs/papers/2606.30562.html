Morphing into Hybrid Attention Models (FlashMorph)

Lan, Zheng, Ren, Xia, Wang, Xiao, Qiu, Cheng · arXiv 2026 · arXiv:2606.30562 · Appears in: Mamba / SSM

What

FlashMorph decides which layers of a Transformer should keep full attention and which should be converted to linear attention, under a fixed full-attention budget. It frames hybrid layer selection as a budget-constrained subset optimization problem. It builds a morphable model where each full-attention layer also has a linear-attention branch, freezes the weights, and jointly learns per-layer gates on synthetic long-context retrieval data with a regularizer that favors linear attention. The gates are discretized under the budget, then distillation and long-context fine-tuning follow.

Why

Hybrid attention models cut long-context cost by keeping full attention in only a subset of layers, but their quality depends heavily on which layers are chosen. Existing methods use fixed placement patterns or score layers one at a time, which treats layer importance as isolated and ignores that layers interact under a global hybrid configuration. That leaves quality on the table and makes selection expensive.

How

The morphable model equips every full-attention layer with a converted linear-attention branch. With all weights frozen, layerwise gates are optimized together on synthetic long-context retrieval data, and a linearization regularizer pushes the model to lean on linear attention for efficiency. The learned continuous gates are then discretized to meet the preset full-attention budget, instantiating the hybrid architecture, which is finished with logits distillation and long-context fine-tuning.

Builds on

  • Mamba (Gu 2024)Supplies the efficient linear-time state space alternative to attention that fills the converted layers.
  • Attention Is All You Need (Vaswani 2017)The full-attention Transformer that hybrid conversion starts from.

Solves vs predecessors

Mamba replaces attention wholesale, which is efficient but gives up the recall that full attention provides on some layers. Fixed-pattern or per-layer-score hybrid methods pick layers without accounting for how layers interact. FlashMorph fixes that by optimizing all the gates jointly under a budget, which it reports finds better hybrid configurations at lower selection cost while keeping long-context recall.

Method vs predecessors

Compared with Mamba's uniform replacement, FlashMorph keeps a chosen subset of full-attention layers and converts only the rest. Compared with heuristic hybrid selection, it treats the choice as a single global optimization with a differentiable gate per layer, rather than scoring layers independently.
