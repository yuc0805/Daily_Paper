Reversing the text is not reversing the path: a fixed-text measure without a zero, and an assembly-order comparison that vanishes identically

Y. -H. Taguchi · 2026 · Explore · arXiv:2611.23049

Why recommended

Picked for exploration — from another field, outside your usual research areas.

Abstract

For an ensemble of trajectories the fluctuation theorems give the entropy production its meaning and zero. The same expression has recently been evaluated on one fixed text, $Δ= [\log P(x) - \log P(\tilde x)]/L$ with $\tilde x$ the text in reversed order, read as an entropy production separating causally from arbitrarily ordered prose. A single text is neither a distribution nor a dynamics, and the texts at issue were produced by other models. No theorem fixes where the zero of a fixed-text quantity lies: the expression carries over from an ensemble, its zero does not. We supply a zero from an exact antisymmetry. Scoring a text's $n$ sentences in all $n!$ orders, reversal carries $π$ to $π\circ \mathrm{rev}$, a measure-preserving involution, so $Δ(π) = -Δ(π\circ \mathrm{rev})$ identically and the values are centred at zero for any score. The authored arrangement's midrank is then unitless, bounded, and 0.50 for a score blind to order. Across five autoregressive evaluators from 124M to 4B parameters, and taking the prompt-defined labels at face value, 8% to 15% of the median midrank's distance from the null is attributable to causal order and the rest to the arrangement being the order the passage was produced in. The thermodynamic reading needs a comparison whose zero does hold on the text read, and a masked model supplies one: the any-order chain rule gives a genuine log-probability, and its left-to-right and right-to-left orders for one text give $σ$, a comparison of two assembly orders. Both run from the empty sequence to the same text, so it is not a path against its time reverse. It vanishes identically when the evaluator's conditionals come from a joint, and a non-zero reading reports the evaluator's incompatibility, not the text. Neither is a fixed-text entropy production, for different reasons.
