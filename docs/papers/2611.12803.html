OCEAN: A Five-Phase Framework for Meaningful Change

A. Fronzetti Colladon · 2026 · Explore · arXiv:2611.12803

Why recommended

Picked for exploration — from another field, outside your usual research areas.

Abstract

Managing meaningful change requires more than motivation, insight, or isolated techniques. This paper introduces OCEAN, a five-phase conceptual framework for structuring nonclinical developmental conversations and change-oriented work in coaching and in the practice of leaders and HR professionals. The five phases are Observe, Create, Expose, Awaken, and Nurture. In Observe, individuals develop a systemic, present-oriented understanding of the situation. In Create, they turn that understanding into goals that are meaningful and actionable. In Expose, they identify the attempted solutions that maintain the difficulty they are meant to remove, along with the resistances, biases, and self-deceptions that keep them in place. In Awaken, they activate new behaviors through planning, reframing, paradoxical movement, and emotionally significant experiences. In Nurture, they consolidate progress through repetition, habits, connection, and sustainable contexts. OCEAN integrates insights from systemic and interactional thinking, goal construction, emotional awareness, and habit consolidation into a structured process.
