Dynamic Panel Multinomial Logit Models

Kevin Dano, Bo E. Honoré, Martin Weidner · 2026 · Explore · arXiv:2610.17104

Why recommended

Picked for exploration — from another field, outside your usual research areas.

Abstract

This paper provides moment conditions for dynamic multinomial logit panel data models with individual specific fixed effects. The moment conditions do not depend on the fixed effects and thus form the basis for generalized method of moments estimation. For a leading case, we provide sufficient conditions under which the moment conditions identify the common parameters, and we illustrate their use in an empirical application to household soft drink purchases. The construction of moment conditions is based on transformed models obtained by permuting outcomes across time periods. For any cyclic permutation, the ratio of the transformed model probability to the true model probability is free of the fixed effects, and comparing two such ratios yields restrictions on the common parameters of interest. This construction is not specific to the multinomial logit. It applies to any dynamic panel data model whose static counterpart admits a symmetric sufficient statistic for the fixed effects. This unifies our main result with analogous results for other models.
