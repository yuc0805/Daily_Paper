OpenTSLM: Time-Series Language Models for Reasoning over Multivariate Medical Text- and Time-Series Data

Langer et al. · arXiv preprint 2025 · arXiv:2510.02410 · Appears in: Signal (Time Series)

What

Time-series language models for reasoning over multivariate medical text and time-series data.

Builds on

  • ChatTS: Aligning Time Series with LLMs (Xie 2025)extends TS-LLM alignment to multivariate medical text plus series.
  • NormWear (Luo 2024)builds on wearable-sensing foundation models for the physiological side.
