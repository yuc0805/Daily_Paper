Wu et al. · arXiv preprint 2025 · arXiv:2503.12799 · Appears in: Multi-Modal

What

Grounds chain-of-thought steps in image regions for multimodal LLMs.

Builds on

  • Chain-of-Thought Prompting (Wei 2023)grounds each chain-of-thought step.
  • Interleaved-Modal CoT (Gao 2024)builds on interleaved-modal reasoning traces.
