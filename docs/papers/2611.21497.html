The CYBER-BRIDGE project: An AI-Powered Platform for Cross-Border Cybersecurity, Compliance and Forensics in the EU

Nikolaos Kekatos, Georgios Koutidis, Charalampos Bratsas, Stefan Bucur et al. · 2026 · arXiv · arXiv:2611.21497

Why recommended

Very recent in your research areas.

Abstract

Cross-border cyber attacks on European SMEs and critical-infrastructure operators expose a structural cyber-resilience gap: cybersecurity (threat sharing, detection, response), compliance (framework assessment, certification) and digital forensics (cross-jurisdictional evidence) are jointly regulated by NIS2, the CRA, GDPR and the LED, yet their tooling evolved in silos and rarely interoperates across Member States. The EU-funded CYBER-BRIDGE project integrates the three domains under one architecture and a hash-chained evidence layer: AI-driven CTI sharing with ENISA-aligned notification, multi-framework assessments with retrieval-augmented advisory, and RFC 3161-anchored custody with lawful GDPR/LED exchange, all aligned with MITRE ATT&CK. A sanitised replica of the back-end image hosts a Cyber-Range for upskilling. The contribution is not the individual tools but the reconciliation mechanism that lets one incident produce, from a single evidence record, a cross-border CTI advisory, the regulatory notifications its deadlines demand and a re-verifiable forensic pack; we set out why binding three differently regulated evidence regimes onto one record is non-trivial and how the shared evidence layer resolves the resulting tensions. This paper introduces the project's scope, requirements, pilots, architecture and toolkits; quantitative KPIs follow in later deliverables.
