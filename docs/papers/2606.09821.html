Rethinking the Divergence Regularization in LLM RL (DRPO)

Yao, Zhou, Qi, Lee, Bo, Pang · arXiv 2026-06-08 · arXiv:2606.09821 · Appears in: Reasoning

What

DRPO (Divergence Regularized Policy Optimization) replaces the hard divergence-based mask of DPPO with a smooth advantage-weighted quadratic regularizer on policy shift, for RL post-training of LLMs. It preserves DPPO's trust-region geometry while producing bounded, continuous gradient weights that attenuate diverging updates and provide corrective signal beyond the trust-region boundary.

Why

LLM RL is usually off-policy because of training-inference mismatch and policy staleness, so trust-region control determines training stability. PPO and GRPO use ratio clipping, but the importance ratio is a poor proxy for distributional shift on long-tailed vocabularies. DPPO improved this with a divergence-based mask, yet a hard mask discards the gradient of any token that crosses the boundary instead of correcting it.

How

DRPO keeps the trust region defined by the sampled token's absolute probability shift and replaces the binary keep-or-drop decision with a quadratic penalty weighted by the advantage. Tokens inside the region train normally; tokens beyond it receive bounded corrective gradients rather than none. Experiments across model scales, architectures, and precision settings show improved stability and efficiency. Code: github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/UniRL.

Builds on

  • DeepSeek-R1 (2025)Made RL the main engine of reasoning ability and exposed the off-policy stability problems DRPO targets.
  • Latent-GRPO (Deng 2026)Works inside the GRPO objective family whose trust-region approximation this paper revises.

Solves vs predecessors

R1-style RL training is notoriously unstable to reproduce, and the failure is usually optimizer-level: clipping either over-penalizes rare tokens or under-constrains confident ones, and DPPO's mask silently wastes gradient signal at the boundary. DRPO fixes the wasted-signal failure mode by making every token contribute a bounded, direction-corrected gradient.

Method vs predecessors

Compared to DeepSeek-R1's GRPO recipe, DRPO changes the constraint, not the reward: same group-relative advantages, different trust-region enforcement. Compared to Latent-GRPO, which moved reasoning into latent space while keeping the objective family, DRPO keeps token space and revises the objective itself.
