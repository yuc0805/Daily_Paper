PointDiT: Pixel-Space Diffusion for Monocular Geometry Estimation

Xu, Wu, Henzler, Kalischek, Oechsle, Manhardt, Pollefeys, Geiger, Tombari, Niemeyer · Google · arXiv 2026 · arXiv:2607.02515 · Appears in: Generative CV

What

The paper estimates 3D geometry from a single image with a plain diffusion transformer that denoises in pixel and point-map space rather than a compressed latent space. It conditions on image tokens from a pretrained DINOv3 encoder and needs no point-map tokenizer.

Why

State-of-the-art single-image reconstruction relies on hybrid architectures, intricate loss functions, or compressing geometry into a latent to reuse pretrained latent diffusion models. The paper argues this overhead is unnecessary and can blur sharp geometric structure, especially in ambiguous regions such as transparent objects.

How

PointDiT is a plain ViT diffusion transformer trained from scratch that denoises raw 3D point-map patches conditioned on DINOv3 image tokens. By operating directly in pixel space it removes the latent tokenizer entirely; despite the simpler design it reports sharper geometry and better robustness than latent-based diffusion baselines.

Builds on

  • DiTThe diffusion transformer that replaced the U-Net denoiser with a plain transformer; PointDiT is a DiT for point maps.
  • DDPMThe denoising diffusion framework that defines the training objective.
  • ViTThe plain vision transformer backbone kept unchanged.

Solves vs predecessors

Latent-diffusion geometry models compress the target into a learned latent, which adds a tokenizer to train and can blur fine structure. PointDiT removes that failure mode by denoising raw point-map patches in pixel space, giving sharper output with a simpler pipeline.

Method vs predecessors

Where DiT and DDPM targeted image synthesis in latent or pixel space, PointDiT retargets the same denoiser to dense geometric prediction and conditions on self-supervised DINOv3 features rather than class labels or text. The method contribution is showing a minimalist pixel-space setup beats the more elaborate latent pipelines it descends from.
