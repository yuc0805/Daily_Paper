The landscape of quantum technology companies in 2026

Zeki Can Seskir, Orcun Kocak, Arsev Umur Aydinoglu · 2026 · Explore · arXiv:2611.16838

Why recommended

Picked for exploration — from another field, outside your usual research areas.

Abstract

We update our 2022 mapping of 441 quantum technology (QT) companies and examine how the industry has developed. The principal dataset now includes 1,092 companies founded with a QT focus, of which 935 remain active. Public sources and archived company websites provide evidence on company formation, technology fields and geography, alongside acquisitions, closures, valuations and involvement in artificial intelligence, defence and space. Company formation continued beyond the apparent 2018 peak in the earlier study. Computing software and applications remain the largest field, with suppliers and supporting businesses accounting for almost a third of active companies. Countries with similar company totals can have different technological profiles, and regional comparisons reveal concentrations obscured by country rankings. The first companies generally preceded the selected national policy milestones. Supporting businesses usually followed technical companies in the same jurisdiction. AI is more prevalent than defence in current company activities, while defence shows the larger increase from the earliest assessed evidence. Space relevance is documented for more than a quarter of active companies, often alongside defence involvement.
