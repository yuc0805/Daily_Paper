MetaWorld: Scaling Multi-Agent Video World Models from Single-view Video Data

Hu, Lu, Wang, Zhang, Hao, Pan, Yi, Ma, Tao · arXiv 2026-06 · arXiv:2606.02753 · Appears in: Generative CV

What

MetaWorld trains multi-agent video world models from ordinary single-view videos, avoiding expensive coordinated multi-camera recordings. Monocular World-State Unrolling decomposes each clip into the camera operator's ego-motion and the visible subject's trajectory, recovering synchronized multi-agent motion inside one shared 3D space. A Subject-Aware World Generator conditions on per-agent identity images, and a World-State Alignment module inserts per-frame cross-attention at every layer of the video diffusion transformer so multiple views denoise together and stay geometrically and dynamically consistent. Experiments report better cross-view consistency and identity fidelity than single-view baselines.

Why

Video world models are a base technology for embodied AI and virtual environments, but existing models simulate a scene from a single agent and a single view. Extending them to several agents raises two problems: coordinated multi-view recordings are expensive to collect for open-domain scenes, and independently generated views cannot be trusted to keep the shared world consistent. Prior single-view approaches therefore do not scale to multi-agent settings.

How

Monocular World-State Unrolling separates ego-motion from subject trajectory in a single clip, which yields synchronized multi-agent motion in one 3D space without any multi-camera rig. The Subject-Aware World Generator drives appearance from per-agent identity images. World-State Alignment inserts per-frame inter-branch cross-attention at every transformer layer of the video diffusion transformer, synchronizing the denoising of the views so that static geometry and dynamic motion agree across them.

Solves vs predecessors

A plain DiT denoises one latent stream and generates a single consistent view. Applied independently to several agents, it produces overlapping views that instantiate different versions of the same scene. MetaWorld fixes this failure by coupling the denoising of the views with per-frame cross-attention, so the shared 3D environment evolves identically across views, and by extracting the needed multi-view training signal from single-view footage.

Method vs predecessors

Compared to DiT and DDPM, MetaWorld runs coupled latent streams rather than one, adds inter-branch cross-attention at every layer, and adds a monocular decomposition step so single-view video becomes multi-view training data. The generative machinery is inherited; the coupling and the data-extraction steps are the additions.
