TiRex-2: Generalizing TiRex to Multivariate Data and Streaming

NX-AI · arXiv 2026-07-01 · arXiv:2607.01204 · Appears in: Signal/Time Series

What

TiRex-2 is an xLSTM-based time-series foundation model that forecasts one or many target series directly from their history, optionally conditioned on past and future-known covariates, in a streaming fashion and zero-shot. Its recurrent, memory-centric design keeps cost per new patch constant as observations arrive. It pairs a bidirectional time mixer with an asymmetric grouped-attention variate mixer that mixes across variables while keeping strict causality on the target variables.

Why

Transformer-based time-series foundation models capture cross-variate dependencies but incur quadratic cost in context length and must recompute the full history each time a new observation arrives, which makes them awkward for streaming use. A model that keeps multivariate cross-series information while updating at constant per-step cost has been missing.

How

TiRex-2 uses an xLSTM recurrent backbone so state carries forward and per-patch cost stays flat under streaming. The bidirectional time mixer reads temporal structure, and the asymmetric grouped-attention variate mixer integrates future-known covariates without breaking causality on the targets. To pretrain at scale, the authors compose synthetic multivariate samples on the fly from large univariate corpora, giving cross-series structure without requiring large aligned multivariate datasets. The model runs zero-shot with no task-specific fine-tuning.

Solves vs predecessors

Transformer time-series foundation models pay quadratic context cost and recompute full history under streaming. TiRex-2 fixes this by using a recurrent xLSTM backbone that updates at constant per-patch cost, while still handling multivariate targets and future-known covariates that univariate recurrent models such as the original TiRex could not.

Method vs predecessors

Where HARMamba and Bi-Mamba+ applied recurrent state-space backbones to activity recognition and univariate or paired forecasting, TiRex-2 keeps the recurrent constant-cost idea but adds an asymmetric grouped-attention variate mixer for cross-series and covariate integration and a synthetic multivariate pretraining pipeline. Where the survey framed time-series ability as something to add by attaching an LLM, TiRex-2 stays fully numeric.
