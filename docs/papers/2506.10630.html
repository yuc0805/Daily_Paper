Time-R1: Time Series Forecasting as Reasoning — A Slow-Thinking Approach with Reinforced LLMs

Zhou, Luo, Cheng, Liu, Wang, Wang, Chen · arXiv 2025-06-12 (v2 2026-04-19) · arXiv:2506.10630 · Appears in: Signal/Time Series

What

Time-R1 is a two-stage reinforcement fine-tuning framework that teaches LLMs to perform multi-step reasoning for time series forecasting. Stage 1 runs supervised fine-tuning on reasoning traces as warmup. Stage 2 applies RL with a fine-grained multi-objective reward for time series plus GRIP (Group-based Relative Importance for Policy Optimization), which uses non-uniform sampling to push the model toward effective reasoning paths.

Why

Existing time series forecasting methods follow a "fast thinking" paradigm — extract patterns and map them forward without explicit reasoning about why patterns should continue or change. Slow-thinking LLMs like DeepSeek-R1 show strong multi-step reasoning, but prompt engineering is expensive and domain-shallow for time series. Training LLMs to internalize time-series reasoning via RL is unexplored.

How

Stage 1: SFT on curated time-series reasoning traces for domain adaptation. Stage 2: RL with two innovations — (a) a multi-objective reward that separately penalizes directional errors, magnitude errors, and temporal misalignment, and (b) GRIP, which extends GRPO by weighting exploration samples by their relative reward improvement, encouraging the model to find reasoning paths that produce better forecasts.

Builds on

  • DeepSeek-R1 (2025)Established RL-only reasoning in LLMs; Time-R1 transfers this paradigm to time series with a domain-specific reward.
  • TS-Agent (Liu 2025)Demonstrated LLM reasoning over TS via tool-use; Time-R1 instead internalizes reasoning via RL fine-tuning.
  • ChatTS (Xie 2025)Aligned time series as an LLM modality; Time-R1 takes a different route — training reasoning into the model rather than aligning modalities.

Solves vs predecessors

TS-Agent and ChatTS both rely on frozen or lightly adapted LLMs — TS-Agent routes reasoning through external tools, and ChatTS aligns time series as a visual-like modality. Both inherit the LLM's general reasoning ability without specializing it for time series patterns. Time-R1 directly trains the LLM to reason about time series via RL, producing domain-specific reasoning traces rather than repurposing general-purpose ones. The multi-objective reward addresses the failure mode where a single MSE reward conflates directional, magnitude, and timing errors.

Method vs predecessors

DeepSeek-R1 uses GRPO with uniform sampling across reasoning trajectories. Time-R1 replaces this with GRIP, which assigns higher weight to trajectory groups showing larger relative reward improvements. The multi-objective reward decomposes forecasting error into three components, whereas TS-Agent's quality gate uses a single holistic check. Compared to ChatTS's modality-alignment training, Time-R1 requires no multimodal architecture — it operates on numeric sequences represented as text tokens.
