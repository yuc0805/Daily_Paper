AnyPPG: An ECG-Guided PPG Foundation Model Trained on Over 100,000 Hours of Recordings

AnyPPG authors · arXiv 2025 · arXiv:2511.01747 · Appears in: LLM-Health

What

AnyPPG is a foundation model for photoplethysmography (PPG) pretrained on over 100,000 hours of synchronized PPG and ECG from 58,796 participants across five public datasets. It aligns PPG and ECG in a shared embedding space so the PPG encoder learns physiologically meaningful features from unlabeled signals. Across eleven physiological analysis tasks over six datasets it reports average gains of 12.8% on regression and 9.1% on classification over the next-best model, and on a disease screen across 1,014 ICD-10 categories, 13 reach AUC above 0.8 and 137 above 0.7, including non-cardiac conditions such as Parkinson's disease (AUC 0.78) and chronic kidney disease (AUC 0.74).

Why

PPG is cheap and ubiquitous in wearables but noisy, so a representation learned from PPG alone is unstable. ECG carries a cleaner cardiac timing signal. Prior single-modality biosignal foundation models did not exploit the fact that PPG and ECG are often recorded together, leaving a cleaner teaching signal unused. The paper sets out to use synchronized ECG to constrain what the PPG representation should capture.

How

During pretraining the model aligns PPG and ECG representations in a shared space, using ECG as guidance for the PPG encoder, on large-scale paired recordings. After pretraining the PPG encoder is evaluated on conventional physiological tasks (heart-rate and blood-pressure estimation, affect recognition, atrial-fibrillation detection, signal-quality assessment) and on multi-organ disease screening, with the reported state-of-the-art margins above.

Solves vs predecessors

HeAR and other single-modality biosignal models learn from one signal, so a noisy modality like PPG has no external anchor and the representation drifts on low-quality segments. AnyPPG fixes this by using synchronized ECG as a supervisory anchor during pretraining, so the PPG encoder is pulled toward cardiac structure that PPG alone would not reliably recover.

Method vs predecessors

Where the Gu 2025 survey described single-modality pretraining and HeAR built one for acoustics, AnyPPG adds a paired-modality alignment objective and scales to over 100,000 hours. The technical move is cross-modal supervision (a clean modality teaching a noisy one), common in vision-language, applied here ECG-to-PPG. The downstream evaluation protocol stays close to prior biosignal foundation-model practice.
