Large Language Models Are Zero-Shot Time Series Forecasters

Gruver et al. · arXiv preprint 2023 · arXiv:2310.07820 · Appears in: Signal (Time Series)

What

Frozen LLMs forecast zero-shot by tokenizing numbers as text, matching purpose-built forecasters on some benchmarks.
