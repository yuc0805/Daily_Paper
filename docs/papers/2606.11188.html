ARM: An AutoRegressive Large Multimodal Model with Unified Discrete Representations

Wang et al. · arXiv 2026 · paper · Appears in: Multi-Modal, Generative CV

What

ARM is a 7B autoregressive model that performs image understanding, generation, and editing in one next-token-prediction framework. A semantic visual tokenizer maps images into a compact discrete token sequence, trained for discriminability, language alignment, and reconstruction at the same time. Text and image tokens are interleaved and modeled by a single transformer, after which reinforcement learning optimizes task rewards, raising WISE overall from 0.50 to 0.56 and GEdit-Bench-EN from 5.75 to 6.68 with cross-task gains.

Why

Many multimodal models keep separate machinery for understanding and for generation, often pairing a language model with a diffusion decoder. That split makes it hard to share representations and to transfer skill between generation and editing. A single discrete representation and one objective would let both tasks reinforce each other.

How

Images become discrete tokens through a tokenizer supervised by several objectives at once, then those tokens are interleaved with text and modeled by next-token prediction. On top of the pretrained model, reinforcement learning optimizes task-level objectives such as visual quality, instruction adherence, and edit consistency, which is the step that produces measurable cross-task synergy between text-to-image generation and instruction-guided editing.

Builds on

  • Attention Is All You NeedTransformer backbone and next-token prediction that ARM reuses for both modalities.
  • DALL-E 2Text-to-image generation through learned visual representations; ARM replaces its separate decoder with a shared discrete vocabulary.
  • FlamingoInterleaved image and text tokens in one sequence; ARM folds both into a single autoregressive model.

Solves vs predecessors

DALL-E 2 used a separate diffusion-style decoder over CLIP latents and Flamingo kept vision and language in distinct modules joined by cross-attention, so generation and understanding did not share parameters. ARM removes that split by putting both modalities in one discrete vocabulary and one objective, which lets editing and generation skill transfer between each other.

Method vs predecessors

Where the original transformer predicted text tokens only, ARM predicts a unified text-plus-image token stream and adds reinforcement learning on task rewards. The novel pieces are the single discrete tokenizer trained for several objectives at once and the reward optimization stage, not the autoregressive idea itself.
