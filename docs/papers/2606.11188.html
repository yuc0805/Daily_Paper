ARM: An AutoRegressive Large Multimodal Model with Unified Discrete Representations

Wang, Wang, Pan, et al. · arXiv 2026 · link · Appears in: Multi-Modal

What

ARM is a 7B autoregressive model that handles image understanding, text-to-image generation, and instruction-guided editing within one next-token prediction framework. A discrete semantic visual tokenizer maps an image into a short token sequence, trained against three objectives at once: semantic discriminability, language alignment, and faithful reconstruction. The single transformer backbone is then trained over interleaved text and image tokens, and a reinforcement-learning stage optimizes task-level rewards for visual quality, instruction adherence, and edit consistency.

Why

Unified vision-language models face a split: autoregressive models reason well across modalities but generate weak images, while diffusion models generate strong images but reason and edit poorly. Prior unified attempts kept understanding and generation in separate sub-models or bolted a diffusion decoder onto a language model, which limits parameter sharing and cross-task transfer. The question this paper takes on is whether a single next-token model can carry all three abilities at once.

How

The discrete tokenizer puts perception and generation in a shared latent space, so the same token vocabulary serves reading and writing of images. A 7B transformer is trained with plain next-token prediction over large-scale text and image token sequences. After pretraining, reinforcement learning optimizes rewards that the pretraining loss does not capture directly: visual quality, instruction adherence, and edit consistency. The reported effect is both higher target-task scores and cross-task synergy, where reward optimization on generation also improves editing.

Builds on

  • DALL-E 2 (Ramesh 2022)Text-to-image generation through a diffusion path, kept separate from understanding.
  • DiT (Peebles 2023)Transformer-backbone diffusion for scalable image generation.
  • Attention Is All You Need (Vaswani 2017)The next-token transformer backbone ARM reuses for both reading and writing images.
  • DeepSeek-R1 (2025)Reinforcement-learning post-training that ARM adapts from text reasoning to image tasks.
  • SigLIP (Zhai 2023)Sigmoid-loss vision-language alignment, the closest parent. Not in library yet.

Solves vs predecessors

Where DALL-E 2 and DiT generate pixels through iterative denoising and keep understanding in a separate model, ARM does understanding and generation as one next-token stream over discrete tokens, so a single 7B parameter set serves perception, generation, and editing. The reinforcement-learning stage raises WISE overall from 0.50 to 0.56 and GEdit-Bench-EN G_O from 5.75 to 6.68, and the gains on generation and editing reinforce each other rather than trade off.

Method vs predecessors

The diffusion parents model a continuous denoising process; ARM models a discrete token sequence with autoregression, which lets it inherit the language-model toolchain, including reinforcement-learning post-training from the DeepSeek-R1 line. The novel piece relative to both lines is the tokenizer trained jointly for discriminability, alignment, and reconstruction, which is what makes one latent space usable for both reading and writing images.
