DailySafe: Benchmarking Dynamic Safety Awareness in Everyday Multi-Turn Multimodal Interactions

Xuankun Rong, Xinhao Deng, Wenzhang Jiang, Ziheng Peng et al. · 2026 · Explore · arXiv:2611.26298

Why recommended

Picked for exploration — from another field, outside your usual research areas.

Abstract

As multimodal large language models (MLLMs) become increasingly integrated into everyday life, their ability to provide safe and reliable assistance in realistic daily activities remains insufficiently explored. Existing safety benchmarks primarily focus on isolated harmful requests or adversarial scenarios, overlooking the safety risks that may arise as ordinary tasks unfold. In practice, users pursue legitimate goals through multi-turn, multimodal interactions, where seemingly benign requests may become safety-critical when considered alongside earlier actions and visual observations. This calls for Dynamic Safety Awareness, the ability to track evolving task conditions, recognize implicit contextual risks, and provide safe and helpful assistance. To systematically evaluate this capability, we introduce DailySafe, a benchmark comprising 195 naturalistic multi-turn, multi-image interactions across 10 everyday domains and 42 task categories. Grounded in authentic user dialogue topics and documented safety knowledge, DailySafe captures realistic scenarios where safety-critical evidence is progressively revealed across conversational turns and visual observations. Using case-specific safety annotations, DailySafe assesses whether MLLMs can recognize and appropriately address evolving risks while supporting users' legitimate goals, bringing multimodal safety evaluation closer to real-world everyday AI assistance. DailySafe is publicly available at: https://github.com/XuankunRong/DailySafe.
