Particle physics science communication with grass-roots Instagram: a success story

Freya Blekman, Lucia Coll Saravia · 2026 · Explore · arXiv:2611.25806

Why recommended

Picked for exploration — from another field, outside your usual research areas.

Abstract

Communicating science to the public is an integral part of the work of a research collaboration, not an afterthought. Institutional and collaboration-level social media accounts are often run by professional communicators rather than by practising scientists, and single-scientist ``influencer'' accounts are demanding to sustain. We describe {\em CMSatDESY}, a crowd-sourced Instagram account on which the CMS group at DESY tells its own stories, each member in their own voice. In the first half of 2026, the science communication activities through this reached 140k+ unique accounts and received 250k+ views, with an engagement rate of 7\%. We discuss experiences and best practices that can be used by other groups, and describe how the account doubles as a science communication playground for outreach training for a large particle physics group.
