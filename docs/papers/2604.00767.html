ActivityNarrated: An Open-Ended Narrative Paradigm for Wearable Human Activity Understanding

ActivityNarrated authors · arXiv 2026 · arXiv:2604.00767 · Appears in: Signal/Time Series

What

ActivityNarrated reframes wearable human activity recognition (HAR) away from a fixed label set toward open-ended natural-language narratives of behavior. It contributes a dataset of unscripted multi-position IMU recordings with time-aligned narration, an evaluation framework based on sensor-language retrieval that generalizes closed-set classification, and a model, ActNarrator, that maps variable-duration and partially observed sensor streams to text. ActNarrator reports near doubling of Macro-F1 over closed-set HAR baselines when classification is run as a downstream task.

Why

Standard HAR fixes a small activity vocabulary and a single sensor position, which does not match real wearable use that is long-tailed, personalized, and made of overlapping activities. Prior sensor-to-language work produced descriptions tied to a closed taxonomy, so it could not score understanding of behavior outside that taxonomy. The paper sets out to measure activity understanding by semantic alignment between motion and free-text narration instead of top-1 accuracy over fixed classes.

How

The dataset pairs multi-position IMU streams with time-aligned natural-language narration of unscripted behavior. ActNarrator aligns motion representations with these narratives and supports sequence-to-text inference over variable-duration, heterogeneous, partially observed sensor placements. Evaluation uses sensor-language retrieval, which generalizes closed-set classification and gives metrics beyond accuracy and F1; when classification is evaluated as a downstream task, the learned alignment nearly doubles Macro-F1 against closed-set baselines.

Builds on

  • Sensor2Text (Chen 2024)Established natural-language interaction with activity-tracking sensor data via a trained sensor-to-language bridge; this paper removes its closed vocabulary and moves to open-ended narration. Sensor2Text is the time-series to llm-health bridge node in your library.

Solves vs predecessors

Sensor2Text produces fixed natural-language descriptions tied to a closed activity vocabulary and a single sensor position. It fails the specific case where the activity is not in the taxonomy or the sensor placement varies. ActivityNarrated fixes this by removing the predefined taxonomy: it models open-ended narratives over multi-position, partially observed streams and scores them by sensor-language retrieval, so out-of-vocabulary and overlapping activities are measurable rather than collapsed into a wrong label.

Method vs predecessors

Where Sensor2Text trains a bridge to caption within a fixed scheme, ActNarrator keeps the sensor-language alignment idea but supervises it with retrieval against free-text narration and handles heterogeneous, partially observed placements. The change of integration boundary is from "describe within a closed scheme" to "retrieve the matching open narrative", which imports the contrastive-retrieval evaluation style from vision-language learning into IMU HAR.
