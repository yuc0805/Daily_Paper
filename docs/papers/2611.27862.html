OpenAHBU: Open-sourced Automatic Human Behaviour Understanding Toolkit Through Facial Behaviour Analysis

Zijian Wu, Yupeng Huo, Cheng Luo, Yichen Huang et al. · 2026 · arXiv · arXiv:2611.27862

Why recommended

Very recent in your research areas.

Abstract

Automatic facial behaviour understanding (AFBU) is fundamental to various human-centred applications, yet existing academically available toolkits offer limited functionality, closed training pipelines, and weak generalisation. This paper presents OpenAHBU, a fully open-sourced, generalisable toolkit integrating 11 AFBU functions across three semantic levels: (i) short-term external facial attributes, including landmarks, blendshapes, Action Units, and expressions; (ii) short-term facial behaviour-related human psychological states, including valence/arousal and pain; and (iii) long-term facial behaviour-related psychological traits, including depression and personality. Particularly, we re-developed eight functions, where at least four deep learning models covering different computational complexities are developed for each function, providing flexible deployment on resource-constrained and high-throughput computing scenarios. More importantly, models of most functions are trained on a comprehensive corpus aggregating multiple academically available datasets, ensuring robust cross-dataset generalisability.In addition, we include a Synergistic Multi-task Enhancement (SMTE) module to efficiently and adaptively enhance each OpenAHBU function by leveraging the complementary cues contained in its most-related support function's predictions.Extensive evaluations demonstrate that our OpenAHBU outperformed existing open-source toolkits not only in functional coverage but also the predictive performances, while providing superior deployment flexibility across diverse computational scenarios.With fully publicly available training and inference scripts, pre-trained weights, user-friendly GUI, Python library, and cross-platform compatibility, our OpenAHBU provides a reproducible, scalable and extensible toolkit at https://github.com/OpenAHBU/OpenAHBU.
