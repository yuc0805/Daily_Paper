VideoChat3: Fully Open Video MLLM for Efficient and Generalist Video Understanding

MCG-NJU · arXiv 2026 · arXiv:2607.14935 · Appears in: Multi-Modal

What

VideoChat3 is a fully open 4B-parameter video multimodal LLM built to handle short motion, hour-long video, temporal grounding, and live streaming in a single model. It introduces an Inflated 3D Vision Transformer (I3D-ViT) for spatiotemporal features and an Adaptive Frame Resolution scheme for streaming perception. It is trained with a scalable video data-synthesis pipeline that produces three datasets covering general, long-form, and streaming scenarios (Academic2M, LV116K, OL617K).

Why

Open-source video MLLMs struggle to cover short motion, very long video, and live streams at once, and processing many frames is expensive. Fixed frame-sampling schemes inherited from image-language models waste compute on redundant frames and still miss fine motion, so an efficient and generalist open model has been missing.

How

The model inflates a 2D Vision Transformer into an I3D-ViT to read spatiotemporal structure, then applies Adaptive Frame Resolution so that streaming and long inputs are processed at a resolution matched to their information content, holding cost down. A data-synthesis pipeline curates the three training corpora. The reported result is that VideoChat3 surpasses prior open-source video MLLMs of equal or larger parameter count using only 4B parameters and higher efficiency.

Builds on

  • I3D (Carreira & Zisserman 2017)Inflated 2D convolutions into 3D to read motion; VideoChat3 inflates a Vision Transformer the same way.
  • Flamingo (Alayrac 2022)Bridged a frozen vision encoder to an LLM for image-and-text dialogue.
  • BLIP-2 (Li 2023)Connected a frozen image encoder and LLM through a lightweight query transformer.

Solves vs predecessors

Flamingo and BLIP-2 handled images or short clips with fixed frame sampling, which becomes too costly for hour-long or live video and misses fine motion. VideoChat3 fixes this by adding streaming-aware adaptive frame resolution and an inflated-transformer video encoder, so one model covers short, long, and streaming video without a separate design per regime.

Method vs predecessors

Where I3D inflated ConvNets, VideoChat3 carries the inflation trick to transformers, giving I3D-ViT. Where Flamingo and BLIP-2 kept a fixed sampling front end, VideoChat3 varies frame resolution with the input, which is what makes constant-cost streaming perception possible at 4B parameters.
