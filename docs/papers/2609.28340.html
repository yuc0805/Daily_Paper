Dynamics-structure interchangeability in binary opinion models on multiplex networks

Julian Sienkiewicz, Anna Chmiel · · Explore · arXiv:2609.28340

Why recommended

Picked for exploration — from another field, outside your usual research areas.

Abstract

We examine the interchangeability between two duplex systems with opinion dynamics: one characterized by different dynamics parameters $a_1$ and $a_2$ on each layer and full node overlap, and the second with the same parameter $a_1$ on both layers but only partial node overlap $r$. We introduce a general framework for identifying the critical line between the ordered and disordered phases, thereby translating one system into another. The description is supported by several examples of opinion dynamics models and a rigorous measure of the distance between the stationary solutions of the rate equations in both settings. Our results show that even large values of $r$ are sufficient to obtain very good correspondence between the two settings.
